# 阿拉伯语持续预训练与预训练语料综述

## ⭐ Dataset Quality Ranking — Hands-On Deep Sampling / 数据集质量实测排名（深度抽样）

*Method / 方法:* Real HuggingFace dataset-viewer rows were pulled directly via curl at **deep, varied offsets** — pages p=0, 20, 30, 100, 500, 2000 (rows 0 → 200k+), ~20–30 real rows per dataset. Judged on fluency, cleanliness (boilerplate/HTML/ads/tatweel), MSA-vs-dialect-vs-foreign leakage, repetition, informational value, and **deep-offset degradation** (shallow sampling misses this). Only general-domain corpora actually sampled are ranked; gated sets are listed at the bottom. / 通过 curl 直接抓取 HF viewer 的真实数据行，覆盖 p=0 至 p=2000 的深层、多样页面，每个数据集读取约 20–30 条真实文本，重点考察深层是否退化。仅排名实际采样到的通用语料，受限数据集列于末尾。

**Ranking (best → worst, actually sampled) / 排名（最佳→最差，均为实测）:**

**1. FineWeb-2 (Arabic) — `HuggingFaceFW/fineweb-2`, config `arb_Arab`/train — Tier: High / 高.** ~26 rows across p=0–2000. Best general-domain choice: fluent, coherent MSA spanning news, politics, science, encyclopedic, how-to, lifestyle — broad topical diversity, clean text, negligible HTML/boilerplate, well deduped. | 最佳通用选择：流畅连贯的标准阿语，涵盖新闻、政治、科学、百科、生活等，主题多样、文本干净、几乎无 HTML/样板、去重良好。
> `السبيل لحل أزمة القيادة الفلسطينية بلغ الانهيار البطيء لمؤسسات القيادة الفلسطينية الجماعية…` — *EN: "The path to solving the Palestinian leadership crisis…" / 中文：解决巴勒斯坦领导层危机之道……*
> **Deep-offset note:** Confirmed — at p=30 a minority of rows are machine-translated garble (incoherent, embedded English tokens); bulk stays clean, MT noise is a real but minority contaminant at depth. | 已证实：p=30 少数行为机翻乱码，但主体干净，深层机翻噪声属少数污染。

**2. Arabic Billion Words — `MohamedRashad/arabic-billion-words`, default/train — Tier: High (narrow domain) / 高（领域窄）.** ~15 rows across p=0–2000. The **cleanest** per-document text sampled — pure journalistic MSA (Abu El-Khair news): no HTML/ads/dialect/foreign leakage, stable even at p=2000. Ranked #2 only because it is essentially single-domain (news/press). | 单文档最干净：纯新闻体标准阿语，无 HTML/广告/方言/外语混入，p=2000 仍稳定；排第二仅因领域单一（新闻）。
> `وقعت الإمارات مذكرة تفاهم للنقل الجوي مع جمهورية نيبال في كاتماندو مؤخرا…` — *EN: "The UAE recently signed an air-transport MoU with Nepal in Kathmandu…" / 中文：阿联酋近日在加德满都与尼泊尔签署航空运输谅解备忘录……*
> **Deep-offset note:** No degradation; consistent clean news across all pages. | 无退化，各页均稳定干净。

**3. HPLT 2.0 cleaned (Arabic) — `HPLT/HPLT2.0_cleaned`, config `ara_Arab`/train — Tier: Medium-High / 中高.** ~25 rows across p=0–2000. Mostly coherent (news, rights reports, SANA agency copy at depth), but noticeably noisier: forum boilerplate (print-page), Egyptian/Gulf **dialect** rows, and clear **foreign-language leakage** (Polish Twitter strings mid-document). Usable with extra filtering. | 大体连贯，但更嘈杂：论坛样板、埃及/海湾方言行、明显外语混入（文中夹波兰语推文）；需额外清洗。
> `…سرقوهم و لمن شافو كاميرات المراقبة… — Dziękujemy. Twitter skorzysta z tych informacji` — *EN: "[dialect] They robbed them, and when they checked the cameras… — [Polish] Thank you. Twitter will use this info…" / 中文：（方言）……一查监控…（波兰语）谢谢，Twitter 将使用这些信息……*
> **Deep-offset note:** Noise present from p=0; deep pages (p=2000) actually cleaner (SANA news blocks). Language leakage is the main defect throughout. | 噪声从 p=0 即存在，p=2000 反而更干净；外语混入为全程主要缺陷。

**4. C4 (Arabic) — `allenai/c4`, config `ar`/train — Tier: Medium / 中.** ~18 rows across p=0–2000. Raw web crawl: coherent long-form (cultural essays, health/how-to, news) mixed with heavy nav/SEO boilerplate and machine-translated product spam. Broad but needs aggressive dedup/quality filtering. | 原始网页抓取：连贯长文与大量导航/SEO 样板、机翻商品垃圾混杂；覆盖广但需强力过滤。
> `الصفحة الرئيسية > قائمة المنتجات > Otis Door Controller … مصنع من الصين` — *EN: "Home > Product List > Otis Door Controller … factory from China." / 中文：首页 > 产品列表 > 奥的斯门控…来自中国的工厂。*
> **Deep-offset note:** Boilerplate/SEO spam at every offset (worst p=500–2000); coherent content diluted, not degraded. | 各页均有样板/SEO 垃圾（p=500–2000 最重）；连贯内容被稀释而非退化。

**5. 101 Billion Arabic Words — `ClusterlabAi/101_billion_arabic_words_dataset`, default/train — Tier: Medium-Low / 中低.** ~19 rows across p=0–500. Lowest of the sampled set: **empty rows**, repeated titles, and pervasive machine-translated SEO/ad spam (binary-options, jewelry manufacturing, iron-ore flowcharts). Some genuine news/culture, but poor signal-to-noise. | 采样中最差：空行、重复标题、大量机翻 SEO/广告垃圾；虽有真实内容但信噪比低。
> `ورقة تدفق معالجة خام الحديد … ورقة عملية تدفق خام النحاس` — *EN: "Iron-ore processing flow sheet … copper-ore process flow sheet." / 中文：铁矿石加工流程表……铜矿石工艺流程表。*
> **Deep-offset note:** Spam/empty rows from p=0 onward; no clean deep region found. | 从 p=0 起即有垃圾与空行，未见干净深层区。

**Recommendation / 建议:** **FineWeb-2 `arb_Arab`** is the top pick (best scale + breadth + cleanliness) — add a light MT-garble/language-ID filter to catch the minority machine-translated rows that surface at depth. Pair with **`MohamedRashad/arabic-billion-words`** as a high-purity MSA news booster (cleanest, but single-domain). Use **HPLT 2.0 `ara_Arab`** and **C4 `ar`** only with aggressive dedup + language-ID + boilerplate filtering; treat **101B** as a low-tier web dump needing heavy filtering. | 首选 **FineWeb-2 `arb_Arab`**（规模+广度+洁净度最佳），加轻量机翻/语种过滤；辅以 **arabic-billion-words** 作高纯度 MSA 新闻补强；**HPLT 2.0** 与 **C4** 须强力去重+语种识别+样板过滤；**101B** 视为需重度清洗的低层转储。

*Gated / inaccessible (not ranked) / 受限或不可访问（未排名）:* `uonlp/CulturaX` (ar), `oscar-corpus/OSCAR-2301` (ar), `BAAI/ArabicText-2022` — all returned 0 rows (gated). Note: earlier shallow sampling had ranked 101B higher; **deep sampling revealed its heavy MT/SEO spam**, hence its drop to last. | 上述均返回 0 行（受限）。注：早期浅层采样曾将 101B 排名更高，深度采样暴露其大量机翻/SEO 垃圾，故降至末位。

> **Scope / 范围:** This review covers research that (a) performs **continued / continual pretraining (CPT)** to adapt an existing LLM to Arabic (Modern Standard Arabic and dialects), and (b) **introduces a pretraining corpus / dataset** for Arabic, including from-scratch Arabic-centric foundation models and pretrained language models (PLMs) whose papers describe their pretraining corpus.
> 本综述涵盖：(a) 通过**持续/继续预训练（CPT）**将既有大模型适配到阿拉伯语（现代标准阿拉伯语 MSA 及方言）；(b) 为阿拉伯语**引入预训练语料/数据集**，包括从零训练的以阿语为中心的基座模型与预训练语言模型（PLM），其论文描述了预训练语料。

> **Method / 方法说明:** Candidate papers were discovered via comprehensive web search; each paper's full text was then read by a dedicated sub-agent that extracted real figures and produced the bilingual (English + Chinese) summary below following a fixed template. Inaccessible papers and one non-existent title are flagged explicitly (see *Access Limitations*).
> 候选论文通过全面网络检索获得；随后由专门子代理阅读每篇论文全文、抽取真实数据，并按固定模板生成下方双语（英文+中文）摘要。无法访问的论文以及一个并不存在的标题已明确标注（见*访问限制*）。

## 1. Continued Pretraining & Dialectal Adaptation / 一、持续预训练与方言适配

### AceGPT, Localizing Large Language Models in Arabic (Huang et al., 2023)
- **Venue / Link:** NAACL 2024 (main conference), https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.12053
- **Motivation / 动机:** Mainstream LLMs inadequately capture Arabic's unique cultural characteristics and local values, raising cultural-sensitivity concerns (e.g., inserting non-Arab/Western names or non-local references into Arabic outputs); the goal is a "localized," culturally cognizant and value-aligned Arabic LLM. | 主流大模型无法充分体现阿拉伯语独特的文化特征与本地价值观，存在文化敏感性问题（例如在阿拉伯语输出中插入非阿拉伯/西方人名或非本地化内容）；目标是打造"本地化"、具备文化认知且价值观对齐的阿拉伯语大模型。
- **Corpus & Data / 语料与数据:** Continued pre-training on Arabic + a small English replay set. LLaMA2-7B was trained on 30B tokens (19.2B Arabic + 10.8B English); LLaMA2-13B on 10B tokens (6B Arabic + 4B English). Arabic sources: Arabic Wikipedia, CC100, OSCAR; English from SlimPajama (sampled subset). Focus is MSA (no explicit dialect corpus). SFT uses native Arabic questions in the wild + GPT-4 Arabic responses; RLAIF uses a culture/value-aligned reward model. Code/data/models open-sourced under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. | 在阿拉伯语数据上进行继续预训练，并加入少量英语回放数据。LLaMA2-7B 使用 300 亿 token（192 亿阿拉伯语 + 108 亿英语）；LLaMA2-13B 使用 100 亿 token（60 亿阿拉伯语 + 40 亿英语）。阿拉伯语来源：阿拉伯语维基百科、CC100、OSCAR；英语来自 SlimPajama（抽样子集）。以标准阿拉伯语（MSA）为主（无专门方言语料）。SFT 使用真实场景的阿拉伯语问题 + GPT-4 阿拉伯语回答；RLAIF 使用文化/价值观对齐的奖励模型。代码/数据/模型以 CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 开源。
- **Method / 方法:** Continued pre-training (CPT) on LLaMA2 (7B & 13B) as backbone. Three stages: (1) localized pre-training on Arabic; (2) SFT with native Arabic instructions + GPT-4 responses; (3) RLAIF with a reward model attuned to local culture/values. Deliberately did NOT expand the vocabulary — reused LLaMA2's original tokenizer (which contains all 53 Arabic letters) to save training cost. | 以 LLaMA2（7B 与 13B）为骨干进行继续预训练（CPT）。三阶段：(1) 阿拉伯语本地化预训练；(2) 使用真实阿拉伯语指令 + GPT-4 回答进行 SFT；(3) 使用贴合本地文化/价值观的奖励模型进行 RLAIF。刻意不扩展词表——沿用 LLaMA2 原始分词器（其包含全部 53 个阿拉伯字母）以节省训练成本。
- **Models & Eval / 模型与评测:** Released AceGPT-7B and AceGPT-13B (base + chat). Benchmarks: Arabic Vicuna-80, Arabic AlpacaEval (GPT-4 scored, ratio vs GPT-3.5 Turbo), Arabic MMLU, EXAMs, and the newly introduced ACVA (Arabic Cultural & Value Alignment, >8000 yes/no questions). Results: AceGPT-13B-base best Arabic MMLU among open models (37.26%); AceGPT-13B-chat reaches 100.88% of GPT-3.5 Turbo on Arabic Vicuna-80 and trails GPT-3.5 by only −0.55% on ACVA "all"; AceGPT-7B-chat beats Jais-13B by ~20 points. SOTA among open Arabic LLMs. | 发布 AceGPT-7B 与 AceGPT-13B（base + chat）。评测基准：阿拉伯语 Vicuna-80、阿拉伯语 AlpacaEval（由 GPT-4 评分、与 GPT-3.5 Turbo 的比值）、阿拉伯语 MMLU、EXAMs，以及新提出的 ACVA（阿拉伯文化与价值观对齐，>8000 道是非题）。结果：AceGPT-13B-base 在开源模型中阿拉伯语 MMLU 最高（37.26%）；AceGPT-13B-chat 在阿拉伯语 Vicuna-80 上达到 GPT-3.5 Turbo 的 100.88%，在 ACVA "all" 上仅落后 GPT-3.5 0.55%；AceGPT-7B-chat 比 Jais-13B 高约 20 分。为开源阿拉伯语大模型的 SOTA。
- **Key Findings / 主要发现:** Localized CPT on modest Arabic data markedly improves Arabic culture/value alignment — AceGPT-7B-base even surpasses the larger LLaMA2-13B on ACVA; the full pipeline (CPT+SFT+RLAIF) yields open-source SOTA and RLAIF further improves value alignment. | 在有限阿拉伯语数据上的本地化继续预训练即可显著提升文化/价值观对齐——AceGPT-7B-base 在 ACVA 上甚至超过更大的 LLaMA2-13B；完整流程（CPT+SFT+RLAIF）实现开源 SOTA，且 RLAIF 进一步改善价值观对齐。
- **Limitations / 局限:** Vocabulary was not expanded, hurting Arabic encoding efficiency; limited compute restricted token budget (untapped potential); reasoning/misinformation/bias not evaluated, so safety alignment is uncertain and use is limited to academic research; the cultural (ACVA) dataset needs quality/quantity improvement. | 未扩展词表，损害阿拉伯语编码效率；算力有限导致 token 预算受限（潜力未充分释放）；未评测推理/虚假信息/偏见，安全对齐存疑，仅限学术研究使用；文化数据集（ACVA）在质与量上仍需提升。

### Bilingual Adaptation of Monolingual Foundation Models (Jais-adapted method) (Gosal et al., 2024)
- **Venue / Link:** arXiv preprint (CC BY 4.0), https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.12869
- **Motivation / 动机:** Adapting an English-dominant monolingual foundation LLM to a new language cheaply, while overcoming catastrophic forgetting of English, English-dominant tokenizer inefficiency (Llama 2 needs ~4x more tokens for Arabic), and the need for hyperparameter re-tuning. | 以低成本将以英语为主的单语基础大模型适配到新语言，同时克服英语能力的灾难性遗忘、英语主导分词器的低效（Llama 2 编码阿拉伯语所需 token 约多 4 倍）以及需要重新调参的问题。
- **Corpus & Data / 语料与数据:** Bilingual mix of Arabic (AraV5) + English (the Pile, 22 sources incl. ArXiv, Wikipedia, PubMedCentral, CommonCrawl, OpenWebText, GitHub). AraV5 totals 140B tokens / 345 GB. Llama 2 7B adaptation used 20B AraV5 tokens; Llama 2 13B & 70B used the full 140B. Mix ratios: 1:1 En:Ar for 7B/13B, 1:9 En:Ar for 70B (to cut compute). Also adapted Llama 3 8B (Arabic) and Llama 2 13B (Hindi, 65B-token Hindi corpus). Primarily MSA. Detailed training recipe released. | 阿拉伯语（AraV5）+ 英语（the Pile，22 个来源，含 ArXiv、维基百科、PubMedCentral、CommonCrawl、OpenWebText、GitHub）的双语混合。AraV5 共 1400 亿 token / 345 GB。Llama 2 7B 适配使用 200 亿 AraV5 token；Llama 2 13B 与 70B 使用全部 1400 亿。混合比例：7B/13B 为英:阿 1:1，70B 为 1:9（以减少算力）。另适配 Llama 3 8B（阿拉伯语）与 Llama 2 13B（印地语，650 亿 token 印地语语料）。以 MSA 为主。公开了详细训练配方。
- **Method / 方法:** CPT on Llama 2 (7B/13B/70B), Llama 3 8B and Llama 2 13B-Hindi. Two-stage: (1) expand vocabulary and train ONLY the embedding matrix (embedding alignment) — "Llama-extend100" adds 32,000 new Arabic tokens, doubling the vocab/embedding from [32000, d] to [64000, d] (extend-100% chosen over vocabulary-replace-30% and over MLV2; reduces Llama 2 Arabic fertility by 72.17% without hurting English); then (2) full-model continual pre-training on the bilingual corpus. Explores 3 embedding-initialization techniques (incl. projecting Jais embeddings via a learned linear map W,b, and weighted-average of base tokens, k=5) plus data-mix and learning-rate ablations (peak LR best). | 对 Llama 2（7B/13B/70B）、Llama 3 8B 及 Llama 2 13B-印地语进行 CPT。两阶段：(1) 扩展词表并仅训练嵌入矩阵（嵌入对齐）——"Llama-extend100" 新增 32,000 个阿拉伯语 token，将词表/嵌入从 [32000, d] 翻倍至 [64000, d]（选用扩展 100% 而非替换 30% 或 MLV2；使 Llama 2 阿拉伯语 fertility 降低 72.17% 且不损害英语）；(2) 在双语语料上进行全模型继续预训练。探索 3 种嵌入初始化方法（含用学习到的线性映射 W,b 投影 Jais 嵌入、以及基础 token 的加权平均 k=5），并做数据混合与学习率消融（峰值学习率 lrpeak 最佳）。
- **Models & Eval / 模型与评测:** Jais-adapted models at Llama 2 7B/13B/70B and Llama 3 8B scales. Benchmarks grouped as Knowledge (MMLU, RACE, EXAMS), Commonsense reasoning (HellaSwag, etc.), and Misinformation/Bias, in both Arabic and English. Results: +7.5% Arabic MMLU for adapted Llama2-70B vs base, +~2% for 7B/13B; slight English gains on 70B; Llama 3 8B adaptation gives +4.5% average Arabic improvement; overall ~significant average gain in Arabic downstream tasks with a ~1% improvement in English downstream tasks; competitive with SOTA Arabic models. | 提供 Llama 2 7B/13B/70B 与 Llama 3 8B 规模的 Jais-adapted 模型。评测按知识（MMLU、RACE、EXAMS）、常识推理（HellaSwag 等）、虚假信息/偏见分组，涵盖阿拉伯语与英语。结果：适配后 Llama2-70B 阿拉伯语 MMLU 较基座 +7.5%，7B/13B 约 +2%；70B 英语略有提升；Llama 3 8B 适配阿拉伯语平均提升 +4.5%；总体阿拉伯语下游任务平均显著提升，英语下游任务约 +1%；与 SOTA 阿拉伯语模型相当。
- **Key Findings / 主要发现:** A two-stage (embedding-alignment then full CPT) bilingual recipe with vocabulary extension enables cost-effective cross-lingual transfer that improves Arabic AND slightly improves English (avoiding catastrophic forgetting); larger models gain more (lower token-per-parameter regime → less over-training degradation); the recipe generalizes across model families/sizes and to a second language (Hindi). | 采用"先嵌入对齐、再全模型 CPT"的两阶段双语配方加词表扩展，能实现高性价比的跨语言迁移，既提升阿拉伯语、又略微提升英语（避免灾难性遗忘）；更大模型收益更大（更低的 token/参数比 → 过训练退化更少）；该配方可跨模型族/规模并推广到第二种语言（印地语）。
- **Limitations / 局限:** Focused mainly on MSA (no dialect emphasis); 70B used a compute-saving 1:9 mix rather than 1:1; the paper reports no explicit dedicated limitations section; gains on smaller models are modest (~2% Arabic MMLU). | 主要聚焦 MSA（未强调方言）；70B 为节省算力采用 1:9 混合而非 1:1；论文未设专门的局限性章节；小模型收益有限（阿拉伯语 MMLU 约 +2%）。

### Second Language (Arabic) Acquisition of LLMs via Progressive Vocabulary Expansion — AraLLaMA (Zhu et al., 2024)
- **Venue / Link:** ACL 2025 (Long Papers), https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.12310
- **Motivation / 动机:** Arabic LLMs lag due to mainstream focus on English/Chinese; an Arabic-specific tokenizer would speed decoding, but swapping vocabularies causes many out-of-vocabulary (OOV) tokens at training start, degrading learned knowledge. Inspired by human Second Language Acquisition. | 由于主流关注英语/中文，阿拉伯语大模型进展滞后；阿拉伯语专用分词器能加速解码，但更换词表会在训练初期产生大量未登录词（OOV），损害已学知识。方法受人类第二语言习得启发。
- **Corpus & Data / 语料与数据:** ~480B pre-training tokens (16 stages × 30B + extra 20B post-expansion), final ~251.4B Arabic + ~204.6B English. Arabic sources: filtered Common Crawl (101.3B), WebText (10.62B), Books+Newspapers (2.5B), two Wikipedia dumps (0.36B + 0.51B); English from SlimPajama + Proof-Pile-2 (math/code held at 5%). Arabic proportion ramped 30%→90% via cosine annealing. Primarily MSA. SFT dataset "ALAN": 127 topics → 11,430 subjects + 244,812 knowledge points → 733,419 instruction items (GPT-4-0613 generated), plus reused AceGPT data (Quora-Arabic, Alpaca-Arabic, Code-Alpaca-Arabic, Evol-Instruct-Arabic, ShareGPT). - **Limitations / 局限:** Models were not evaluated by native Arabic speakers; confined to academic research rather than online deployment; writing was AI-assisted; the paper does not report corpus size in GB nor an explicit MSA-vs-dialect split. | 模型未经母语阿拉伯语者评估；仅限学术研究而非线上部署；写作有 AI 辅助；论文未给出以 GB 计的语料规模，也未明确 MSA 与方言的比例划分。

Benchmarks (EgyptianBench): EgyptianMMLU, Belebele-Arz, EgyptianHellaSwag/PIQA/WinoGrande/OpenBookQA, EgyptianRACE-H/M, EgyptianAlpacaEval, translation, transliteration. Arabic-script (12B, best on all): MMLU 62.59, Belebele 79.44, HellaSwag 64.04, PIQA 70.69, AlpacaEval 95.56, transliteration BLEU 52.21 / chrF 80.97. The 3x4B-A6B MoE achieves the highest scores across all translation and transliteration tasks. | 模型：Nile-Chat-4B、Nile-Chat-12B（稠密），Nile-Chat-3x4B-A6B（主 MoE）与 2x4B-A6B。基线：LLaMa、Jais、ALLaM、Qwen2.5-14B-Instruct。基准（EgyptianBench）：EgyptianMMLU、Belebele-Arz、EgyptianHellaSwag/PIQA/WinoGrande/OpenBookQA、EgyptianRACE-H/M、EgyptianAlpacaEval、翻译、转写。阿拉伯文字（12B，全项最佳）：MMLU 62.59、Belebele 79.44、HellaSwag 64.04、PIQA 70.69、AlpacaEval 95.56，转写 BLEU 52.21 / chrF 80.97。Nile-Chat-4B 超过 ≤7B 模型（PIQA +1.2%、OpenBookQA +0.9%、AlpacaEval +1.6%）；在拉丁文字上 4B 相对次优模型大幅领先（HellaSwag +18.38%、PIQA +12.97%、WinoGrande +8.48%、RACE-H +11.91%、RACE-M +11.12%）。12B 在拉丁文字基准上比 Qwen2.5-14B-Instruct 提升 14.4%。3x4B-A6B MoE 在所有翻译与转写任务上取得最高分。
- **Key Findings / 主要发现:** Nile-Chat is the first LLM providing script-aware support (both Arabic and Latin) for a widely spoken dialect and significantly outperforms leading multilingual and Arabic LLMs on Egyptian benchmarks. The MoE (Branch-Train-MiX) approach mitigates catastrophic forgetting better than dense models. The work provides a comprehensive methodology for adapting LLMs to dual-script languages. | Nile-Chat 是首个为广泛使用的方言提供文字感知支持（阿拉伯文字与拉丁文字）的 LLM，在埃及基准上显著超越主流多语言与阿拉伯语 LLM。MoE（Branch-Train-MiX）方法比稠密模型更好地缓解灾难性遗忘。该工作提供了将 LLM 适配到双文字语言的完整方法论。
- **Limitations / 局限:** Occasional hallucinations; potential dataset biases; heavy reliance on Claude for translation/transliteration, which reflects Western cultural values and may miss Egyptian nuances. | 偶发幻觉；潜在数据集偏差；翻译/转写严重依赖 Claude，其体现西方文化价值观，可能遗漏埃及本土细微差别。

### Resource-Aware Arabic LLM Creation: Model Adaptation, Integration, and Multi-Domain Testing (Aryan, 2024)
- **Venue / Link:** arXiv preprint (arXiv:2412.17548; cs.CL/cs.AI), https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.17548
- **Motivation / 动机:** Demonstrate a resource-efficient method for building specialized Arabic language models on consumer-grade hardware (as little as 4GB VRAM), aiming to democratize access to advanced NLP for under-resourced linguistic communities and address Arabic's morphological complexity, dialectal variation, and diacritics. | 展示一种在消费级硬件（低至 4GB 显存）上构建专门化阿拉伯语模型的资源高效方法，旨在让欠资源语言社区也能使用先进 NLP，并应对阿拉伯语的形态复杂性、方言差异与变音符号问题。
- **Corpus & Data / 语料与数据:** Final dataset of 1,272,420 entries combining three sources: Bactrian corpus (67,017 entries; diverse Arabic across domains), Arabic portion of OpenAssistant (56 entries; task-oriented/conversational Arabic), and selected Arabic Wikipedia (1,205,403 entries; broad MSA coverage). Primarily MSA with some exposure to dialectal variation; a simple dialect-identification/tagging step marks dialectal portions. Preprocessing: text cleaning (remove non-Arabic chars, normalize whitespace), Alif/Ya normalization, configurable diacritics retention/removal, custom Arabic sentence segmentation. License: paper CC BY 4.0 (corpus licenses not detailed). | 最终数据集含 1,272,420 条，合并三个来源：Bactrian 语料（67,017 条；跨领域多样阿拉伯语）、OpenAssistant 阿拉伯语部分（56 条；任务导向/对话式阿拉伯语）、精选阿拉伯语维基百科（1,205,403 条；广泛 MSA 覆盖）。以 MSA 为主，兼有部分方言接触；一个简单的方言识别/标注步骤标记方言片段。预处理：文本清洗（移除非阿拉伯字符、规范空白）、Alif/Ya 归一化、可配置的变音符号保留/移除、自定义阿拉伯语句子切分。许可证：论文 CC BY 4.0（语料许可证未详述）。
- **Method / 方法:** Fine-tuning (adaptation), not from-scratch. Base model Qwen2-1.5B (1.5B parameters). Technique: Quantized Low-Rank Adaptation (QLoRA) with 4-bit NF4 quantization (double quant, bfloat16 compute); LoRA rank r=8, alpha=32, dropout 0.05, target modules Q/K/V/O projections. No vocabulary expansion (uses Arabic-specific embedding initialization on a subset of the embedding layer and diacritic-weighted attention instead). Memory optimizations: gradient accumulation, mixed-precision (16-bit), gradient checkpointing, 8-bit optimizer states in CPU memory, dynamic allocation capped at 3.5GB GPU / 12GB CPU. Multi-stage dialectal fine-tuning (MSA then dialect-specific). Hardware: AMD Ryzen 5 3550H, 16GB RAM, NVIDIA GTX 1650 4GB VRAM. | 微调（适配），非从头训练。基座模型 Qwen2-1.5B（15 亿参数）。技术：量化低秩适配（QLoRA），4-bit NF4 量化（双重量化，bfloat16 计算）；LoRA rank r=8、alpha=32、dropout 0.05，目标模块为 Q/K/V/O 投影。无词表扩展（改为在嵌入层子集上用阿拉伯语专用嵌入初始化，并采用变音符号加权注意力）。显存优化：梯度累积、混合精度（16-bit）、梯度检查点、8-bit 优化器状态放于 CPU 内存、动态分配上限 3.5GB GPU / 12GB CPU。多阶段方言微调（先 MSA 后特定方言）。硬件：AMD Ryzen 5 3550H、16GB 内存、NVIDIA GTX 1650 4GB 显存。
- **Models & Eval / 模型与评测:** 1.5B model. Training: batch size 1, gradient accumulation 16, LR 5e-5, warmup 100, max grad norm 0.3, up to 10,000 steps; final loss converged to 0.1083 (from ~0.18). Resource use: GPU memory steady ~1.97GB, RAM 6.5→9.2GB. Evaluation across text classification, question answering, dialect identification, and robustness. Perplexity: MSA 4.72 (fine-tuned) vs 6.81 (base); dialectal ~6.31 for both. Per-dialect (PPL / BLEU / F1): MSA 4.72/42.3/0.83, EGY 6.31/36.8/0.76, GLF 6.85/35.2/0.74, LEV 6.54/35.9/0.75, MGR 7.12/33.7/0.71. QA average F1 0.3 (vs 0.25 base), no exact matches. Robustness: at noise level 0.5 the fine-tuned model keeps a semantic similarity of 3.5 while the base model drops to 0. | 15 亿参数模型。训练：批大小 1，梯度累积 16，学习率 5e-5，warmup 100，最大梯度范数 0.3，最多 10,000 步；最终损失收敛至 0.1083（从约 0.18）。资源占用：GPU 显存稳定约 1.97GB，内存 6.5→9.2GB。评测覆盖文本分类、问答、方言识别与鲁棒性。困惑度：MSA 4.72（微调后）对 6.81（基座）；方言两者均约 6.31。各方言（PPL / BLEU / F1）：MSA 4.72/42.3/0.83、EGY 6.31/36.8/0.76、GLF 6.85/35.2/0.74、LEV 6.54/35.9/0.75、MGR 7.12/33.7/0.71。问答平均 F1 为 0.3（对基座 0.25），无精确匹配。鲁棒性：在噪声等级 0.5 时微调模型语义相似度保持 3.5，而基座模型降至 0。
- **Key Findings / 主要发现:** QLoRA enables effective Arabic adaptation of a 1.5B model on only 4GB VRAM. The fine-tuned model achieves lower MSA perplexity (4.72 vs 6.81), better QA F1 (0.3 vs 0.25), and markedly greater robustness to input perturbations; performance is strongest on MSA and Egyptian, weakest on Maghrebi. Diacritics handling and Arabic-specific preprocessing improve morphology handling. | QLoRA 使 15 亿参数模型仅用 4GB 显存即可有效适配阿拉伯语。微调模型获得更低的 MSA 困惑度（4.72 对 6.81）、更好的问答 F1（0.3 对 0.25），以及对输入扰动显著更强的鲁棒性；性能在 MSA 与埃及方言上最强、在马格里布方言上最弱。变音符号处理与阿拉伯语专用预处理提升了形态处理能力。
- **Limitations / 局限:** Dialectal Arabic remains weak — fine-tuned and base perplexities on dialect are similar (~6.31), suggesting more dialectal data is needed. Neither model achieves exact-match QA, so precise answer generation is limited. Single-author, small-scale study using modest hardware; evaluation is limited in task breadth and the approach is framed as foundational groundwork. | 方言阿拉伯语仍薄弱——方言上微调与基座困惑度相近（约 6.31），说明需要更多方言数据。两模型均未实现问答精确匹配，精确答案生成能力有限。单作者、小规模研究，使用普通硬件；评测任务覆盖面有限，工作定位为基础性铺垫。

### 101 Billion Arabic Words Dataset (Aloui et al., 2024)
- **Venue / Link:** arXiv preprint (arXiv:2405.01590, cs.CL), https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.01590
- **Motivation / 动机:** Arabic LLMs are biased because they rely on English data translated into Arabic, which compromises authenticity and reflects a perceived scarcity of original high-quality Arabic data; the authors want to refute that scarcity argument and enable culturally/linguistically authentic Arabic LLMs. | 阿拉伯语大模型因依赖"由英文翻译而来的阿拉伯语数据"而存在偏见，损害了内容的真实性，也反映出原生高质量阿拉伯语数据被认为稀缺的问题；作者希望反驳这种"数据稀缺"论调，支持在语言和文化上真正贴近本地的阿拉伯语大模型的开发。
- **Corpus & Data / 语料与数据:** Over 101 billion Arabic words; ~116 million documents/data points; ~89.1 million unique web pages after deduplication, amounting to 0.4 TB (400 GB). Source is exclusively Common Crawl WET files (weeks 39/2021–27/2022, i.e. Sept 2021–July 2022); the team analyzed 39,502 compressed WET files (0.8 PB). Initial pool was 13.2 billion web pages from 440,000 gzip sub-splits, ~110 million Arabic, reduced to 99 million URL-unique then 89.1 million post-dedup. Language: Modern Standard Arabic (MSA) only per the data card; dialects not separately covered. License: paper CC BY 4.0; the released HuggingFace dataset is listed as Apache 2.0. Distributed as JSONL. | 超过1010亿阿拉伯语词；约1.16亿个文档/数据点；去重后约8910万个唯一网页，总量0.4 TB（400 GB）。数据源仅为Common Crawl的WET文件（2021年第39周至2022年第27周，即2021年9月—2022年7月）；团队分析了39,502个压缩WET文件（0.8 PB）。初始池为来自440,000个gzip子分片的132亿网页，其中约1.1亿为阿拉伯语，先按URL去重至9900万，去重后剩8910万。语言：据数据卡仅为现代标准阿拉伯语（MSA），未单独覆盖方言。许可：论文为CC BY 4.0；发布在HuggingFace上的数据集标注为Apache 2.0。以JSONL格式分发。
- **Method / 方法:** Corpus-building only (no CPT/from-scratch training). Pipeline (following RefinedWeb principles): (1) URL filtering with a bad-word list plus the Rust `rustrict` crate; (2) URL deduplication via hash + instance UUID; (3) basic cleaning — removing too-short/too-long sequences, HTML tags and non-standard Unicode (Mozilla Readability + Tnkeeh), empty lines/repeated spaces; (4) Unicode normalization and dediacritization with CAMeL Tools; (5) document-level MinHash dedup plus paragraph-level signature dedup. Implemented in Rust with Redis pub/sub distributed computing on AWS EC2, giving a claimed 40x speedup. Only 4% of URLs flagged as undesirable. | 仅构建语料（未做继续预训练或从头训练）。流程（遵循RefinedWeb理念）：(1) 用敏感词表加Rust `rustrict` crate过滤URL；(2) 用哈希+实例UUID对URL去重；(3) 基础清洗——去除过短/过长序列、HTML标签与非标准Unicode（Mozilla Readability + Tnkeeh）、空行/多余空格；(4) 用CAMeL Tools做Unicode规范化与去变音符；(5) 文档级MinHash去重加段落级签名去重。用Rust实现，在AWS EC2上以Redis发布/订阅做分布式计算，号称提速40倍。仅4%的URL被标记为不良内容。
- **Models & Eval / 模型与评测:** No model was trained and no benchmark evaluation was performed (explicitly deferred due to computational constraints). Dataset analysis only: URL geographic distribution shows Saudi Arabian domains dominant; content scarce from North African Arab countries. | 未训练任何模型，也未做任何基准评测（因算力限制明确推迟）。仅做数据集分析：URL地理分布显示沙特阿拉伯域名占主导；北非阿拉伯国家内容稀缺。
- **Key Findings / 主要发现:** Web data alone, with careful filtering and deduplication, can yield the largest available Arabic corpus (101B words), demonstrating that high-quality Arabic web data is not as scarce as commonly believed. | 仅凭网络数据，经细致过滤与去重即可构建出目前最大的阿拉伯语语料（1010亿词），表明高质量阿拉伯语网络数据并不像普遍认为的那样稀缺。
- **Limitations / 局限:** No direct evaluation of cleanliness/effectiveness because no model was trained on it (compute constraints); URL-based filtering alone may not catch all harmful, biased, or inappropriate content; strong geographic skew toward Saudi Arabia and under-representation of North African content. | 未对清洁度/有效性做直接评测，因未在其上训练模型（算力限制）；仅靠基于URL的过滤可能无法捕获所有有害、偏见或不当内容；地理分布严重偏向沙特阿拉伯，北非内容代表性不足。

### On the Importance of Data Scale in Pretraining Arabic Language Models (Ghaddar et al., 2024)
- **Venue / Link:** arXiv preprint (arXiv:2401.07760, cs.CL); Huawei Noah's Ark Lab & Université de Montréal, https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.07760
- **Motivation / 动机:** Despite dozens of Arabic PLMs, it remains unclear which factor (data size/quality, model size, or architecture) drives NLP performance; the authors isolate the role of pretraining data by retraining SOTA Arabic models on much larger, highly filtered corpora. | 尽管已有数十个阿拉伯语预训练模型，但究竟哪个因素（数据规模/质量、模型规模还是架构）主导NLP性能仍不清楚；作者通过在更大、经高度过滤的语料上重训最先进的阿拉伯语模型，来单独考察预训练数据的作用。
- **Corpus & Data / 语料与数据:** Pretraining data derives from AraMUS (Alghamdi et al., 2023), mined from 90 monthly Common Crawl shards spanning 2013–2022: 8.7 TB of plain Arabic text reduced to 512 GB after deduplication and extensive filtering. This is 4x larger than the 128 GB used for the original JABER/AT5S/AT5B, and 2x larger than the corpora for AraBERTv2 and JASMINE. Tokenizer: byte-level BPE (BBPE), 64k vocabulary. Predominantly MSA-style web text; dialect handling not a focus (dialect tasks handled at eval). License: CC BY 4.0; models/code released (Huawei-Noah JABER-PyTorch). | 预训练数据源自AraMUS（Alghamdi等，2023），从跨2013–2022年的90个Common Crawl月度分片中挖掘：8.7 TB纯阿拉伯语文本，经去重与大量过滤后压缩为512 GB。这比原始JABER/AT5S/AT5B所用的128 GB大4倍，比AraBERTv2和JASMINE所用语料大2倍。分词器：字节级BPE（BBPE），词表6.4万。主要为MSA风格网络文本；方言处理非重点（方言任务在评测阶段处理）。许可：CC BY 4.0；模型/代码已开源（Huawei-Noah JABER-PyTorch）。
- **Method / 方法:** From-scratch pretraining of four new models by scaling data from 128 GB to 512 GB: JABERv2 (12-layer BERT-base, 135M), JABERv2-6L (6-layer BERT, 86M), AT5Sv2 (T5-small, 109M), AT5Bv2 (T5-base, 296M). Encoder-only: lr 1e-4, 10k warmup, AdamW, 15 epochs, seq len 128, batch 64/GPU. Encoder-decoder: Adafactor, lr 1, 200k steps, enc/dec seq 512/114. Trained on 8 nodes x 8 V100 (32GB). Then >5200 finetuning experiments across two benchmarks. | 通过将数据从128 GB扩展到512 GB，从头预训练四个新模型：JABERv2（12层BERT-base，135M）、JABERv2-6L（6层BERT，86M）、AT5Sv2（T5-small，109M）、AT5Bv2（T5-base，296M）。仅编码器：学习率1e-4，1万步预热，AdamW，15轮，序列长128，每GPU批量64。编码器-解码器：Adafactor，学习率1，20万步，编/解码序列长512/114。在8节点×8块V100（32GB）上训练。随后在两个基准上做了超过5200次微调实验。
- **Models & Eval / 模型与评测:** Evaluated on ORCA (29 tasks, macro-F1) and ALUE (8 tasks, 6 dialect-focused). SABER (BERT-large, 369M) became the new ORCA SOTA with score 77.1; JABERv2 ranked 2nd overall and 1st among base models, beating ARBERTv2 by 2.1% on ORCA. JABERv2-6L (half of ARBERTv2) trailed by only 0.3%. Notably AraMUS (11B T5) and SABER outperform AceGPT (13B, LLaMA-2 based) on ALUE. | 在ORCA（29任务，macro-F1）和ALUE（8任务，其中6个偏方言）上评测。SABER（BERT-large，369M）以77.1分成为ORCA新SOTA；JABERv2总体排第二、base级别排第一，在ORCA上超过ARBERTv2达2.1%。JABERv2-6L（体量为ARBERTv2一半）仅落后0.3%。值得注意的是AraMUS（11B T5）与SABER在ALUE上都胜过AceGPT（13B，基于LLaMA-2）。
- **Key Findings / 主要发现:** Pretraining data is by far the primary contributor to performance, surpassing model size and architecture. A T5-small on 4x more data matches a T5-base; a 6-layer BERT matches a 12-layer BERT at similar data size. Data scaling benefits encoder-decoder models more than encoder-only: AT5B→AT5Bv2 gains 3.1% on ORCA vs only 0.6% for JABER→JABERv2, narrowing the enc-dec/enc-only gap from 3.1% to 0.6%. | 预训练数据是性能的首要贡献因素，远超模型规模与架构。用4倍数据训练的T5-small可匹敌T5-base；相近数据量下6层BERT可匹敌12层BERT。数据扩展对编码器-解码器模型的收益大于仅编码器模型：AT5B→AT5Bv2在ORCA上提升3.1%，而JABER→JABERv2仅提升0.6%，使编解码/纯编码差距从3.1%缩小到0.6%。
- **Limitations / 局限:** No measurement of gains on Arabic generative tasks (no well-established NLG benchmark comparable to ALUE/ORCA was available at writing); no experiments with decoder-only models. | 未测量在阿拉伯语生成任务上的收益（写作时尚无可与ALUE/ORCA相媲美的成熟NLG基准）；未做仅解码器模型的实验。

### A Large and Diverse Arabic Corpus for Language Modeling (Ali et al., 2022/2023)
- **Venue / Link:** arXiv preprint (arXiv:2201.09227, cs.CL/cs.AI), v3 revised 8 May 2023, https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.09227

### ALLaM: Large Language Models for Arabic and English (Bari et al., 2024)
- **Venue / Link:** arXiv preprint (arXiv:2407.15390), cs.CL/cs.AI, https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.15390
- **Motivation / 动机:** Support the ecosystem of Arabic Language Technologies (ALT) by steering strong English models toward Arabic without catastrophic forgetting of English. | 通过将强大的英语模型迁移到阿拉伯语且不发生英语的灾难性遗忘，来支撑阿拉伯语语言技术（ALT）生态。
- **Corpus & Data / 语料与数据:** From-scratch model: 4T English tokens then 1.2T mixed AR/EN tokens; CPT models (7B/13B) continue from Llama-2 on 1.2T tokens (70B up to 600B). - **Limitations / 局限:** Exact original→expanded vocabulary sizes not reported; some detailed benchmark tables provided only as images; optimal AR/EN ratio reported inconsistently (45/55 vs 45/45); no specific open-source license named in text. | 未报告词表由原始扩展到最终的具体规模；部分详细基准表仅以图片形式给出；最佳阿/英比例报告不一致（45/55 与 45/45）；正文未指明具体开源许可证。

- **Limitations / 局限:** Arabic still a minority share (~40%) vs English despite Arabic-centric framing; 7B/9B scale caps capability; heavy reliance on web/Common Crawl and translated/derived data; benchmark leadership is size-conditional. | 尽管以阿拉伯语为核心，阿拉伯语仍为少数占比（约 40%），低于英语；7B/9B 规模限制能力上限；高度依赖网络/Common Crawl 及翻译/派生数据；基准领先是"同规模条件下"的领先。

- **Venue / Link:** ACL 2021, https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.01785
- **Motivation / 动机:** Multilingual models are costly at inference and underperform on Arabic's many dialects, and dialectal Arabic evaluation is fragmented. The authors build two dedicated models—one for MSA, one for MSA+dialects—plus a unified benchmark. | 多语言模型推理代价高且在阿拉伯语众多方言上表现不佳，方言评测也很分散。作者构建两个专用模型——一个面向 MSA，一个面向 MSA+方言——并提出统一基准。
- **Corpus & Data / 语料与数据:** ARBERT: 61 GB of MSA text, 6.5B tokens, from Arabic Gigaword 5th Edition, OSCAR (MSA + Egyptian portions), OSIAN, and Arabic Wikipedia (Dec 2019). MARBERT: ~1 billion Arabic tweets (filtered from an in-house set of ~6B tweets, keeping those with ≥3 Arabic words), totaling 128 GB / 15.6B tokens, covering both MSA and diverse dialects. MARBERTv2 (later): trained on additional data (~29B tokens) and longer. Paper is CC BY 4.0; models on GitHub (UBC-NLP/marbert). | ARBERT：61 GB MSA 文本、65 亿词元，来自 Arabic Gigaword 第五版、OSCAR（MSA 与埃及方言部分）、OSIAN 及阿拉伯语维基百科（2019 年 12 月）。MARBERT：约 10 亿条阿拉伯语推文（从内部约 60 亿条推文中筛选，保留含至少 3 个阿拉伯词者），共 128 GB / 156 亿词元，覆盖 MSA 与多种方言。MARBERTv2（后续）：使用额外数据（约 290 亿词元）并训练更久。论文采用 CC BY 4.0；模型在 GitHub（UBC-NLP/marbert）发布。
- **Method / 方法:** Both trained from scratch on BERT-base config: 12 layers, 768 hidden, 12 heads (ARBERT ~163M params; MARBERT ~160M params), 100K WordPiece vocabulary. ARBERT trained 8M steps (~42 epochs over 6.5B tokens). MARBERT drops the next-sentence-prediction (NSP) objective since tweets are short; tweets normalized with generic URL/USER/HASHTAG tokens. | 两者均从零训练，采用 BERT-base 配置：12 层、768 隐藏维、12 头（ARBERT 约 1.63 亿参数；MARBERT 约 1.60 亿参数），10 万 WordPiece 词表。ARBERT 训练 800 万步（在 65 亿词元上约 42 轮）。MARBERT 因推文较短去掉下一句预测（NSP）目标；推文用通用 URL/USER/HASHTAG 词元归一化。
- **Models & Eval / 模型与评测:** Introduces ARLUE benchmark: 42 datasets in 6 task clusters (sentiment, social meaning, topic, dialect identification, NER, QA). New SOTA on 37/48 classification tasks. Top ARLUE macro-average 77.40 (MARBERTv2) > XLM-R Large 76.55 > ARBERT 76.07; best model is ~3.4x smaller than XLM-R Large (~160M vs ~550M params). | 提出 ARLUE 基准：42 个数据集、6 个任务簇（情感、社会意义、主题、方言识别、NER、问答）。在 48 个分类任务中的 37 个取得新 SOTA。ARLUE 宏平均最高 77.40（MARBERTv2）> XLM-R Large 76.55 > ARBERT 76.07；最佳模型比 XLM-R Large 小约 3.4 倍（约 1.6 亿 vs 约 5.5 亿参数）。
- **Key Findings / 主要发现:** Dedicated Arabic models beat much larger multilingual models; MARBERT excels on social-media/dialect tasks thanks to Twitter pre-training; MARBERTv2 gives the best overall performance. | 专用阿拉伯语模型胜过体量大得多的多语言模型；MARBERT 凭借推特预训练在社交媒体/方言任务上表现突出；MARBERTv2 整体表现最佳。
- **Limitations / 局限:** Twitter data carries demographic/topical bias (discussed in ethical considerations); dialect coverage is uneven across the Arab world; ARBERT is MSA-only. | 推特数据带有人口/主题偏差（伦理部分有讨论）；方言覆盖在阿拉伯世界各地不均衡；ARBERT 仅覆盖 MSA。

### CAMeLBERT: The Interplay of Variant, Size, and Task Type in Arabic Pre-trained Language Models (Inoue et al., 2021)
- **Venue / Link:** WANLP 2021 (ACL Anthology), https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.06678
- **Motivation / 动机:** To disentangle how three factors—language variant (MSA/DA/CA), pre-training data size, and fine-tuning task type—affect Arabic PLM performance, and to derive a principled model-selection strategy. | 旨在厘清三个因素——语言变体（MSA/DA/CA）、预训练数据规模、微调任务类型——如何影响阿拉伯语预训练模型的表现，并推导出有原则的模型选择策略。
- **Corpus & Data / 语料与数据:** Total 167 GB / 17.3B words across variants. MSA: 107 GB (Arabic Gigaword 5th, Abu El-Khair Corpus, OSIAN, Arabic Wikipedia, unshuffled Arabic OSCAR). Dialectal (DA): 54 GB (collection of dialectal corpora incl. CALLHOME, multidialectal parallel corpora). Classical (CA): 6 GB (OpenITI corpus v1.2). Mix: full 167 GB concatenation. Also scaled-down MSA subsets (half, quarter, eighth, sixteenth) to isolate size effects. | 三种变体合计 167 GB / 173 亿词。MSA：107 GB（Arabic Gigaword 第五版、Abu El-Khair 语料、OSIAN、阿拉伯语维基百科、未打乱的阿拉伯语 OSCAR）。方言（DA）：54 GB（方言语料集合，含 CALLHOME、多方言平行语料）。古典（CA）：6 GB（OpenITI 语料 v1.2）。混合：全部 167 GB 拼接。另有按比例缩减的 MSA 子集（1/2、1/4、1/8、1/16）用于隔离规模影响。
- **Method / 方法:** From scratch, BERT-base architecture. A single WordPiece tokenizer (vocab 30,000) trained on the full 167 GB. Whole-word masking, duplicate factor 10. Pre-trained 1M steps total (900K steps at seq-len 128 batch 1024, then 100K steps at seq-len 512 batch 256) on a TPU v3-8, ~4.5 days per model. Preprocessing removes diacritics/kashida via CAMeL Tools. | 从零训练，BERT-base 架构。在全部 167 GB 上训练单一 WordPiece 分词器（词表 30,000）。采用整词掩码，重复因子 10。共训练 100 万步（前 90 万步序列长 128、批 1024，后 10 万步序列长 512、批 256），使用 TPU v3-8，每个模型约 4.5 天。预处理用 CAMeL Tools 去除变音符号/kashida。
- **Models & Eval / 模型与评测:** Releases CAMeLBERT-MSA, -DA, -CA, -Mix plus 4 scaled-down MSA models; compared to 8 public models across 5 tasks / 12 datasets: NER (ANERcorp), POS (PATB/ARZTB/Gumar), sentiment (ASTD/ArSAS/SemEval), dialect identification (MADAR-26/6/Twitter-5, NADI), and poetry classification (APCD). | 发布 CAMeLBERT-MSA、-DA、-CA、-Mix 及 4 个缩减版 MSA 模型；在 5 个任务 / 12 个数据集上与 8 个公开模型对比：NER（ANERcorp）、词性标注（PATB/ARZTB/Gumar）、情感（ASTD/ArSAS/SemEval）、方言识别（MADAR-26/6/Twitter-5、NADI）以及诗歌分类（APCD）。
- **Key Findings / 主要发现:** Variant proximity between pre-training data and task data matters more than pre-training data size—e.g., the 6 GB CA model beats the 107 GB MSA model on CA-proximal tasks; CAMeLBERT-Mix is the best choice for dialectal tasks. This yields an optimized model-selection recipe. | 预训练数据与任务数据之间的变体接近度比预训练数据规模更重要——例如 6 GB 的 CA 模型在与古典阿拉伯语接近的任务上胜过 107 GB 的 MSA 模型；CAMeLBERT-Mix 是方言任务的最佳选择。由此得出优化的模型选择方案。
- **Limitations / 局限:** DA is highly heterogeneous (many dialects lumped together); the study covers only three broad variants; size-vs-proximity trade-off measured on a limited task set. | DA 高度异质（众多方言被合并处理）；研究仅覆盖三大变体；规模与接近度的权衡仅在有限任务集上衡量。

### AraT5: Text-to-Text Transformers for Arabic Language Generation (Nagoudi et al., 2022)
- **Venue / Link:** ACL 2022, https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.12068
- **Motivation / 动机:** It is unclear how well multilingual mT5 fares on non-English tasks with diverse data; the authors build dedicated Arabic T5 encoder-decoders for generation, choosing Arabic for its wide dialectal variety and heavy social-media use. | 尚不清楚多语言 mT5 在数据多样的非英语任务上表现如何；作者构建专用阿拉伯语 T5 编码器-解码器用于生成，选择阿拉伯语是因其方言多样且社交媒体使用广泛。
- **Corpus & Data / 语料与数据:** AraT5-MSA: 70 GB MSA (7.1B tokens) from AraNews, Abu El-Khair, Gigaword, OSCAR, OSIAN, Arabic Wikipedia. AraT5-Tweet (TW): 178 GB of tweets (filtered from an in-house set of ~10B tweets, keeping ≥3 Arabic words). AraT5 (combined MSA+Twitter): 248 GB / 29B tokens—about 49% less than the 57B Arabic tokens mT5 saw. Twitter data is ~28.39% dialect / 71.61% MSA. Vocabulary: 110K WordPieces (SentencePiece), deliberately including 11 non-Arabic languages (e.g., English, French). | AraT5-MSA：70 GB MSA（71 亿词元），来自 AraNews、Abu El-Khair、Gigaword、OSCAR、OSIAN、阿拉伯语维基百科。AraT5-Tweet（TW）：178 GB 推文（从内部约 100 亿条推文中筛选，保留含至少 3 个阿拉伯词者）。AraT5（MSA+推特合并）：248 GB / 290 亿词元——比 mT5 所见的 570 亿阿拉伯语词元少约 49%。推特数据约 28.39% 为方言 / 71.61% 为 MSA。词表：11 万 WordPieces（SentencePiece），有意纳入 11 种非阿拉伯语言（如英语、法语）。
- **Method / 方法:** Trained from scratch on the T5-Base encoder-decoder; each of encoder/decoder is BERT-base-like (12 layers, 12 heads, 768 hidden), ~220M parameters total. Span-corruption denoising objective (15% of tokens dropped, spans replaced by sentinel tokens). Three models pre-trained (AraT5-MSA, AraT5-TW, AraT5) each for 1M steps, ~80 days each on a TPU v3-8. | 基于 T5-Base 编码器-解码器从零训练；编码器/解码器各类似 BERT-base（12 层、12 头、768 隐藏维），总计约 2.2 亿参数。采用片段破坏去噪目标（丢弃 15% 词元，连续片段以哨兵词元替代）。预训练三个模型（AraT5-MSA、AraT5-TW、AraT5），各训练 100 万步，每个在 TPU v3-8 上约 80 天。
- **Models & Eval / 模型与评测:** Introduces ARGEN benchmark: 19 datasets / 59 test splits across 7 tasks—machine translation, code-switched translation, summarization, news-title generation, question generation, transliteration, paraphrasing. Models beat mT5 on 52/59 test sets and set new SOTA on the majority of datasets; e.g., AraT5-MSA transliteration 65.88 BLEU (+7.1 over prior SOTA); best MT in 37/42 tests. Also new SOTA on the ARLUE understanding benchmark. | 提出 ARGEN 基准：19 个数据集 / 59 个测试划分，覆盖 7 个任务——机器翻译、语码转换翻译、摘要、新闻标题生成、问题生成、转写、复述。模型在 59 个测试集中的 52 个上胜过 mT5，并在多数数据集取得新 SOTA；例如 AraT5-MSA 转写 65.88 BLEU（比先前 SOTA 高 7.1 分）；机器翻译在 42 项测试中的 37 项最优。同时在 ARLUE 理解基准上取得新 SOTA。
- **Key Findings / 主要发现:** Dedicated Arabic T5 models substantially outperform mT5 on both generation and understanding despite ~49% less pre-training data, demonstrating the value of language-specific pre-training. | 尽管预训练数据少约 49%，专用阿拉伯语 T5 模型在生成与理解任务上都大幅胜过 mT5，证明了语言专用预训练的价值。
- **Limitations / 局限:** Pre-training is very expensive (~80 days per model); a few tasks (e.g., EASC summarization) are still beaten by other systems; only base-size models released. | 预训练成本极高（每个模型约 80 天）；少数任务（如 EASC 摘要）仍被其他系统超越；仅发布 base 规模模型。

### EgyBERT: A Large Language Model Pretrained on Egyptian Dialect Corpora (Qarah, 2024)
- **Venue / Link:** arXiv preprint (2024), https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.03524
- **Motivation / 动机:** Existing Arabic models excel on MSA but underperform on dialectal Arabic; this is the first study to systematically evaluate language models on Egyptian-dialect datasets, motivating a dedicated Egyptian model to fill the gap. | 现有阿拉伯语模型在 MSA 上表现优异，但在方言阿拉伯语上表现欠佳；这是首个系统评估语言模型在埃及方言数据集上表现的研究，从而推动构建专用的埃及方言模型以填补空白。
- **Corpus & Data / 语料与数据:** 10.4 GB of Egyptian dialectal text from two newly introduced corpora: Egyptian Tweets Corpus (ETC)—over 34.33M tweets (24.89M sentences), 2.5 GB, filtered from an in-house set of 800M+ Arabic tweets; and Egyptian Forums Corpus (EFC)—44.42M+ sentences, 7.9 GB, from various Egyptian online forums. Described as the largest Egyptian dialectal corpora reported to date; released on Hugging Face under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. | 10.4 GB 埃及方言文本，来自两个新提出的语料：埃及推文语料（ETC）——超过 3433 万条推文（2489 万句），2.5 GB，从内部 8 亿多条阿拉伯语推文中筛选；埃及论坛语料（EFC）——4442 万多句，7.9 GB，来自各类埃及在线论坛。号称迄今报道的最大埃及方言语料；在 Hugging Face 以 CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 发布。
- **Method / 方法:** Trained from scratch following the original BERT architecture: 12 encoder layers, 12 attention heads, 768 hidden units, vocabulary 75,000 WordPieces (SentencePiece tokenizer). MLM-only objective (15% token masking, no NSP). Pre-trained 24.5 epochs (10.65M steps, 774 hours), max sequence length 128, batch size 64, AdamW, learning rate 5e-5. (~110M params, BERT-base.) | 遵循原始 BERT 架构从零训练：12 层编码器、12 注意力头、768 隐藏单元，词表 75,000 WordPieces（SentencePiece 分词器）。仅 MLM 目标（15% 词元掩码，无 NSP）。预训练 24.5 轮（1065 万步，774 小时），最大序列长 128，批大小 64，AdamW，学习率 5e-5。（约 1.1 亿参数，BERT-base。）
- **Models & Eval / 模型与评测:** Single EgyBERT model compared against five multidialectal Arabic models (AraBERTv02-Twitter, QARiB, CAMeLBERT-DA, MARBERTv1, MARBERTv2) across 10 Egyptian dialectal datasets. EgyBERT achieved the highest average F1 84.25% and accuracy 87.33%, ahead of the second-best MARBERTv2 (F1 83.68%, accuracy 87.19%). | 单一 EgyBERT 模型与五个多方言阿拉伯语模型（AraBERTv02-Twitter、QARiB、CAMeLBERT-DA、MARBERTv1、MARBERTv2）在 10 个埃及方言数据集上对比。EgyBERT 取得最高平均 F1 84.25% 与准确率 87.33%，领先第二名 MARBERTv2（F1 83.68%，准确率 87.19%）。
- **Key Findings / 主要发现:** A dialect-specific model pretrained solely on Egyptian data outperforms all general multidialectal baselines on Egyptian-dialect tasks, and cross-model performance varies markedly—strong evidence for the need for dialect-specific models. | 仅用埃及数据预训练的方言专用模型在埃及方言任务上胜过所有通用多方言基线，且各模型表现差异显著——为方言专用模型的必要性提供了有力证据。
- **Limitations / 局限:** Covers only the Egyptian dialect; corpus is moderate (10.4 GB) versus general Arabic models; single model size; evaluation limited to classification-style Egyptian datasets. | 仅覆盖埃及方言；相较通用阿拉伯语模型语料规模适中（10.4 GB）；仅单一模型规模；评测局限于分类式埃及数据集。
