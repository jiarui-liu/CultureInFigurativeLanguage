> **Scope / 范围:** This review covers research that (a) performs **continued / continual pretraining (CPT)** to adapt an existing LLM to Hindi, and (b) **introduces a pretraining corpus / dataset** for Hindi, including from-scratch Hindi/Indic foundation models whose papers describe their Hindi pretraining data. Because Hindi work is frequently framed as pan-**Indic**, multilingual Indic efforts in which Hindi is a central language are included, with the Hindi portion emphasized. Tokenization work for Devanagari/Indic is also covered.
> 本综述涵盖：(a) 通过**持续/继续预训练（CPT）**将既有大模型适配到印地语；(b) 为印地语**引入预训练语料/数据集**，包括其论文描述了印地语预训练数据的从零训练印地语/印度语系（Indic）基座模型。由于印地语研究常以泛**印度语系**形式出现，本综述纳入以印地语为核心的多语印度语系工作，并突出其中的印地语部分；同时收录面向天城文/印度语系的分词（tokenization）工作。

> **Method / 方法说明:** Candidate papers were discovered via comprehensive web search; each paper's full text was then read by a dedicated sub-agent that extracted real figures (Hindi token counts, corpus sizes, benchmark scores) and produced the bilingual (English + Chinese) summary below following a fixed template. Two prompt-level descriptive errors and one ID mismatch surfaced during full-text reading are corrected in *Access Limitations & Corrections*.
> 候选论文通过全面网络检索获得；随后由专门子代理阅读每篇论文全文、抽取真实数据（印地语 token 数、语料规模、基准分数），并按固定模板生成下方双语（英文+中文）摘要。在全文阅读中发现的两处描述性偏差与一处编号不匹配，已在*访问限制与更正*中修正。

It is the base ("ancestor") model that Airavata (below) instruction-tunes. No peer-reviewed paper accompanies OpenHathi; details beyond this are not fabricated here. | **OpenHathi**（Sarvam AI，2023）仅以博客形式发布——一个在印地语上持续预训练、并扩展了印地语分词器/词表的 LLaMA-2 7B 模型。它是下文 Airavata 进行指令微调所基于的基座（“前身”）模型。OpenHathi 没有配套的同行评审论文，本文不对其做超出此范围的杜撰。

### Airavata: Introducing Hindi Instruction-tuned LLM (Gala et al., 2024)
- **Venue / Link:** arXiv preprint (work-in-progress, AI4Bharat), Jan 2024, https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.15006
- **Motivation / 动机:** Existing multilingual LLMs underperform on Hindi, and lacked instruction-tuned open models with proper Hindi vocabulary expansion; the goal is an open Hindi assistant plus reusable Indic instruction data and evaluation. | 现有多语言大模型在印地语上表现不佳，且缺乏经过恰当印地语词表扩展的开源指令微调模型；目标是构建一个开源印地语助手，并提供可复用的印度语系指令数据与评测框架。
- **Corpus & Data / 语料与数据:** Releases **IndicInstruct**: ~404k translated examples filtered (chrF++≥50 back-translation) to **385k** used for tuning. Sources: FLAN-v2 (~67K), Anthropic-HHH (5K), Dolly (~15K), OpenAssistant (~20K), LMSYS-Chat (50K), NMT/BPCC bitext (50K), native wikiHow (~27K En+Hi), native Anudesh (crowd prompts + Llama-2-70B responses). Languages: Hindi + English. Translation via IndicTrans2 (no proprietary-model outputs). Mixed licenses (Apache-2.0, MIT, CC-BY-SA-3.0, CC-BY-4.0, CC-0); paper CC-BY-4.0. | 发布 **IndicInstruct** 数据集：约 40.4 万条翻译样本经 chrF++≥50 回译过滤后保留 **38.5 万条** 用于微调。来源包括 FLAN-v2（约 6.7 万）、Anthropic-HHH（5 千）、Dolly（约 1.5 万）、OpenAssistant（约 2 万）、LMSYS-Chat（5 万）、NMT/BPCC 双语平行语料（5 万）、原生 wikiHow（约 2.7 万，英+印）、原生 Anudesh（众包提示 + Llama-2-70B 回复）。语言：印地语 + 英语。翻译使用 IndicTrans2（未使用专有模型输出）。许可证混合（Apache-2.0、MIT、CC-BY-SA-3.0、CC-BY-4.0、CC-0）；论文为 CC-BY-4.0。
- **Method / 方法:** **CPT-then-SFT**: base model is **OpenHathi** (LLaMA-2 7B extended with Hindi continued pretraining + Hindi vocab/tokenizer expansion by Sarvam AI). Airavata itself does supervised fine-tuning via **LoRA** (rank 16, α 32, dropout 0.05, 4 epochs, LR 5e-4, batch 128, bf16), plus checkpoint averaging (interp. ~0.6). LoRA chosen over full FT because full FT degraded English. | **先持续预训练后指令微调**：基座为 **OpenHathi**（Sarvam AI 在 LLaMA-2 7B 上做印地语持续预训练并扩展印地语词表/分词器）。Airavata 本身用 **LoRA**（秩 16，α 32，dropout 0.05，4 轮，学习率 5e-4，批量 128，bf16）做监督微调，并做检查点平均（插值系数约 0.6）。选择 LoRA 而非全量微调，因全量微调会损害英语能力。
- **Models & Eval / 模型与评测:** **7B**. Native Indic benchmarks (IndicXTREME: IndicSentiment, IndicCOPA, IndicXNLI, IndicXParaphrase, IndicQA; IndicNLG Headline/WikiBio; Flores/IN22; Hindi HateCheck) + translated (MMLU, HellaSwag, ARC, Winogrande, BoolQ, TruthfulQA, Toxigen, Implicit Hate). Results: IndicXNLI F1 16.67→**73.26**; IndicXParaphrase **76.53**; MMLU-En 36.16→**41.39** (Hi-translated 34.96); IndicQA F1 20.69→**37.69**. Reported 5–15 pt English-vs-translated-Hindi gap. | **7B**。原生印度语系基准（IndicXTREME：IndicSentiment、IndicCOPA、IndicXNLI、IndicXParaphrase、IndicQA；IndicNLG 标题/WikiBio；Flores/IN22；印地语 HateCheck）+ 翻译基准（MMLU、HellaSwag、ARC、Winogrande、BoolQ、TruthfulQA、Toxigen、Implicit Hate）。结果：IndicXNLI F1 16.67→**73.26**；IndicXParaphrase **76.53**；MMLU-英 36.16→**41.39**（印地语翻译 34.96）；IndicQA F1 20.69→**37.69**。英语与翻译印地语之间存在 5–15 分差距。
- **Key Findings / 主要发现:** Instruction tuning greatly improves NLU over OpenHathi; Airavata produces more natural-sounding Hindi than GPT-4 in human eval but trails on instruction-following/content quality; weaker on open-ended NLG/creative tasks; limited cross-lingual transfer between English and Hindi. | 指令微调相较 OpenHathi 大幅提升自然语言理解；人工评测中 Airavata 生成的印地语比 GPT-4 更自然，但在指令遵循/内容质量上落后；在开放式生成/创意任务上较弱；英印之间的跨语言迁移有限。
- **Limitations / 局限:** Prone to hallucination and fabricated/biased content; struggles on complex/specialized topics and mixed-language handling; human eval not robust (50 prompts, single annotator); explicitly research-only, not for production. | 易产生幻觉与虚构/带偏见内容；在复杂/专业主题及混合语言处理上表现欠佳；人工评测不够充分（50 条提示、单一标注者）；明确仅供研究，不建议生产使用。

### Llama-3-Nanda-10B-Chat: An Open Generative LLM for Hindi (Choudhury et al., 2025)
- **Venue / Link:** arXiv preprint, Apr 2025 (G42), CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0, https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.06011
- **Motivation / 动机:** Building LLMs for moderate-resource Hindi faces data-availability, model-adaptation, and evaluation challenges; goal is a state-of-the-art open Hindi-centric instruction-tuned model via efficient adaptation of a strong English base. | 为中等资源的印地语构建大模型面临数据可得性、模型适配与评测方面的挑战；目标是通过对强英语基座的高效适配，打造领先的开源印地语中心指令微调模型。
- **Corpus & Data / 语料与数据:** **65B-token Hindi corpus** (trained on 55B Hindi tokens) with a **1:1 English replay mix** to prevent forgetting / stability gap. Sources: websites, news, books, Wikipedia, HuggingFace Hindi datasets, IIT-Bombay En-Hi Parallel Corpus, HPLT. Preprocessing: filtering + LSH dedup (kept ~42% of raw). SFT: ~81K instructions (English ~39K/~17M tokens; Hindi ~22K machine-translated, formal + casual/Hinglish, ~14M tokens), oversampled 300%, ~100M SFT tokens; ~20K safety samples (8 attack types, 100+ categories). | **650 亿词元印地语语料**（实际训练 550 亿印地语词元），并按 **1:1 英语回放混合** 以防遗忘/缓解稳定性缺口。来源：网站、新闻、书籍、维基百科、HuggingFace 印地语数据集、IIT-Bombay 英印平行语料、HPLT。预处理：过滤 + LSH 去重（保留约 42% 原始文本）。指令微调：约 8.1 万条指令（英语约 3.9 万/约 1700 万词元；印地语约 2.2 万条机翻，含正式与口语/Hinglish，约 1400 万词元），过采样 300%，约 1 亿 SFT 词元；约 2 万条安全样本（8 类攻击、100+ 类别）。
- **Method / 方法:** **CPT via block expansion**: base **Llama-3-8B** grown to **10B** using the Llama Pro method (32→40 decoder blocks, one new block every 4, identity-initialized, backbone frozen, adapters ~25% of layers). **Vocabulary expansion** to 153,856 (+20%, "ExtVocab20"), cutting Hindi fertility 2.61→**1.19** (English 1.35); new embeddings via WECHSEL/semantic similarity (OpenAI text-embedding-3-large, top-k=5). Context 8,192; peak LR 1.5e-4; batch 4M tokens. Then SFT + safety alignment. | **通过块扩展做持续预训练**：基座 **Llama-3-8B** 用 Llama Pro 方法扩展至 **10B**（解码器块 32→40，每 4 块插入 1 个新块，恒等初始化，冻结主干，适配层约占 25%）。**词表扩展** 至 153,856（+20%，即 “ExtVocab20”），将印地语 fertility 从 2.61 降至 **1.19**（英语 1.35）；新嵌入通过 WECHSEL/语义相似度初始化（OpenAI text-embedding-3-large，top-k=5）。上下文 8,192；峰值学习率 1.5e-4；批量 400 万词元。随后做 SFT + 安全对齐。
- **Models & Eval / 模型与评测:** **10B chat**. Hindi log-likelihood avg **0.4096** (vs Nemotron-4-Mini-Hindi 0.4103); MMLU-hi 0.4299, HellaSwag-hi 0.4922. English avg **0.6096** (TruthfulQA-MC2 0.6190 best). Safety (% safe): English 85.97, Hindi 87.96 (comparable to Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct). Generation (Vicuna-80, GPT-4o judge): best in Hindi generation, beating all baselines incl. Nemotron-Hi-4B. | **10B 对话模型**。印地语对数似然平均 **0.4096**（对比 Nemotron-4-Mini-Hindi 0.4103）；MMLU-hi 0.4299，HellaSwag-hi 0.4922。英语平均 **0.6096**（TruthfulQA-MC2 0.6190 最佳）。安全（安全回复占比）：英语 85.97%，印地语 87.96%（与 Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct 相当）。生成（Vicuna-80，GPT-4o 评判）：印地语生成最佳，超越包括 Nemotron-Hi-4B 在内的所有基线。

Tools open-sourced (IndicXlit, IndicTrans2, open-instruct); code at github.com/AI4Bharat/romansetu. | CPT 语料为每种语言约 5 亿词的文档级网络爬取文本(IndicCorp)。指令数据:每种语言 12 万条 = Flan 6.5 万 + Dolly 1.5 万 + BPCC 人工翻译子集每方向 2 万。五种印度语言,涵盖两个语系四种文字——印度雅利安语系:印地语、马拉地语、古吉拉特语;达罗毗荼语系:泰米尔语、马拉雅拉姆语(均为中低资源、SOV 语序、元音附标文字)。印地语是全篇核心的印度雅利安语系测试语言(其原生 fertility 7.36 罗马化后降至 2.98;罗马化嵌入与英语余弦相似度 0.50,原生仅 0.40)。工具开源(IndicXlit、IndicTrans2、open-instruct),代码见 github.com/AI4Bharat/romansetu。
- **Method / 方法:** CPT (full fine-tuning, NOT LoRA, NO vocabulary expansion) of Llama 2 7B on romanized target-language text, mixing equal English data to prevent catastrophic forgetting, followed by instruction tuning on romanized data (loss only on output tokens). Romanization uses IndicXlit (natural/lossy transliteration, lower fertility, preferred over deterministic ITRANS/IndicNLP). IFT datasets are English data translated via IndicTrans2 then romanized via IndicXlit. Model variants: base, CPT-Native, CPT-Roman, IFT-Native, IFT-Roman. Hyperparams: CPT 1M-token batch, LR 5e-5, 1 epoch, seq 2048; IFT batch 128, LR 5e-5, 1 epoch. | 对 Llama 2 7B 在罗马化的目标语言文本上做 CPT(全参数微调,非 LoRA,不扩充词表),混入等量英文数据以防灾难性遗忘,随后在罗马化数据上做指令微调(仅在输出 token 上计损失)。罗马化用 IndicXlit(自然/有损音译,fertility 更低,优于确定性的 ITRANS/IndicNLP)。指令微调数据由英文经 IndicTrans2 翻译再经 IndicXlit 罗马化得到。模型变体:base、CPT-原生、CPT-罗马、IFT-原生、IFT-罗马。超参:CPT 每批 100 万 token,学习率 5e-5,1 轮,序列长 2048;IFT 批 128,学习率 5e-5,1 轮。
- **Models & Eval / 模型与评测:** Base Llama 2 7B (findings replicated on BLOOM 7B). Metrics: chrF (SacreBLEU) for MT, RougeL for other NLG, F1/Accuracy for NLU. Romanization reduces token fertility 2x-4x (Hindi 7.36->2.98, Gujarati 18.44->3.39). Zero-shot IFT results (Native vs Roman): MT chrF En->XX 37.40 vs 46.87 (~+8 for roman), XX->En 49.78 vs 50.75; NLG XLSum RougeL 7.77 vs 12.56, IndicHeadline 12.61 vs 16.03; NLU IndicCOPA 37.3 vs 45.45 (roman better) but IndicSentiment 88.88 vs 82.44 and IndicXNLI 42.3 vs 38.38 (native better); MMLU 24.59 vs 26.28; IndicQA-without-context ~+10 F1 for roman. | 基座 Llama 2 7B(结论在 BLOOM 7B 上复现)。指标:机翻用 chrF,其他 NLG 用 RougeL,NLU 用 F1/准确率。罗马化将 token fertility 降低 2-4 倍(印地语 7.36->2.98,古吉拉特语 18.44->3.39)。零样本 IFT 结果(原生 vs 罗马):机翻 chrF En->XX 37.40 vs 46.87(罗马约 +8),XX->En 49.78 vs 50.75;NLG XLSum RougeL 7.77 vs 12.56,IndicHeadline 12.61 vs 16.03;NLU IndicCOPA 37.3 vs 45.45(罗马更优),但 IndicSentiment 88.88 vs 82.44、IndicXNLI 42.3 vs 38.38(原生更优);MMLU 24.59 vs 26.28;无上下文 IndicQA 罗马约 +10 F1。
- **Key Findings / 主要发现:** Romanized representation matches or outperforms native script across most NLU, NLG and MT tasks in a decoder-only English LLM — the first work to show romanization benefits generation (not just understanding). Romanized embeddings align more closely with English than native-script ones. Reduced fertility (2x-4x) yields efficiency gains. Results hold on both Llama 2 and BLOOM. | 在纯解码器英语大模型上,罗马化表示在多数 NLU、NLG 与机翻任务上追平或超越原生文字——这是首篇证明罗马化对生成(而不仅是理解)有益的工作。罗马化嵌入比原生文字更贴近英语。fertility 降低(2-4 倍)带来效率提升。结论在 Llama 2 与 BLOOM 上均成立。
- **Limitations / 局限:** Natural romanizations are lossy; back-transliteration to native script introduces errors. Limited to 7B model and limited pretraining data due to compute. Only Indian languages tested; some benchmarks are translated. High-quality transliteration models may not exist for all languages. | 自然罗马化有损;回转成原生文字会引入错误。受算力所限只用 7B 模型和有限预训练数据。仅测试印度语言;部分基准为翻译得来。并非所有语言都有高质量音译模型。

### Continually Adding New Languages to Multilingual Language Models (Owodunni & Kumar, 2025)
- **Venue / Link:** arXiv preprint (cs.CL), CC BY 4.0, https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.11414
- **Motivation / 动机:** Multilingual models cover a fixed language set and adding languages usually needs full retraining, which is costly and often infeasible because original pretraining data is unreleased. Naive continued pretraining causes catastrophic forgetting, and replay-based mitigation fails without access to original data. The paper studies adding new languages assuming access ONLY to target-language pretraining data. | 多语模型覆盖固定语言集,新增语言通常需完整重训,代价高且常不可行(原始预训练数据未公开)。朴素的持续预训练会导致灾难性遗忘,而基于回放的缓解在无原始数据时失效。本文研究在仅能访问目标语言预训练数据的前提下新增语言。
- **Corpus & Data / 语料与数据:** All pretraining data from FineWeb 2 (Penedo et al., 2024). Swahili uses all available data (~1.2B tokens); Galician and Urdu subsampled to match this token count for a controlled comparison. Three new target languages: Galician (mid-resource Romance, Latin script), Swahili (low-resource Bantu, Latin, ~100M speakers, unrelated to model languages), Urdu (low-resource Indo-Aryan, Perso-Arabic script). Hindi is NOT a target — it is already one of Llama 3.1's supported languages (Devanagari); Urdu is linguistically related to Hindi but uses a different script. Authors will open-source the datasets on acceptance. | 全部预训练数据来自 FineWeb 2。斯瓦希里语用全部可得数据(约 12 亿 token);加利西亚语与乌尔都语下采样到相同 token 量以做受控对比。三种新增目标语言:加利西亚语(中资源罗曼语,拉丁文)、斯瓦希里语(低资源班图语,拉丁文,约 1 亿使用者,与模型语言无关)、乌尔都语(低资源印度雅利安语,波斯-阿拉伯文)。印地语并非目标——它已是 Llama 3.1 支持的语言之一(天城文);乌尔都语与印地语在语言学上相关但文字不同。作者拟在录用后开源数据集。
- **Method / 方法:** Continual pretraining via LayRA (Layer-selective LoRA): inserts LoRA into the earliest 10 and final 2 layers plus fine-tunes the embedding layer and LM head, freezing the rest (LoRA rank r=8, alpha=16, dropout 0.05). Motivated by two insights — LoRA "learns less and forgets less", and multilingual LMs encode input in source language (initial layers), reason in English (middle), then translate back (final layers). Model arithmetic (a language vector) adds instruction-following without any target-language instruction data: theta_it = gamma*(theta_new - theta_base) + theta_base_it, gamma=0.7; sequential variants LayRA-series (lambda'=0.5) and LayRA-merge (mu=0.5). Base models pre-support N languages; new-language CPT for 1 epoch, LR 3e-4 linear, seq 2048, AdamW, 2xH100. | 通过 LayRA(分层选择性 LoRA)做持续预训练:在最前 10 层与最后 2 层插入 LoRA,并微调嵌入层与 LM head,其余冻结(LoRA 秩 r=8,alpha=16,dropout 0.05)。基于两点洞见——LoRA"学得少也忘得少",以及多语模型在初始层用源语言编码、中间层用英语推理、末层再翻译回源语言。模型算术(语言向量)在没有任何目标语言指令数据的情况下赋予指令跟随能力:theta_it = gamma*(theta_new - theta_base) + theta_base_it,gamma=0.7;顺序变体 LayRA-series(lambda'=0.5)与 LayRA-merge(mu=0.5)。基座预支持 N 种语言;新语言 CPT 训练 1 轮,学习率 3e-4 线性,序列长 2048,AdamW,2 张 H100。
- **Models & Eval / 模型与评测:** Primary base Llama 3.1 8B (supports 8 languages: English, German, French, Italian, Portuguese, Spanish + Hindi/Devanagari + Thai); secondary Qwen 2.5 7B. Benchmarks (LM Eval Harness): base models on XNLI, PAWS-X, XCOPA, XStoryCloze; instruction models on XNLI, MGSM (3-shot), MMLU-Lite; missing-language sets machine-translated (Google, March 2025). Selected averages — XNLI (PT baseline 47.55): Swahili Full 40.95 / LoRA 46.82 / LayRA 46.64; Galician LoRA 46.49 / LayRA 47.14. XCOPA (PT 67.14): Urdu LoRA 65.57 / LayRA 66.26. LayRA gives >1 point average improvement over all other methods on 12 of 19 task groups. | 主基座 Llama 3.1 8B(支持 8 种语言:英、德、法、意、葡、西 + 印地语/天城文 + 泰语);次基座 Qwen 2.5 7B。基准(LM Eval Harness):基座模型用 XNLI、PAWS-X、XCOPA、XStoryCloze;指令模型用 XNLI、MGSM(3-shot)、MMLU-Lite;缺失语言的数据集用机器翻译(Google,2025年3月)。部分均值——XNLI(PT 基线 47.55):斯瓦希里语 全参 40.95 / LoRA 46.82 / LayRA 46.64;加利西亚语 LoRA 46.49 / LayRA 47.14。XCOPA(PT 67.14):乌尔都语 LoRA 65.57 / LayRA 66.26。LayRA 在 19 个任务组中的 12 个上较所有其他方法平均提升 >1 分。
- **Key Findings / 主要发现:** LayRA offers the best tradeoff between retaining previously-supported languages and competitively learning new ones. Model arithmetic adds strong instruction-following with no target-language instruction data. Galician shows the strongest tradeoff (positive transfer from Spanish/Portuguese); Swahili responds well; Urdu is worst on Llama due to Perso-Arabic tokenizer over-fragmentation — but Qwen 2.5 handles Urdu better. LayRA-merge matches or surpasses parallel training for Latin-script retention. | LayRA 在保留原有语言与有竞争力地学习新语言之间取得最佳权衡。模型算术在无目标语言指令数据时也能赋予强指令跟随能力。加利西亚语权衡最好(来自西/葡的正迁移);斯瓦希里语反应良好;乌尔都语在 Llama 上最差(波斯-阿拉伯文分词过度切分)——但 Qwen 2.5 对乌尔都语处理更好。LayRA-merge 在拉丁文字保留上追平或超过并行训练。
- **Limitations / 局限:** Urdu excluded from two-stage experiments due to tokenizer issues. Instruction-tuning results inconsistent for MGSM/MMLU (Galician/Urdu declines). Model-size and data scaling left to future work; no learning-rate schedule exploration. Only three target languages studied. | 因分词问题乌尔都语被排除在两阶段实验之外。指令微调在 MGSM/MMLU 上结果不一致(加利西亚语/乌尔都语下降)。模型规模与数据扩展留待未来工作;未探索学习率调度。仅研究了三种目标语言。

### Tamil-Llama: A New Tamil Language Model Based on Llama 2 (Balachandran, 2023)
- **Venue / Link:** arXiv preprint (cs.CL), arXiv nonexclusive-distrib 1.0 license, https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.05845
- **Motivation / 动机:** Tamil and other Indian languages are severely underrepresented in leading LLMs — in Llama 2's 2T-token pretraining corpus 89.7% is English, other European languages ~10%, and Tamil/Hindi/other under-represented languages combined are <0.21%. Llama 2 has few Tamil characters in its vocabulary, so a single Tamil character becomes 3-4 byte tokens, lengthening sequences and yielding incoherent Tamil output. The goal is efficient, high-quality Tamil generation and comprehension. | 泰米尔语及其他印度语言在主流大模型中严重代表不足——Llama 2 的 2 万亿 token 预训练语料中 89.7% 为英文,其他欧洲语言约 10%,而泰米尔语/印地语等代表不足语言合计 <0.21%。Llama 2 词表中泰米尔字符很少,单个泰米尔字符会变成 3-4 个字节 token,拉长序列并产生不连贯的泰米尔输出。目标是高效、高质量的泰米尔语生成与理解。
- **Corpus & Data / 语料与数据:** Pretraining: 12 GB Tamil text = 600k documents selected from the 4.72M-document CulturaX dataset (Nguyen et al. 2023, which includes OSCAR). Instruction tuning: Google-Translate-API Tamil version of the full Stanford Alpaca (52,000 instructions) + a no-code subset of OpenOrca (~93,000 instructions), plus Wikipedia-sourced Self-Instruct (GPT-4-generated) data — ~145,000 instructions total. Primarily Tamil; Hindi is mentioned only as another under-represented Indian language in Llama 2's corpus (not a training/eval language here). NLU eval uses IndicSentiment (1,000 Tamil samples) and iNLTK/IndicGLUE Tamil headline classification. Models, datasets, code publicly released. | 预训练:12 GB 泰米尔文本 = 从 472 万篇文档的 CulturaX 数据集(含 OSCAR)中选取的 60 万篇文档。指令微调:用 Google 翻译 API 得到的完整 Stanford Alpaca 泰米尔版(52,000 条指令)+ OpenOrca 的无代码子集(约 93,000 条指令),再加来自维基百科的 Self-Instruct(GPT-4 生成)数据——共约 145,000 条指令。以泰米尔语为主;印地语仅作为 Llama 2 语料中另一种代表不足的印度语言被提及(非此处训练/评测语言)。NLU 评测用 IndicSentiment(1,000 条泰米尔样本)与 iNLTK/IndicGLUE 泰米尔语标题分类。模型、数据、代码均公开发布。
- **Method / 方法:** CPT of Llama 2 (7B and 13B) with vocabulary expansion, following the Chinese-LLaMA recipe. Train a SentencePiece Tamil tokenizer, merge to add 16,000 Tamil tokens (vocab 32,000 -> 48,000), resize embeddings/LM-head V x H -> V' x H preserving original rows. Two-stage training, both via LoRA (rank 64, alpha 128, target modules QKVO + MLP, LR 2e-4, seq len 512, FP16): (1) pretraining with causal LM on 12GB (7B) / 4GB (13B) for 1 epoch (adapters on attention, plus training embeddings+LM-head+new-token params; the Chinese-LLaMA exclusive-embedding first stage was dropped to cut cost); (2) instruction fine-tuning on ~145k for 2 epochs (7B) / 1 epoch (13B), dropout 0.1. Tamil tokenizer needs only 20-25% of the tokens the original uses (~3x faster / 3x more content). | 按中文 LLaMA 方案对 Llama 2(7B 与 13B)做带词表扩充的 CPT。训练 SentencePiece 泰米尔分词器,合并加入 16,000 个泰米尔 token(词表 32,000 -> 48,000),将嵌入/LM-head 从 V x H 调整为 V' x H 并保留原有行。两阶段训练,均用 LoRA(秩 64,alpha 128,目标模块 QKVO + MLP,学习率 2e-4,序列长 512,FP16):(1)在 12GB(7B)/4GB(13B)上用因果语言建模预训练 1 轮(注意力上加适配器,并训练嵌入+LM-head+新 token 参数;为省成本略去了中文 LLaMA 的先训嵌入阶段);(2)在约 14.5 万条上做指令微调,7B 训 2 轮、13B 训 1 轮,dropout 0.1。泰米尔分词器仅需原始所用 token 的 20-25%(约快 3 倍/信息量约 3 倍)。
- **Models & Eval / 模型与评测:** Released Tamil-LLaMA base + instruct at 7B and 13B (HuggingFace). Instruction-following graded by GPT-4 on 10-point scale (with manual review) over 120+ examples across QA, reasoning, literature, translation, coding, ethics, etc., normalized to 100. Overall: Tamil-LLaMA-7B 63.83, Tamil-LLaMA-13B 71.17, gpt-3.5-turbo 61.33 — both Tamil models beat gpt-3.5-turbo overall on Tamil (13B ~10% higher; standout Creative Writing 92.50/95.62 vs 59.69). gpt-3.5-turbo still wins Literature (71.00) and Translation (92.78). Pretraining infra: A100 80GB, 48h (7B) / 60h (13B). NLU: Tamil-LLaMA reaches 81.3% on IndicSentiment (vs original LLaMA 50.5%, near-random) and 80.12% on the Tamil headline classification set. Authors note even the 7B surpasses Llama 2 70B on Tamil generation in manual review. | 发布 Tamil-LLaMA 的 base 与 instruct 各 7B、13B(HuggingFace)。指令跟随由 GPT-4 按 10 分制打分(辅以人工复核),覆盖 120+ 例,涉及问答、推理、文学、翻译、编程、伦理等,归一化到 100。总分:Tamil-LLaMA-7B 63.83、Tamil-LLaMA-13B 71.17、gpt-3.5-turbo 61.33——两个泰米尔模型在泰米尔语总分上均胜过 gpt-3.5-turbo(13B 约高 10%;创意写作尤为突出 92.50/95.62 对 59.69)。gpt-3.5-turbo 在文学(71.00)与翻译(92.78)上仍占优。预训练硬件:A100 80GB,7B 48 小时/13B 60 小时。NLU:Tamil-LLaMA 在 IndicSentiment 上达 81.3%(原始 LLaMA 仅 50.5%,近乎随机),泰米尔标题分类达 80.12%。作者指出人工复核中,即便 7B 也在泰米尔生成上超过 Llama 2 70B。
- **Key Findings / 主要发现:** Vocabulary expansion (+16k Tamil tokens) plus LoRA-based CPT dramatically improves Tamil generation and NLU while cutting token count to 20-25% (~3x efficiency). The adapted 7B/13B models beat gpt-3.5-turbo overall and Llama 2 70B on Tamil. Method is presented as a template for other Indian languages (the Indic-Llama family). | 词表扩充(+1.6 万泰米尔 token)加基于 LoRA 的 CPT 显著提升泰米尔生成与 NLU,同时把 token 数降到 20-25%(约 3 倍效率)。适配后的 7B/13B 模型在泰米尔语上总体胜过 gpt-3.5-turbo 与 Llama 2 70B。该方法被作为其他印度语言的模板(Indic-Llama 家族)。
- **Limitations / 局限:** Constrained knowledge base from a relatively small Tamil dataset (cost limits); weak on Tamil culture/literature specifics. No detoxification/alignment, so outputs may be harmful. Weak robustness under adversarial prompts, and poor mathematical reasoning. Over-generation tendencies. Scarce standardized Tamil benchmarks make evaluation hard (and GPT-4 may favor GPT-lineage outputs). Translation loss from English-translated instruction data. | 因泰米尔数据集相对小(受成本限制)知识面受限,对泰米尔文化/文学细节较弱。未做去毒/对齐,输出可能有害。对抗提示下鲁棒性弱,数学推理差。有过度生成倾向。标准化泰米尔基准稀缺使评测困难(且 GPT-4 可能偏向 GPT 系输出)。指令数据由英文翻译而来,存在翻译损失。

### IndicLLMSuite: A Blueprint for Creating Pre-training and Fine-Tuning Datasets for Indian Languages (Sangraha) (Khan et al., 2024)
- **Venue / Link:** ACL 2024 (Outstanding Paper), https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.06350
- **Motivation / 动机:** Despite rapid progress in English LLMs, comparable models for Indian languages are held back by the scarcity of tailored, openly-licensed data and the absence of open-source pipelines to curate text from diverse sources (websites, PDFs, audio/video); the authors aim to provide a reusable "blueprint" of pre-training and fine-tuning resources for 22 scheduled Indian languages. | 尽管英语大模型进展迅速，印度语言的同类模型却因缺乏量身定制且开放许可的数据、以及缺少能从多样来源（网站、PDF、音视频）整理文本的开源流水线而受阻；作者旨在为印度宪法第八附表中的 22 种语言提供一套可复用的预训练与微调资源"蓝图"。
- **Corpus & Data / 语料与数据:** Sangraha pre-training corpus totals 251B tokens across 22 languages, split into Sangraha Verified 64.3B (64,306.1M), Sangraha Synthetic 162.7B (162,707.9M) and Sangraha Unverified 24.3B (24,307.7M) tokens. HINDI (hin) is the single largest language: total 34,544.3M (~34.5B) tokens — Verified 12,617.3M (~12.6B), plus large Synthetic and Unverified (~12,348.3M) portions. Hindi Verified breaks down (Table 2) into Web 9,312.5M, PDFs 2,435.7M, Speech 112.4M tokens. Sources: manually-verified Indic websites (base URLs seeded from mC4 + all Indian government sites), books/PDFs, and YouTube speech (Hindi videos transcribed with a Hindi Conformer ASR model). - **Models & Eval / 模型与评测:** IndicXTREME benchmark: 9 human-supervised NLU tasks (5 sentence classification, 2 structure prediction, 1 QA, 1 sentence retrieval) over 20 languages, 105 evaluation sets (52 new), including IndicSentiment, IndicXNLI, IndicCOPA, IndicXPara, MASSIVE-Intent, Naamapadam-NER, MASSIVE-SlotFill and IndicQA. - **Key Findings / 主要发现:** Scaling clean, family-specific monolingual data (2.3x, largest gain in Hindi) plus a from-scratch Indic-only model yields SOTA Indic NLU because languages no longer compete for capacity; in-family development sets (e.g. Hindi for Indo-European) can substitute for scarce in-language dev sets, and shared-script modeling especially helps low-resource Devanagari languages. | 扩展干净的、面向语系的单语数据（增至 2.3 倍，印地语增幅最大）并从零训练仅印度语的模型，可获得 SOTA 印度语 NLU，因语言不再争夺容量；同语系开发集（如以印地语代表印欧语言）可替代稀缺的同语开发集，且共享文字建模尤其有助于低资源天城文语言。
- **Limitations / 局限:** News-primarily sourcing means public-channel biases propagate and no debiasing is applied; LID tools are unavailable for very low-resource languages (Bodo, Dogri, Khasi, etc.), limiting cleaning; models benefit from in-language train/dev data that is still scarce; hundreds of extremely low-resource languages remain unrepresented. | 以新闻为主的来源使公共渠道偏见得以传播，且未做去偏；语种识别工具对极低资源语言（博多语、多格拉语、卡西语等）不可用，限制了清洗；模型受益于仍稀缺的同语训练/开发数据；数百种极低资源语言仍未被覆盖。

- **Corpus & Data / 语料与数据:** 41.8 million news articles across 14 Indic languages + English (15 total); 34.5 million non-English article–headline pairs. Per-language counts range from 1.5K (Bhojpuri) to 14.4M articles for HINDI — Hindi is by far the largest subset. Source: crawled from DailyHunt, an Indian news aggregator pulling from 1,773 publishers, covering articles published January 2010–August 2022. Cleaning: discard articles <50 words, discard articles dominated by image/video, discard articles needing navigation to publisher site; retain only text (strip HTML/media/links); deduplicate using the headline as identifier, removing ~7.2M "stale" re-aggregated versions. Data and pretrained models released on GitHub (github.com/rahular/varta); paper under CC BY 4.0 (data license not explicitly stated in paper). | 数据集含 41.8 百万篇新闻文章，覆盖 14 种印度语言 + 英语（共 15 种）；非英语"文章-标题"对为 34.5 百万。各语言数量从 1.5 千篇（博杰普尔语）到 1440 万篇（印地语）不等——印地语是迄今为止最大的子集。来源：从印度新闻聚合平台 DailyHunt 爬取，涵盖 1,773 家出版商、2010 年 1 月至 2022 年 8 月发表的文章。清洗：剔除少于 50 词的文章、以图片/视频为主的文章、需跳转出版商网站才能读全文的文章；仅保留正文（去除 HTML、媒体、链接）；以标题为标识去重，移除约 720 万篇"陈旧的"重复聚合版本。数据与预训练模型发布于 GitHub；论文采用 CC BY 4.0（数据许可证论文中未明确说明）。
- **Method / 方法:** The corpus is used three ways: (1) as a headline-generation benchmark, (2) for cross-lingual/transliteration transfer studies (English-only, Hindi-only, Latin, Devanagari, all-language finetuning settings), and (3) as a pretraining corpus. Because its size is comparable to existing Indic pretraining corpora, the authors pretrain Vārta-T5 (encoder-decoder, using span corruption + gap-sentence generation objectives sampled uniformly) and Vārta-BERT (encoder-only, masked-language-modeling). A SMALL subset caps each language at 100K articles for faster ablations. | 该语料以三种方式使用：(1) 作为标题生成基准；(2) 用于跨语言/转写迁移研究（仅英语、仅印地语、拉丁转写、天城文转写、全语言微调等设定）；(3) 作为预训练语料。由于其规模与现有印度语预训练语料相当，作者预训练了 Vārta-T5（编码器-解码器，采用均匀采样的 span corruption 与 gap-sentence generation 目标）和 Vārta-BERT（仅编码器，掩码语言建模）。SMALL 子集将每种语言限制在 10 万篇以加速消融实验。
- **Models & Eval / 模型与评测:** Vārta-T5 and Vārta-BERT pretrained from scratch. NLG: Vārta-T5 evaluated on headline generation (ROUGE-L, language-wise) and IndicNLG; it outperforms competitive baselines such as mT5. NLU: Vārta-BERT evaluated zero-shot on IndicXTREME, outperforming strong baselines (e.g., mBERT, IndicBERT, MuRIL). State-of-the-art abstractive models perform only slightly better than extractive baselines (Lead-1, Extractive Oracle), showing the task is hard. | Vārta-T5 与 Vārta-BERT 均从零预训练。NLG：Vārta-T5 在标题生成（分语言的 ROUGE-L）与 IndicNLG 上评测，超过 mT5 等强基线。NLU：Vārta-BERT 在 IndicXTREME 上做零样本评测，超过 mBERT、IndicBERT、MuRIL 等强基线。最先进的抽象式模型仅略优于抽取式基线（Lead-1、Extractive Oracle），说明任务本身很难。

### Samanantar: The Largest Publicly Available Parallel Corpora Collection for 11 Indic Languages (Ramesh et al., 2022)
- **Venue / Link:** Transactions of the Association for Computational Linguistics (TACL), https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.05596
- **Motivation / 动机:** Neural MT for Indic languages is bottlenecked by scarce parallel data; existing English–Indic corpora are small and fragmented. The goal is to assemble and mine, at scale, the largest publicly available English↔Indic parallel corpus to advance NMT and multilingual NLP. | 印度语言的神经机器翻译受制于稀缺的平行数据；现有英语-印度语平行语料规模小且分散。目标是大规模汇编并挖掘出最大的公开英语↔印度语平行语料，以推动 NMT 与多语言 NLP。
- **Corpus & Data / 语料与数据:** 49.7 million English↔Indic sentence pairs across 11 Indic languages (two language families). Of these, 12.4M compiled from existing public corpora and 37.4M newly mined (~3–4x the existing data). Per-pair totals range from 141K (English–Assamese) to 10.1M pairs for English–HINDI — Hindi is the largest pair. Additionally, 83.4M pairs across all 55 (11 choose 2) Indic–Indic pairs were extracted by pivoting through English (a 5.33x increase in publicly available Indic–Indic pairs). Largest single mining contributor is IndicCorp (~67% of the corpus). Sources: web-crawled monolingual corpora, OCR of scanned documents, and existing parallel datasets (incl. WAT 2021 MultiIndicMT). Released publicly at ai4bharat.iitm.ac.in/samanantar (license terms not explicitly captured from the paper text). | 覆盖 11 种印度语言（两个语系）的 49.7 百万英语↔印度语句对。其中 1240 万来自现有公开语料，3740 万为新挖掘（约为现有数据的 3–4 倍）。各语对总量从 14.1 万（英语-阿萨姆语）到 1010 万（英语-印地语）——印地语是最大的语对。此外，通过以英语为枢轴，抽取出覆盖全部 55（C(11,2)）个印度语-印度语语对的 8340 万句对（使公开的印度语间句对增加 5.33 倍）。最大的单一挖掘来源是 IndicCorp（约占语料 67%）。来源：网络爬取的单语语料、扫描文档 OCR、以及现有平行数据集（含 WAT 2021 MultiIndicMT）。公开发布于 ai4bharat.iitm.ac.in/samanantar（许可证条款未从论文正文中明确提取）。
- **Method / 方法:** Mining pipeline aligns sentences via LaBSE embeddings: candidate English/Indic sentences are embedded, indexed in FAISS (100k clusters, Product Quantization on the 768-d embeddings for storage), retrieved by approximate nearest neighbor, then re-scored with full LaBSE embeddings to compute a LaBSE Alignment Score (LAS); a LAS threshold selects parallel pairs. For non-machine-readable comparable corpora, the same LaBSE mechanism is applied. Indic–Indic pairs derived by pivoting shared English sentences. The corpus is then used to train IndicTrans, a multilingual NMT model. | 挖掘流程用 LaBSE 向量对齐句子：将候选英语/印度语句子编码，存入 FAISS 索引（10 万个聚类，对 768 维向量做乘积量化以节省存储），用近似最近邻检索，再用完整 LaBSE 向量重打分，计算 LaBSE 对齐分数（LAS）；用 LAS 阈值筛选平行句对。对非机读的可比语料采用同样的 LaBSE 机制。印度语-印度语句对通过共享英语句子做枢轴推导。随后用该语料训练多语言 NMT 模型 IndicTrans。
- **Models & Eval / 模型与评测:** IndicTrans (multilingual NMT) trained on Samanantar. Compared against (a) commercial systems (Google, Microsoft), (b) public multilingual models, (c) models trained only on existing sources. On benchmarks including FLORES, IndicTrans outperforms all existing open-source models and even beats commercial systems on several Indic directions. Data quality validated by human evaluation: 38 annotators judged 9,566 sentence pairs across 11 languages; >85% annotator agreement and high semantic-similarity (STS) scores, confirming mined-pair quality and that LAS thresholds regulate quality. | 在 Samanantar 上训练多语言 NMT 模型 IndicTrans。与 (a) 商用系统（Google、Microsoft）、(b) 公开多语言模型、(c) 仅用现有来源训练的模型对比。在包括 FLORES 的基准上，IndicTrans 超过所有现有开源模型，并在若干印度语方向上超越商用系统。数据质量经人工评测验证：38 名标注者对 11 种语言的 9,566 个句对打分；标注者一致率 >85%，语义相似度（STS）分数高，证实挖掘句对的质量，且 LAS 阈值能有效调控质量。
- **Key Findings / 主要发现:** Large-scale LaBSE-based mining (with FAISS + PQ) can build a high-quality parallel corpus ~4x larger than prior public data; English pivoting cheaply yields 83.4M Indic–Indic pairs; models trained on it (IndicTrans) surpass both open-source and commercial baselines, establishing Samanantar's practical utility. | 基于 LaBSE 的大规模挖掘（配合 FAISS + 乘积量化）能构建出比以往公开数据大约 4 倍的高质量平行语料；以英语为枢轴可低成本得到 8340 万印度语间句对；在其上训练的 IndicTrans 超越开源与商用基线，确立了 Samanantar 的实用价值。
- **Limitations / 局限:** English-centric (all pairs mined via English), so Indic–Indic pairs are pivot-derived rather than natively aligned; mining quality depends on LaBSE/LAS thresholds and can miss valid or admit noisy pairs; strong imbalance across languages (Hindi 10.1M vs Assamese 141K); coverage limited to 11 Indic languages; existing sources were not further cleaned/post-processed. | 以英语为中心（所有句对经英语挖掘），印度语间句对是枢轴推导而非原生对齐；挖掘质量依赖 LaBSE/LAS 阈值，可能漏掉有效句对或引入噪声；语言间严重不均衡（印地语 1010 万 vs 阿萨姆语 14.1 万）；仅覆盖 11 种印度语言；现有来源未做进一步清洗/后处理。

- **Models & Eval / 模型与评测:** No models are trained or benchmarked in this paper; it is a dataset-contribution / resource paper. Value is argued via scale, coverage (167 languages), and the rigor of the cleaning/deduplication pipeline rather than downstream LLM evaluation. | 本文未训练或评测任何模型；这是一篇数据集贡献/资源论文。其价值通过规模、覆盖面（167 种语言）以及清洗/去重流程的严谨性来论证，而非通过下游 LLM 评测。

- **Limitations / 局限:** Marked "Ongoing Work"; inherits biases/limitations of mC4 and OSCAR (web-crawl noise, coverage skew toward high-resource languages — Hindi is only ~0.27% of tokens); no downstream LLM validation of quality; license is constrained by upstream mC4/OSCAR terms; access requires accepting contact-sharing conditions. | 标注为"进行中的工作"；继承 mC4 与 OSCAR 的偏差/局限（网络爬取噪声、偏向高资源语言的覆盖倾斜——印地语仅占约 0.27% 的 token）；未对质量做下游 LLM 验证；许可证受上游 mC4/OSCAR 条款约束；访问需接受联系信息共享条件。

### Krutrim LLM: A Novel Tokenization Strategy for Multilingual Indic Languages with Petabyte-Scale Data Processing (Kumar et al., 2024)
- **Venue / Link:** arXiv preprint (arXiv:2407.12481, v2 Apr 2025; CC BY 4.0; Krutrim/Ola team), https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.12481
- **Motivation / 动机:** Indic languages are heavily underrepresented in web corpora — Hindi is the 3rd most spoken language globally yet does not rank in the top 20 languages of processed Common Crawl, and existing multilingual LLMs devote only ~11% of data to non-English (14 languages accounting for ~90% of that), yielding weak Indic coverage; the paper aims to build a high-quality Indic dataset and a tokenizer that is efficient for Indic scripts. | 印度语言在网络语料中严重欠缺代表性——印地语是全球第三大口语，却排不进已处理 Common Crawl 语料的前 20 种语言；现有多语言 LLM 仅将约 11% 数据分给非英语（其中 14 种语言占约 90%），导致印度语言覆盖薄弱。本文旨在构建高质量的印度语言数据集，以及对印度文字高效的分词器。
- **Corpus & Data / 语料与数据:** Petabyte-scale acquisition from open-source and proprietary sources: Common Crawl (billions of web pages, ~2 billion duplicates, ~70% redundancy addressed by deduplication), Indic books, news articles, and Wikipedia; covers 12 languages = English + 11 Indic languages (Hindi, Marathi, Telugu, Tamil, Assamese, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Odia, Punjabi, Bengali). Hindi is a first-class target (character-coverage tuning is illustrated specifically for Hindi's ~50 characters: 36 consonants + 12 vowels). Custom per-language preprocessing and deduplication pipelines. Paper licensed CC BY 4.0; underlying corpus is partly proprietary and not fully released. | 从开源与专有来源进行 PB 级数据采集：Common Crawl（数十亿网页，约 20 亿重复页，约 70% 冗余通过去重处理）、印度语书籍、新闻文章和维基百科；覆盖 12 种语言 = 英语 + 11 种印度语言（印地语、马拉地语、泰卢固语、泰米尔语、阿萨姆语、古吉拉特语、卡纳达语、马拉雅拉姆语、奥里亚语、旁遮普语、孟加拉语）。印地语是核心目标（字符覆盖率调优专门以印地语约 50 个字符为例：36 个辅音 + 12 个元音）。为每种语言定制预处理与去重流程。论文采用 CC BY 4.0 许可；底层语料部分为专有数据，未完全公开。
- **Method / 方法:** SentencePiece tokenizer trained with Byte Pair Encoding (BPE). Ablations over vocabulary size (70k / 85k / 100k), training-corpus size (a 225M-token sample reaches nearly the same token-to-word ratio as a 12B-token sample), and character coverage (0.997 found optimal, avoiding emoji/gibberish). A "dummy" tokenizer plus linguist review removes non-Indic/gibberish characters (mapped to UNK) before training the final cleaned tokenizer. The data + tokenizer are prepared for from-scratch pretraining of 3B and 7B Indic LLMs (the paper focuses on data/tokenizer preparation; full LLM training and downstream benchmarking are not reported in detail). | 采用字节对编码（BPE）训练 SentencePiece 分词器。就词表大小（70k / 85k / 100k）、训练语料规模（2.25 亿 token 采样即可达到与 120 亿 token 采样几乎相同的 token-词比）及字符覆盖率（发现 0.997 最优，可避免表情符号/乱码）进行消融。先用"虚拟"分词器加语言学家审核，将非印度语/乱码字符（映射为 UNK）剔除，再训练最终净化后的分词器。数据与分词器用于从零预训练 3B 和 7B 的印度语言 LLM（本文侧重数据/分词器准备，未详述完整 LLM 训练与下游评测）。
- **Models & Eval / 模型与评测:** Targets 3B and 7B Indic LLMs. Tokenizer evaluated via token-to-word ratio ("fertility") and an "exact score." On the AI4Bharat Sangraha corpus (Table 4), the Indic 100k tokenizer beats GPT-4o on essentially all Indic languages — e.g., Hindi 1.39 vs 1.62, Malayalam 2.71 vs 3.37, Odia 1.80 vs 6.39 — while being slightly worse on English (1.48 vs 1.33); it also outperforms OpenAI Tiktoken across all 11 Indic languages. | 目标为 3B 和 7B 印度语言 LLM。分词器以 token-词比（"fertility"）及"exact score"评测。在 AI4Bharat Sangraha 语料（表 4）上，Indic 100k 分词器在几乎所有印度语言上优于 GPT-4o——例如印地语 1.39 对 1.62、马拉雅拉姆语 2.71 对 3.37、奥里亚语 1.80 对 6.39——在英语上略逊（1.48 对 1.33）；并在全部 11 种印度语言上优于 OpenAI Tiktoken。
- **Key Findings / 主要发现:** Increasing vocabulary size improves the token-to-word ratio far more than increasing corpus size (which saturates once major words are covered); a character coverage of 0.997 best matches Indic scripts; a linguistically cleaned custom Indic tokenizer clearly beats Tiktoken/GPT-4o on Indic fertility, though larger vocabularies trade off inference speed. | 增大词表比增大语料规模对 token-词比的改善大得多（语料在覆盖主要词汇后即饱和）；字符覆盖率 0.997 最契合印度文字；经语言学净化的定制印度分词器在印度语言 fertility 上明显优于 Tiktoken/GPT-4o，但更大词表会牺牲推理速度。
- **Limitations / 局限:** No explicit limitations section; only tokenizer-level metrics (token-to-word ratio) are reported — actual 3B/7B LLM downstream benchmarks are not; results rely partly on proprietary, not-fully-released data; English fertility is slightly worse; larger vocabularies reduce inference speed. | 无明确的局限章节；仅报告分词器层面指标（token-词比），未报告 3B/7B LLM 的实际下游基准；结果部分依赖未完全公开的专有数据；英语 fertility 略差；更大词表降低推理速度。

### BhashaKritika: Building Synthetic Pretraining Data at Scale for Indic Languages (Manoj et al., 2025)
- **Venue / Link:** arXiv preprint (arXiv:2511.10338, v2 Nov 2025; cs.CL/cs.AI; Krutrim, India; CC BY 4.0). VERIFIED — arXiv ID resolves to the stated title. https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.10338
- **Motivation / 动机:** High-quality pretraining data is finite and Indic languages make up less than 1% of Common Crawl (Hindi again absent from the top 20); synthetic data is proposed as a scalable, culturally grounded alternative for low-resource Indic LLM pretraining. | 高质量预训练数据有限，印度语言在 Common Crawl 中占比不足 1%（印地语再次不在前 20）；本文提出合成数据作为可扩展、具文化根基的替代方案，用于低资源印度语言 LLM 预训练。
- **Corpus & Data / 语料与数据:** BhashaKritika — a 540B-token synthetic corpus across 10 Indian languages (Bengali, Gujarati, Hindi, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Oriya, Punjabi, Tamil, Telugu). Grounding sources: English FineWeb documents plus curated "Indic-context" documents (identified by a FastText classifier and clustered for topics); PersonaHub (371M personas) used to synthesize 164.3M English Indic-context personas plus 50K native Indic personas; Indic topics harvested by traversing the Wikipedia "Category:India" knowledge graph. Generation uses a pool of multilingual LLMs (Gemma-3, Krutrim-2, LLaMA-3.3 70B). Hindi is one of the 10 target languages, produced by both direct native generation and English→Hindi translation. License CC BY 4.0; a portion of the data is publicly shared. | BhashaKritika——覆盖 10 种印度语言（孟加拉语、古吉拉特语、印地语、卡纳达语、马拉雅拉姆语、马拉地语、奥里亚语、旁遮普语、泰米尔语、泰卢固语）的 5400 亿 token 合成语料。接地来源：英语 FineWeb 文档，加上定制的"印度语境"文档（由 FastText 分类器识别并聚类为主题）；使用 PersonaHub（3.71 亿 persona）合成 1.643 亿英语印度语境 persona 及 5 万本土印度 persona；通过遍历维基百科"Category:India"知识图谱抓取印度主题。生成使用多语言 LLM 池（Gemma-3、Krutrim-2、LLaMA-3.3 70B）。印地语为 10 种目标语言之一，通过本土直接生成与英译印两种方式产生。许可 CC BY 4.0；部分数据公开共享。
- **Method / 方法:** Five generation approaches: (3.1) Document-Grounded Generation (English and Indic docs), (3.2) Persona-Based Generation (persona-only and persona+document), (3.3) Math & Reasoning data (transforming verified Question–Solution pairs into pretraining-style corpora), (3.4) Topic-Aware Retrieval-Augmented Generation, and (3.5) Translation (generate in English then translate to xx). A modular quality-evaluation pipeline integrates script/language detection, metadata-consistency checks, n-gram repetition analysis, KenLM perplexity filtering (thresholds at the 80th percentile from held-out sets), a custom quality classifier, and WEAT social-bias analysis. The corpus is validated in both from-scratch pretraining and annealing (continued-pretraining) experiments. | 五种生成方法：(3.1) 文档接地生成（英语与印度语文档）、(3.2) persona 生成（仅 persona 及 persona+文档）、(3.3) 数学与推理数据（将已验证的问题-解答对转化为预训练风格语料）、(3.4) 主题感知检索增强生成、(3.5) 翻译（先生成英语再翻译为目标语言）。模块化质量评测流程整合脚本/语言检测、元数据一致性检查、n-gram 重复分析、KenLM 困惑度过滤（阈值取留出集分布的第 80 百分位）、定制质量分类器及 WEAT 社会偏见分析。该语料在从零预训练和退火（继续预训练）两类实验中均得到验证。
- **Models & Eval / 模型与评测:** Validation with a 1B-parameter LLaMA-3.2 architecture. (1) Annealing: a pretrained 1B model annealed on 50B tokens of Web vs BhashaKritika (Table 5) shows synthetic data matches or beats Web data on English + Indic benchmarks (Hellaswag, MMLU, ARC-Easy/Challenge, ARC-Challenge-Indic). (2) Low-resource from-scratch: 1B pretrained from scratch on 15B Indic-Web tokens, then continually pretrained on Web vs BhashaKritika (Table 6) — BhashaKritika matches or slightly exceeds Web (Indic Sentiment 0.499 vs 0.491, MILU 0.236 vs 0.235, Indic XNLI 0.339 vs 0.330) and converges faster. | 以 1B 参数的 LLaMA-3.2 架构验证。(1) 退火：预训练 1B 模型在 50B token 的 Web 数据 vs BhashaKritika 上退火（表 5），合成数据在英语+印度语基准（Hellaswag、MMLU、ARC-Easy/Challenge、ARC-Challenge-Indic）上追平或超过 Web 数据。(2) 低资源从零训练：1B 在 15B 印度语 Web token 上从零预训练，再在 Web vs BhashaKritika 上继续预训练（表 6）——BhashaKritika 追平或略超 Web（Indic Sentiment 0.499 对 0.491、MILU 0.236 对 0.235、Indic XNLI 0.339 对 0.330），且收敛更快。
- **Key Findings / 主要发现:** English instructions paired with Indic source texts yield higher-quality outputs; per-language teacher-model selection matters (LLaMA-3.3 70B outperformed the LLaMA-4 series for Indian languages); Indic personas lower discard rates than random persona+document pairing; the pipeline reduces social bias (WEAT effect sizes/scores drop from source to synthetic); high-quality synthetic data can substitute for real web data in low-resource settings. | 英语指令搭配印度语源文本产出更高质量；分语言的教师模型选择很重要（印度语言上 LLaMA-3.3 70B 优于 LLaMA-4 系列）；印度 persona 比随机 persona+文档配对丢弃率更低；该流程降低社会偏见（WEAT 效应量/分数从源数据到合成数据下降）；在低资源场景下高质量合成数据可替代真实网络数据。
- **Limitations / 局限:** Residual English named entities and English noun references degrade perplexity in generated Indic text; measurable social bias remains in several styles; validation is limited to a single 1B-parameter scale under compute constraints; only part of the corpus is released. | 生成的印度语文本中残留的英语命名实体与英语名词引用会拉低困惑度；若干风格中仍存在可测量的社会偏见；验证仅限单一 1B 参数规模且受算力约束；仅部分语料公开。

### L3Cube-HingCorpus and HingBERT: A Code Mixed Hindi-English Dataset and BERT Language Models (Nayak & Joshi, 2022)
- **Venue / Link:** arXiv preprint (arXiv:2204.08398, Apr 2022; cs.CL/cs.LG; L3Cube-Pune; arXiv non-exclusive license), https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.08398
- **Motivation / 动机:** Code-switching (mixing languages within a sentence) is common on social media, but real code-mixed data is scarce for pretraining transformers — prior models were pretrained on at most ~100k real code-mixed sentences — and real code-mixed data has been shown to outperform synthetically generated data. | 语码转换（句内混用多种语言）在社交媒体上很常见，但真实语码混合数据稀缺，难以用于预训练 Transformer——以往模型至多在约 10 万条真实语码混合句上预训练——且真实语码混合数据已被证明优于合成生成的数据。
- **Corpus & Data / 语料与数据:** L3Cube-HingCorpus — the first large-scale real Hindi-English (Hinglish) corpus in Roman script: 52.93M sentences and 1.04B tokens scraped from Twitter (validation split ~99M tokens); a Devanagari version is produced via an in-house transliteration model. Also released: L3Cube-HingLID, the largest code-mixed Hi-En language-identification dataset (31,756 / 6,420 / 6,279 train/test/validation samples, ~30 tokens per sentence). Hindi-English code-mixing is the sole focus. Data and models released on the L3Cube-Pune GitHub and HuggingFace. | L3Cube-HingCorpus——首个大规模罗马字母书写的真实印地语-英语（Hinglish）语料：从 Twitter 抓取的 5293 万句、10.4 亿 token（验证集约 9900 万 token）；并用内部音译模型生成天城文版本。同时发布 L3Cube-HingLID，最大的印英语码混合语言识别数据集（31,756 / 6,420 / 6,279 训练/测试/验证样本，每句约 30 token）。研究专注于印地语-英语语码混合。数据与模型发布于 L3Cube-Pune 的 GitHub 与 HuggingFace。
- **Method / 方法:** Continued pretraining (CPT), not from-scratch: BERT-base, mBERT, and XLM-RoBERTa are further pretrained on HingCorpus with the masked-language-modeling objective (2 epochs, lr 1e-5, batch 64, loading the respective pretrained weights), in both Roman and mixed (Roman + Devanagari) script versions. HingGPT is a 12-layer GPT-2 trained with the causal-LM objective (lr 5e-5, 2 epochs); HingFT is a fastText skip-gram model (300-dim, 10 epochs). A strong HingBERT-LID model (~98% accuracy) was used to filter/select code-mixed sentences for the corpus. | 采用继续预训练（CPT）而非从零训练：以掩码语言建模目标在 HingCorpus 上继续预训练 BERT-base、mBERT 和 XLM-RoBERTa（2 个 epoch，学习率 1e-5，批大小 64，加载各自的预训练权重），提供罗马字母与混合（罗马+天城文）两种脚本版本。HingGPT 为 12 层 GPT-2，用因果语言建模目标训练（学习率 5e-5，2 个 epoch）；HingFT 为 fastText skip-gram 模型（300 维，10 个 epoch）。使用强 HingBERT-LID 模型（约 98% 准确率）过滤/筛选语料中的语码混合句。
- **Models & Eval / 模型与评测:** Released HingBERT, HingMBERT, HingRoBERTa (+ mixed-script variants), HingGPT, HingFT, and HingBERT-LID. Evaluated on the GLUECoS EN-HI benchmark (F1) for LID, POS-UD, POS-FG, NER, and Sentiment. In Roman script (Table 4), HingRoBERTa is strongest — LID 86.69, POS-UD 90.17, POS-FG 71.69, NER 81.13, Sentiment 66.43 — and all Hing* models outperform vanilla BERT/mBERT/XLM-RoBERTa; HingBERT-LID scores 98.77 F1 on the HingLID test set. | 发布 HingBERT、HingMBERT、HingRoBERTa（含混合脚本变体）、HingGPT、HingFT 及 HingBERT-LID。在 GLUECoS EN-HI 基准（F1）上评测 LID、POS-UD、POS-FG、NER 与情感分析。罗马字母版（表 4）中 HingRoBERTa 最强——LID 86.69、POS-UD 90.17、POS-FG 71.69、NER 81.13、情感 66.43——且所有 Hing* 模型均优于原版 BERT/mBERT/XLM-RoBERTa；HingBERT-LID 在 HingLID 测试集上取得 98.77 F1。
- **Key Findings / 主要发现:** Domain-specific continued pretraining on large-scale real code-mixed data consistently improves all downstream code-mixed tasks over multilingual baselines, with the RoBERTa-based variant (HingRoBERTa) the strongest overall; a high-accuracy LID model is central to curating a clean Hinglish corpus. | 在大规模真实语码混合数据上做领域特定的继续预训练，能在所有下游语码混合任务上持续超越多语言基线，其中基于 RoBERTa 的变体（HingRoBERTa）总体最强；高准确率的 LID 模型对构建干净的 Hinglish 语料至关重要。
- **Limitations / 局限:** Data is Twitter-only (domain/register bias); corpus quality hinges on the ~98%-accurate LID filter; fine-tuning sets are small; in mixed-script evaluation some tasks still trail the GLUECoS-mBERT SOTA; scope is limited to Hindi-English code-mixing. | 数据仅来自 Twitter（领域/语体偏差）；语料质量依赖约 98% 准确率的 LID 过滤；微调集较小；在混合脚本评测中部分任务仍落后于 GLUECoS-mBERT 的 SOTA；范围仅限印地语-英语语码混合。

### MuRIL: Multilingual Representations for Indian Languages (Khanuja et al., 2021)
- **Venue / Link:** arXiv preprint (arXiv:2103.10730, cs.CL), Google; models released on HuggingFace (google/muril-base-cased), https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.10730
- **Motivation / 动机:** India has 1,369 languages/dialects and 22 scheduled languages (~1.17B speakers), yet SOTA multilingual models (mBERT, trained on 100+ languages) underrepresent Indian (IN) languages in vocabulary and data, and fail on transliterated (native→Latin) and code-mixed text common in informal settings. MuRIL is a LM built specifically for IN languages. | 印度有 1,369 种语言/方言、22 种宪定语言（约 11.7 亿使用者），但训练于 100 多种语言的 SOTA 多语模型（如 mBERT）在词表和数据上都严重低估印度语言，且难以处理非正式场合常见的转写（母语→拉丁字母）和语码混合文本。MuRIL 是专为印度语言构建的语言模型。
- **Corpus & Data / 语料与数据:** Trained on IN-language corpora only, ~16B unique tokens, covering 17 languages (16 IN + English), including Hindi (hi). Monolingual data from Common Crawl OSCAR + Wikipedia. Translated (parallel) data from PMINDIA (8 IN langs incl. hi) plus in-house MT of the monolingual corpora into English. Transliterated data from the Dakshina Dataset (12 IN langs incl. hi; 10,000 sentence pairs of native-script↔romanization) plus indic-trans-library transliteration of Wikipedia. Wikipedia corpora upsampled with smoothing exponent α=0.3; per-language/per-corpus (incl. Hindi) token counts are given only in Appendix A (not extracted here). | 仅使用印度语言语料，约 160 亿去重 token，覆盖 17 种语言（16 种印度语言 + 英语），含印地语（hi）。单语数据来自 Common Crawl OSCAR + 维基百科；翻译（平行）数据来自 PMINDIA（8 种印度语言，含 hi）以及将单语语料用内部机器翻译系统译成英语；转写数据来自 Dakshina 数据集（12 种印度语言，含 hi；1 万对母语文字↔罗马化句对）以及用 indic-trans 库对维基百科的转写。维基语料按平滑指数 α=0.3 上采样；各语言/各语料（含印地语）的 token 数仅在附录 A 给出（此处未提取）。
- **Method / 方法:** From-scratch BERT-base encoder trained with two objectives: standard MLM (monolingual, unsupervised) + Translation Language Modeling (TLM, on translated & transliterated document pairs, supervised). Cased WordPiece vocabulary of 197,285 (vs mBERT's small IN coverage). Max seq len 512, global batch 4096, 1M steps (50k warmup, linear decay), AdamW lr 5e-4, 236M parameters. | 从零训练的 BERT-base 编码器，采用两个目标：标准 MLM（单语、无监督）+ 翻译语言建模 TLM（在翻译与转写文档对上，有监督）。区分大小写的 WordPiece 词表 197,285（远高于 mBERT 对印度语言的覆盖）。最大序列长度 512，全局批量 4096，训练 100 万步（5 万步预热、线性衰减），AdamW 学习率 5e-4，236M 参数。
- **Models & Eval / 模型与评测:** Single 236M model, evaluated zero-shot on XTREME (fine-tune on English, test on IN languages), averaged over IN test sets. ### IndicBART: A Pre-trained Model for Indic Natural Language Generation (Dabre et al., 2021/2022)
- **Venue / Link:** Findings of ACL 2022 (arXiv:2109.02903); MIT license; https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.02903
- **Motivation / 动机:** Massively-multilingual S2S models (mBART25/50, mT5) cover few languages, are dominated by high-resource data, give inadequate vocabulary to low-resource languages, and are large/slow. Hindi (hi) is a core language; per-language stats are in appendix Table 7 (not extracted). Models under MIT license. | 在 IndicCorp（Kakwani 等，2020）上预训练，覆盖 11 种印度语言（阿萨姆、孟加拉、古吉拉特、印地、卡纳达、马拉雅拉姆、马拉地、奥里亚、旁遮普、泰米尔、泰卢固）+ 英语；约 4.5 亿句 / 约 90 亿 token，用温度 T=5 采样做规模平衡。通过 IndicNLP 库将所有印度语言数据映射到统一的天城文（9 种文字→1 种），提升共享子词。用 1200 万采样句（每语言 100 万）学得 64K SentencePiece 词表。印地语（hi）为核心语言；各语言统计见附录表 7（此处未提取）。模型采用 MIT 许可。
- **Method / 方法:** From-scratch multilingual seq2seq, conceptually mBART-style (masked-span reconstruction). 6 encoder + 6 decoder layers, hidden 1024, filter 4096, 16 heads, 244M params (vs mBART50 611M, mT5-base 580M). - **Models & Eval / 模型与评测:** Evaluated on two NLG tasks — NMT (WAT 2021 MultiIndicMT + FLORES-101 devtest) and extreme summarization — fine-tuned on low-resource PMI (~326K pairs)/PIB (930K) and high-resource Samanantar (46.2M). IndicBART is competitive with or up to ~2 BLEU/ROUGE better than mBART50 despite being ~2.5× smaller, and performs well zero-shot on languages absent from pre-training or fine-tuning. | 在两个生成任务上评测 —— 机器翻译（WAT 2021 MultiIndicMT + FLORES-101 devtest）与极限摘要 —— 分别在低资源 PMI（约 32.6 万对）/PIB（93 万）与高资源 Samanantar（4620 万）上微调。IndicBART 体量约小 2.5 倍，却与 mBART50 相当，甚至最多高约 2 BLEU/ROUGE，并在未参与预训练或微调的语言上有良好的零样本表现。
- **Key Findings / 主要发现:** For related languages, a compact model matches M3 giants; script sharing (mapping to Devanagari), multilingual training, and efficient use of limited capacity/vocabulary are the drivers. Cross-layer parameter sharing (IndicALBART) yields deployable models competitive when fine-tuned on distilled data. | 对相关语言而言，紧凑模型可比肩大型多语模型；文字共享（统一到天城文）、多语训练、以及对有限容量/词表的高效利用是关键。跨层参数共享（IndicALBART）在用蒸馏数据微调时可得到可部署且有竞争力的模型。
- **Limitations / 局限:** Sentence-level training (unlike mBART50's multi-sentence chunks) may limit long-context; single-script mapping requires reversible transliteration; per-language Hindi token counts only in appendix; evaluated mainly on NMT/summarization. | 句级训练（不同于 mBART50 的多句块）可能限制长上下文；单文字映射要求可逆转写；印地语各语言 token 数仅在附录；评测主要限于机器翻译/摘要。

- **Method / 方法:** Trains 4 monolingual variants — BERT, DistilBERT (66M), RoBERTa, XLM-RoBERTa — for each language, under 3 setups: (A) off-the-shelf pretrained multilingual, (B) fine-tune mBERT with ~300 MB monolingual, (C) train from scratch (5 epochs, no parameter transfer). Two eval settings each: frozen (feature extractor + Linear/Multilinear/LSTM/BiLSTM/Transformer heads) and full fine-tuning (+ linear head). HuggingFace Transformers library. | 为每种语言训练 4 个单语变体 —— BERT、DistilBERT（66M）、RoBERTa、XLM-RoBERTa —— 在 3 种设置下：(A) 现成预训练多语模型，(B) 用约 300 MB 单语微调 mBERT，(C) 从零训练（5 轮，不迁移参数）。每种各两种评测设置：冻结（作特征提取器 + Linear/Multilinear/LSTM/BiLSTM/Transformer 头）与全量微调（+ 线性头）。使用 HuggingFace Transformers 库。
- **Models & Eval / 模型与评测:** Tasks: text classification (Acc), POS (F1), QA (F1). From-scratch (Setup C) BERT/DistilBERT beat their multilingual counterparts across all tasks/languages; SOTA reported for Hindi & Bengali classification (Hindi 81.83 Acc, DistilBERT fine-tuned from scratch). Setup A best Hindi: classification 80.63 (XLM-R frozen), POS 97.98 F1 (XLM-R fine-tuned), QA 59.70 F1 (XLM-R fine-tuned). For QA, pretrained models always beat from-scratch (strong cross-lingual transfer benefit); RoBERTa's byte-level BPE hurt morphologically rich languages. | 任务：文本分类（准确率）、词性标注（F1）、问答（F1）。从零训练（设置 C）的 BERT/DistilBERT 在所有任务/语言上均胜过其多语对应模型；印地语与孟加拉语分类达到 SOTA（印地语 81.83 准确率，从零训练的 DistilBERT 微调）。设置 A 印地语最佳：分类 80.63（XLM-R 冻结）、词性 97.98 F1（XLM-R 微调）、问答 59.70 F1（XLM-R 微调）。问答任务上预训练模型总是优于从零训练（跨语迁移收益显著）；RoBERTa 的字节级 BPE 损害形态丰富的语言。
- **Key Findings / 主要发现:** From-scratch monolingual models beat multilingual ones only marginally (except QA, where cross-lingual transfer strongly favors pretrained multilingual); dataset size is not strictly correlated with performance — task-specific model/method selection matters more; Transformers as frozen feature extractors (with LSTM heads) can be competitive; byte-level BPE is suboptimal for Indic morphology. | 从零训练的单语模型仅略胜多语模型（问答除外，那里跨语迁移强烈利好预训练多语模型）；数据量与性能非严格相关 —— 更重要的是按任务选择模型/方法；Transformer 作冻结特征提取器（配 LSTM 头）也能有竞争力；字节级 BPE 对印度语形态并非最优。
- **Limitations / 局限:** Small task datasets and uniform-epoch training (XLM-R-Hindi likely under-trained); no comparable Hindi QA training baseline; older (2020) encoder-only models, no generation; limited to 3 languages. | 任务数据集小、统一轮次训练（XLM-R-Hindi 可能训练不足）；缺可比的印地语问答训练基线；较早（2020）的仅编码器模型、无生成能力；仅限 3 种语言。

### Improving Multilingual Capabilities with Cultural and Local Knowledge in Large Language Models While Enhancing Native Performance (Kadiyala et al., 2025)
- **Venue / Link:** arXiv preprint (arXiv:2504.09753, cs.CL; v3 Jul 2025), https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.09753
- **Motivation / 动机:** LLM development has focused on English and a few high-resource languages, leaving many underserved; the authors aim to build a small Hindi-English bilingual LLM that boosts Hindi performance while retaining English performance, without resource-intensive vocabulary expansion or architectural changes. | 大语言模型的发展主要集中于英语和少数高资源语言，许多语言被忽视；作者旨在构建一个小型的印地语-英语双语模型，在提升印地语性能的同时保持英语能力，且不采用词表扩展或架构修改等资源密集型方法。
- **Corpus & Data / 语料与数据:** A curated collection of ~3.12M samples (near 50:50 English-Hindi), filtered down to ~485K training samples of which 20% cover localized/cultural knowledge and the rest are generic tasks (math, MCQ, reasoning, summarization, rephrasing, translation). Sources are mostly translated/reformatted datasets that permit research/commercial use: Big-Bench-Hard, XNLI, XL-Sum (translated En→Hi via GPT-4o-mini on Azure), plus Hindi subsets of Global MMLU and IndicXNLI, and cultural/local knowledge sources (Indian legal FAQ, UPSC FAQ, IndianTAX FAQ, IndianMedicines, IndiaCuisines, IndiaTravelGuide) turned into instruction-response pairs; also Aya collection subsets (translation, simplification, summarization). Hindi is central (one of the two target languages). Code, datasets, and models released under MIT and Apache licenses (paper text CC BY-NC-SA 4.0). | 精选语料原始约312万条样本（英印比例接近50:50），过滤后得到约48.5万条训练样本，其中20%为本地化/文化知识，其余为通用任务（数学、选择题、推理、摘要、改写、翻译）。数据源以可用于研究/商用的翻译/重构数据集为主：Big-Bench-Hard、XNLI、XL-Sum（用Azure上的GPT-4o-mini从英译印），以及Global MMLU和IndicXNLI的印地语子集，还有文化/本地知识源（印度法律FAQ、UPSC FAQ、印度税务FAQ、印度药物、印度菜肴、印度旅行指南）转化的指令-回复对；另用Aya数据集的翻译、简化、摘要子集。印地语是核心语言之一。代码、数据集和模型以MIT和Apache许可发布（论文本身为CC BY-NC-SA 4.0）。
- **Method / 方法:** This is instruction tuning / SFT, NOT continued pretraining. They use Supervised Fine-Tuning with LoRA via Unsloth, deliberately avoiding vocabulary expansion, block expansion, or added layers. Over 140 fine-tuning attempts varied the Hindi:English ratio per domain to study bilingual balance; instruction data was reformatted with task-specific appended strings. | 属于指令微调/SFT，而非继续预训练（CPT）。使用Unsloth通过LoRA进行监督微调，刻意避免词表扩展、block扩展或增加层。进行了140多次微调实验，改变各领域的印地语:英语比例以研究双语平衡；指令数据用任务特定的附加字符串重新格式化。
- **Models & Eval / 模型与评测:** Primary fine-tuned models are Qwen-2.5-14B-Instruct and Phi-4 (14B), yielding the Mantra family (12-15B, running in ~30GB RAM). Five additional models were also tuned: Gemma 2 9B/2B, Llama 3.1 8B/3B, Qwen 2.5 3B. Benchmarks: ARC-Challenge, ARC-Easy, MMLU, BoolQ, Context-MCQ (per-language En/Hi), plus MMLU-Pro, BigBench-Hard, MuSR, GPQA, MATH-Hard. Reported ~3% average benchmark improvement across both languages, outperforming models twice its size; Phi-4-based model excels at general tasks while the Qwen-based model adapts better to specific domains. | 主要微调模型为Qwen-2.5-14B-Instruct和Phi-4（14B），构成Mantra系列（12-15B，约30GB内存可运行）。另微调了五个模型：Gemma 2 9B/2B、Llama 3.1 8B/3B、Qwen 2.5 3B。评测基准：ARC-Challenge、ARC-Easy、MMLU、BoolQ、Context-MCQ（分英语/印地语），以及MMLU-Pro、BigBench-Hard、MuSR、GPQA、MATH-Hard。双语平均基准提升约3%，超过体量两倍的模型；基于Phi-4的模型擅长通用任务，基于Qwen的模型对特定领域适应更好。
- **Key Findings / 主要发现:** Enhancing low-resource-language capability does not require large-scale architectural changes — modest, culturally/locally informed fine-tuning bridges performance gaps at low compute cost. The approach uses mostly translated data (except culturally specific knowledge), so it is readily transferable to other low-resource languages. | 提升低资源语言能力无需大规模架构改动——适度的、融入文化/本地知识的微调即可以低算力成本弥合性能差距。方法主要使用翻译数据（文化专有知识除外），因此易于迁移到其他低资源语言。
- **Limitations / 局限:** Models may produce inaccurate/incomplete outputs due to training-data knowledge cutoffs; they are better optimized for the authors' custom prompt formats than the original chat templates; the cross-language transferability of the approach is argued but only tested on Hindi. | 由于训练数据的知识截止，模型可能产生不准确/不完整的输出；相比原始对话模板，它们更适配作者的自定义提示格式；跨语言可迁移性仅为推断，实际只在印地语上验证过。

### Sample-Efficient Language Model for Hinglish Conversational AI (Singh et al., 2025)
- **Venue / Link:** arXiv preprint (arXiv:2504.19070, cs.CL, Apr 2025; USC-authored, project-style 5-page paper), https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.19070
- **Motivation / 动机:** Hinglish (romanized Hindi-English code-mixing) is widely used in India yet has scarce, non-standardized conversational resources; the authors want computationally feasible chatbots that generate natural, context-appropriate code-mixed responses without large models or big datasets. | Hinglish（罗马字母拼写的印英混合语）在印度广泛使用，但对话资源稀缺且不规范；作者希望在不依赖大模型和大数据集的前提下，构建算力可行、能生成自然且贴合语境的混合语回复的聊天机器人。
- **Corpus & Data / 语料与数据:** A culturally grounded Hinglish conversational dataset built entirely from synthetic dialogues (no pre-existing corpora used in training), generated via API prompting with Gemini-2.0-Flash. More than 3,000 multi-turn dialogues (~5 million tokens) across 40+ everyday topics (exam stress, love, roommates, etc.), each turn 40-50 words in alternating user-assistant JSON; selective normalization of high-frequency romanized variants (e.g., "bahut"/"bhot"/"bahout"→"bahut"). Existing datasets (HinGE, LinCE, IIITH, Hinglish-TOP, PHINC) were reviewed to shape understanding but NOT used for training. Split 80/10/10. Paper license CC BY 4.0; dataset license not stated. | 一个融入文化背景的Hinglish对话数据集，完全由合成对话构成（训练中未使用任何现成语料），通过Gemini-2.0-Flash的API提示生成。包含3,000多条多轮对话（约500万token），覆盖40多个日常话题（考试压力、恋爱、室友等），每轮40-50词，以用户-助手交替的JSON格式；对高频罗马化拼写变体做选择性归一化（如"bahut"/"bhot"/"bahout"→"bahut"）。现有数据集（HinGE、LinCE、IIITH、Hinglish-TOP、PHINC）仅用于理解语言模式，未用于训练。按80/10/10划分。论文许可为CC BY 4.0；数据集许可未说明。
- **Method / 方法:** Parameter-efficient fine-tuning (LoRA and QLoRA) of pre-trained cross-lingual models on the synthetic Hinglish corpus — NOT continued pretraining (though the conclusion mentions "additional pre-training on Hinglish text" enhancing code-mixed grasp). Hyperparameters (learning rate, batch size, training duration) were tuned; objectives emphasized multi-turn coherence. | 在合成Hinglish语料上对预训练跨语言模型进行参数高效微调（LoRA和QLoRA）——不属于继续预训练（尽管结论提到"在Hinglish文本上的额外预训练"有助于掌握混合语模式）。调优了超参数（学习率、批大小、训练时长）；训练目标强调多轮连贯性。
- **Models & Eval / 模型与评测:** Screened models include Gemma-4B/Gemma3-1B, Qwen2.5-7B/3B, mT5, GPT-2, DistilBERT; finalized deployment on Qwen2.5-3B and Qwen2.5-7B (plus Gemma3-4B). Avoided BLEU/ROUGE; used Code-Mixing Index (CMI), BERTScore, cosine similarity, and human evaluation (fluency, persona adherence, gender correctness, Hindi usage, coherence). Qwen2.5-3B LoRA gained +41.4% Hinglish fluency and +42.4% coherence; Qwen2.5-7B LoRA +21.9% fluency. Fine-tuned model: avg 11.3 words/response, CMI 0.692, BERT-F1 0.828; A/B preference up to 87.8% (vs 12-39% for base). GPT-4o-judged (100 prompts): LLaMA-70B 4.6, Qwen2.5-7B-FT 4.1, Qwen2.5-3B-FT 3.86. | 筛选的模型包括Gemma-4B/Gemma3-1B、Qwen2.5-7B/3B、mT5、GPT-2、DistilBERT；最终部署Qwen2.5-3B和Qwen2.5-7B（外加Gemma3-4B）。避免使用BLEU/ROUGE；采用混合度指数（CMI）、BERTScore、余弦相似度及人工评测（流利度、人设一致性、性别正确性、印地语使用、连贯性）。Qwen2.5-3B LoRA的Hinglish流利度提升41.4%、连贯性提升42.4%；Qwen2.5-7B LoRA流利度提升21.9%。微调模型：平均每条回复11.3词，CMI为0.692，BERT-F1为0.828；A/B测试偏好率高达87.8%（基线为12-39%）。以GPT-4o为评判（100条提示）：LLaMA-70B得4.6，Qwen2.5-7B微调得4.1，Qwen2.5-3B微调得3.86。
- **Key Findings / 主要发现:** High-quality Hinglish chatbots can be built without massive models or datasets; data quality outweighs quantity (curated, normalized synthetic data beats larger noisy sets), and a fine-tuned 7B model approaches a 70B model's quality at far lower compute. | 无需庞大模型或数据集即可构建高质量Hinglish聊天机器人；数据质量优于数量（精选、归一化的合成数据胜过更大但更嘈杂的数据集），微调后的7B模型在远低的算力下逼近70B模型的质量。
- **Limitations / 局限:** Existing metrics lack nuance for code-mixed text; the model falters on highly colloquial/regional Hinglish; models below 3B parameters still underperform even with quality data. Short paper with limited experimental breadth; evaluation is heavily human- and small-sample-based (e.g., 10 A/B prompts, 100 judge prompts). | 现有指标对混合语文本缺乏细腻度；模型在高度口语化/地方性Hinglish上表现欠佳；即使有优质数据，参数低于3B的模型仍表现不足。论文篇幅短、实验广度有限；评测高度依赖人工且样本量小（如10条A/B提示、100条评判提示）。

### Multilingual Tokenization through the Lens of Indian Languages: Challenges and Insights (Brahma et al., 2025)
- **Venue / Link:** Accepted at ACL 2026 Findings (Long paper); arXiv:2506.17789, cs.CL, CC BY-SA 4.0, https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.17789
- **Motivation / 动机:** Existing tokenizers are skewed toward high-resource (mostly English) languages and are reused for morphologically rich, script-diverse Indic languages, causing longer sequences and fragmented units; the paper studies how tokenizer design choices affect Indic languages, especially in multilingual shared-vocabulary settings. | 现有分词器偏向高资源（主要是英语）语言，却被直接沿用于形态丰富、书写系统多样的印度语言，导致序列变长、语义单元被割裂；本文研究分词器设计选择如何影响印度语言，尤其在多语言共享词表的场景下。
- **Corpus & Data / 语料与数据:** 17 Indic languages across 11 scripts and two language families — Hindi (hin), Assamese, Bengali, Konkani, Gujarati, Kannada, Maithili, Malayalam, Marathi, Nepali, Oriya, Punjabi, Sanskrit, Sindhi, Tamil, Urdu, Telugu. Data from the Sangraha corpus (verified subset, 10% sampled, keeping languages with >10k rows; sentences with >10 Roman-script words excluded to avoid code-mixing); balanced temperature sampling (α=0.3), IndicNLP-library normalization. Intrinsic eval on Flores-200 dev (997 sentences); morphological alignment on MorphScore (Gujarati, Tamil) and Brahma et al. 2025 data (Hindi, Marathi). Hindi is one of 17 languages, not a special focus (though used for MorphScore analysis). | 涵盖11种文字、两个语系的17种印度语言——印地语(hin)、阿萨姆语、孟加拉语、贡根语、古吉拉特语、卡纳达语、迈蒂利语、马拉雅拉姆语、马拉地语、尼泊尔语、奥里亚语、旁遮普语、梵语、信德语、泰米尔语、乌尔都语、泰卢固语。数据取自Sangraha语料（已核验子集，抽样10%，保留行数>1万的语言；排除含>10个罗马字母词的句子以避免混合语）；采用平衡温度采样(α=0.3)、IndicNLP库归一化。内在评测用Flores-200开发集（997句）；形态对齐用MorphScore（古吉拉特语、泰米尔语）和Brahma等2025数据（印地语、马拉地语）。印地语是17种语言之一，并非特别聚焦（但用于MorphScore分析）。
- **Method / 方法:** This is an empirical tokenizer analysis study — NOT continued pretraining or instruction tuning. It systematically evaluates (i) subword algorithms BPE vs Unigram LM (ULM), (ii) script/orthography-aware normalization, (iii) vocabulary size (32K/64K/128K/256K), and (iv) multilingual vocabulary construction (joint vs cluster-based, incl. language-family clustering). For extrinsic eval, BERT-base encoders (12 layers, hidden 768, 12 heads) were pre-trained from scratch on Sangraha with joint vs cluster tokenizers, then fine-tuned on IndicXNLI (NLI) and Naampadam (NER); trained ~48h on 8x A6000. | 属于分词器的实证分析研究——不是继续预训练或指令微调。系统评估了(i)子词算法BPE与Unigram LM(ULM)，(ii)文字/正字法感知的归一化，(iii)词表大小(32K/64K/128K/256K)，(iv)多语言词表构建策略（联合式vs聚类式，含按语系聚类）。外在评测中，用联合与聚类分词器在Sangraha上从零预训练BERT-base编码器（12层、隐藏维768、12头），再在IndicXNLI(NLI)和Naampadam(NER)上微调；在8块A6000上训练约48小时。
- **Models & Eval / 模型与评测:** Tokenizers: BPE and ULM at multiple vocab sizes. Intrinsic metrics: Fertility, Characters-per-Token (CPT), MorphScore. Extrinsic: from-scratch BERT-base on NLI (IndicXNLI) and NER (Naampadam), zero-shot and fine-tuned F1 across 11 Indic languages. Key numbers: at 128K vocab, normalization lowers BPE fertility 1.717→1.664 and ULM 1.695→1.663; fertility reductions statistically significant across 17 languages (Wilcoxon p<0.05); ~4.36% of Flores-200 words (up to ~6% of unique words) undergo normalization. Extrinsic: joint vocab wins zero-shot (+3.70 F1 on IndicXNLI, +0.16 on Naampadam avg), but language-family cluster vocab wins after fine-tuning (+1.23 F1 NLI, +1.08 NER). | 分词器：BPE与ULM，多种词表规模。内在指标：Fertility（碎片度）、每token字符数(CPT)、MorphScore。外在评测：从零训练的BERT-base在NLI(IndicXNLI)和NER(Naampadam)上，覆盖11种印度语言的零样本与微调F1。关键数据：128K词表下，归一化使BPE碎片度从1.717降至1.664、ULM从1.695降至1.663；碎片度下降在17种语言上具统计显著性(Wilcoxon p<0.05)；Flores-200中约4.36%的词（唯一词最多约6%）经历归一化。外在结果：联合词表在零样本上更优（IndicXNLI平均+3.70 F1、Naampadam +0.16），但微调后按语系聚类的词表更优（NLI +1.23 F1、NER +1.08）。
- **Key Findings / 主要发现:** (i) Script-specific/orthographic normalization consistently and significantly improves tokenization quality (lower fertility); (ii) Unigram LM preserves morphological boundaries better than BPE; (iii) cluster-based (esp. language-family) vocabulary construction improves fine-tuned downstream performance over joint vocabularies, though joint wins zero-shot — a trade-off; overall, linguistically informed design choices matter for multilingual tokenizers. | (i)针对文字/正字法的归一化能持续且显著提升分词质量（降低碎片度）；(ii)Unigram LM比BPE更好地保留形态边界；(iii)聚类式（尤其按语系）词表构建在微调下游任务上优于联合词表，但联合词表在零样本上更优——存在权衡；总体而言，融入语言学知识的设计选择对多语言分词器至关重要。
- **Limitations / 局限:** Limited to Indic (India-spoken) language families; broader families should be tested. Only two downstream tasks (NLI, NER); cross-lingual transfer effects unexplored. Morphological-alignment analysis restricted to four languages due to lack of Indic morphology datasets.
