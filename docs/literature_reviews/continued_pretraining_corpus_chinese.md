> **Scope / 范围:** This review covers research that (a) performs **continued / continual pretraining (CPT)** to adapt an existing (English or multilingual) LLM to Chinese, and (b) **introduces a pretraining corpus / dataset** for Chinese, including from-scratch Chinese/bilingual foundation models whose technical reports describe their pretraining corpus. Domain-specific efforts (medical, finance, legal, classical Chinese) are included.
> 本综述涵盖两类研究：(a) 通过**持续/继续预训练（CPT）**将既有（英文或多语）大模型适配到中文；(b) 为中文**引入预训练语料/数据集**，包括其技术报告中描述了预训练语料的从零训练中文/双语基座模型。同时收录了领域专用（医疗、金融、法律、古文）工作。

> **Method / 方法说明:** Candidate papers were discovered via comprehensive web search; the full text of each paper was then read by a dedicated sub-agent, which extracted real figures (token counts, corpus sizes, benchmark scores) and produced the bilingual summary below following a fixed template. Papers that could not be accessed in this environment are flagged explicitly (see *Access Limitations*).
> 候选论文通过全面的网络检索获得；随后由专门的子代理阅读每篇论文全文，抽取真实数据（token 数、语料规模、基准分数），并按固定模板生成下方的双语摘要。在本环境中无法访问的论文已明确标注（见*访问限制*）。

## 1. Continued Pretraining — General Chinese Language Adaptation / 一、通用中文持续预训练

### Efficient and Effective Text Encoding for Chinese LLaMA and Alpaca (Cui et al., 2023)
- **Venue / Link:** arXiv preprint (Technical Report), arXiv:2304.08177 (v1 Apr 2023; v3 Feb 2024), https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.08177
- **Motivation / 动机:** Open LLMs like LLaMA are trained mostly on English and encode Chinese poorly (their vocabulary covers fewer than a thousand Chinese characters, so each character splits into 3-4 byte tokens), limiting Chinese usefulness and efficiency. | 像LLaMA这类开源大模型主要在英文语料上训练，对中文编码能力很差（词表覆盖的汉字不足一千，每个汉字被拆成3-4个字节token），限制了中文的实用性与效率。
- **Corpus & Data / 语料与数据:** Basic models pre-trained on a 20GB general Chinese corpus (same as Chinese BERT-wwm/MacBERT); "Plus" models expand to 120GB by adding CommonCrawl and encyclopedia data. Instruction tuning uses ~2M-3M samples for basic and ~4M-4.3M for Plus (translation 550K, pCLUE 250K, Stanford Alpaca 50K+50K, crawled self-instruct data, plus STEM data; OASST1 ~20K for 33B). Code/models released on GitHub. | 基础模型在20GB通用中文语料上预训练（与中文BERT-wwm/MacBERT相同）；"Plus"版加入CommonCrawl和百科数据扩展到120GB。指令微调基础版约2M-3M条、Plus版约4M-4.3M条（翻译550K、pCLUE 250K、Stanford Alpaca 50K+50K、爬取的self-instruct数据及STEM数据；33B另加约20K OASST1）。代码与模型在GitHub开源。
- **Method / 方法:** CPT on LLaMA/Llama-2 (7B/13B/33B for LLaMA; 7B/13B for Llama-2) with vocabulary expansion: trained a 20K SentencePiece Chinese tokenizer and merged it into LLaMA's, yielding a 49,953-token vocab (Alpaca 49,954 with a pad token). Used LoRA (on attention QKVO + MLP) for both CPT and SFT; embeddings and LM head set trainable; 7B used two-stage training. Peak LR 2e-4 (CPT) / 1e-4 (SFT), block size 512, trained on A40 GPUs. | 在LLaMA/Llama-2上做CPT（LLaMA 7B/13B/33B，Llama-2 7B/13B）并扩展词表：用SentencePiece训练2万规模中文分词器并合并进LLaMA词表，得到49,953词表（Alpaca加pad token为49,954）。CPT与SFT均用LoRA（作用于注意力QKVO+MLP），embedding与LM head设为可训练；7B采用两阶段训练。峰值学习率2e-4（CPT）/1e-4（SFT），block大小512，在A40上训练。
- **Models & Eval / 模型与评测:** Chinese-LLaMA/Alpaca (7B/13B/33B) and Plus variants. On the C-Eval leaderboard (June 2023) Chinese-Alpaca-33B scored 41.6 average and Chinese-Alpaca-Plus-13B 41.5, competitive among open-source models and close to Bloomz-mt-176B (44.3) / GLM-130B (44.0) despite far smaller size. New tokenizer roughly halves encoded token count (about 2x speed / context). GPT-4-rated 200-sample generation eval: Alpaca-33B total 82.0 vs Plus-13B 79.4 vs Plus-7B 75.3. | Chinese-LLaMA/Alpaca（7B/13B/33B）及Plus版。在C-Eval榜单（2023年6月）上Chinese-Alpaca-33B平均41.6、Chinese-Alpaca-Plus-13B 41.5，在开源模型中有竞争力，且以远小的规模逼近Bloomz-mt-176B（44.3）/GLM-130B（44.0）。新分词器把编码token数量约减半（约2倍速度/上下文）。GPT-4评分的200样本生成评测：Alpaca-33B总分82.0，Plus-13B 79.4，Plus-7B 75.3。
- **Key Findings / 主要发现:** Vocabulary expansion greatly improves Chinese encoding efficiency. Instruction-following (Alpaca) models clearly beat pure LM (LLaMA) on NLU-like C-Eval; ingesting more data can matter more than model size for dialogue/generation, though larger 33B is better at numerical reasoning and coding. Decoding strategy/prompt template strongly affect scores (Alpaca-13B +5.8 on re-evaluation). | 扩展词表显著提升中文编码效率。指令模型（Alpaca）在C-Eval这类NLU任务上明显优于纯语言模型（LLaMA）；对对话/生成而言"喂更多数据"可能比扩大参数更重要，但33B在数值推理和代码上更强。解码策略/提示模板对分数影响很大（Alpaca-13B重评+5.8）。
- **Limitations / 局限:** LoRA-only training (not full fine-tuning) may cap capacity; base LLaMA transfers little Chinese linguistic knowledge. C-Eval-based NLU conclusions may not generalize to other datasets. Pre-training data increase (20G→120G) gave limited C-Eval gains for the pure LM. Plus-33B not available at writing. | 仅用LoRA（非全参微调）可能限制上限；基座LLaMA几乎不迁移中文语言知识。基于C-Eval的NLU结论未必推广到其他数据集。纯语言模型从20G到120G预训练数据的C-Eval提升有限。撰写时尚无Plus-33B。

### Rethinking LLM Language Adaptation: A Case Study on Chinese Mixtral (Cui & Yao, 2024)
- **Venue / Link:** arXiv preprint (Pre-print), arXiv:2403.01851 (Mar 2024), https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.01851
- **Motivation / 动机:** Mixtral, a sparse mixture-of-experts (SMoE) model, performs strongly but mainly in English; the authors adapt it to Chinese and use the case to rethink common language-adaptation practices (vocabulary extension, choice of initialization model). | Mixtral是表现优异的稀疏专家混合（SMoE）模型，但主要面向英文；作者将其适配到中文，并借此重新审视常见的语言适配做法（词表扩展、初始化模型选择）。
- **Corpus & Data / 语料与数据:** Further pre-trained on a 20GB general Chinese corpus (≈7B tokens, identical to their Chinese-LLaMA base series), then instruction fine-tuned on 5M Chinese instruction samples (identical to Chinese-Alpaca-2). Encoding-efficiency analysis run on Wikipedia-zh (≈1.7GB). Resources on GitHub. | 在20GB通用中文语料（约7B token，与其Chinese-LLaMA基础系列相同）上继续预训练，再用5M条中文指令数据做指令微调（与Chinese-Alpaca-2相同）。编码效率分析在中文维基（约1.7GB）上进行。资源在GitHub开源。
- **Method / 方法:** CPT on Mixtral-8x7B-v0.1 (SMoE, 8 experts, top-2, 32K native context) WITHOUT vocabulary extension (uses original 32K Mixtral tokenizer). Trained with QLoRA (LoRA rank 64, alpha 128, applied to QKVO+W123+Gate) while fully training embedding and LM head; peak LR 1e-4 cosine, aux load-balancing loss α=0.02, training context 1024, on 48 A40 GPUs (CPT 1 epoch, SFT 3 epochs). | 在Mixtral-8x7B-v0.1（SMoE，8专家、top-2、原生32K上下文）上做CPT，且不扩展词表（沿用原始32K Mixtral分词器）。用QLoRA训练（LoRA rank 64、alpha 128，作用于QKVO+W123+Gate），同时全量训练embedding与LM head；峰值学习率1e-4余弦，辅助负载均衡损失α=0.02，训练上下文1024，48块A40（CPT 1轮，SFT 3轮）。
- **Models & Eval / 模型与评测:** Chinese-Mixtral and Chinese-Mixtral-Instruct. C-Eval valid 5-shot: Chinese-Mixtral-Instruct 55.0 (test 51.5), surpassing Mixtral-8x7B-Instruct-v0.1 (54.0/50.7). CMMLU zero-shot rose from 42.5 (Chinese-Mixtral) to 50.0 after SFT. Open LLM Leaderboard average: Chinese-Mixtral 58.69 (near Mixtral 68.47 except a GSM8K collapse to 0.00), Chinese-Mixtral-Instruct 70.19 (MMLU 71.53, GSM8K recovered to 55.65). Best win rate (57.36%) and Elo (1571) in a Chinese chatbot arena. | Chinese-Mixtral与Chinese-Mixtral-Instruct。C-Eval验证集5-shot：Chinese-Mixtral-Instruct 55.0（测试51.5），超过Mixtral-8x7B-Instruct-v0.1（54.0/50.7）。CMMLU零样本从Chinese-Mixtral的42.5经SFT升至50.0。Open LLM榜均分：Chinese-Mixtral 58.69（除GSM8K崩至0.00外接近Mixtral的68.47），Chinese-Mixtral-Instruct 70.19（MMLU 71.53，GSM8K回升至55.65）。在中文聊天竞技场取得最高胜率（57.36%）和Elo（1571）。
- **Key Findings / 主要发现:** Vocabulary extension improves encoding efficiency (Chinese tokens in vocab 4.6%→44.6%; encoded tokens −32.6%) but HURTS downstream accuracy (C-Eval −5.3, CMMLU −4.5, MMLU −1.3), so it is not a necessity. It is better to start adaptation from the foundation model than the instruction model. CPT on a new language can temporarily damage instruction-dependent tasks (GSM8K), fixable via SFT. Mixtral generalizes to very long context (optimal PPL at 48K, decent up to 128K). Lower-layer experts are most important. | 词表扩展能提升编码效率（词表中文token占比4.6%→44.6%，编码token数−32.6%），但会降低下游准确率（C-Eval −5.3、CMMLU −4.5、MMLU −1.3），并非必需。语言适配从基座模型出发优于从指令模型出发。对新语言做CPT会暂时损害依赖指令跟随的任务（GSM8K），可通过SFT修复。Mixtral对超长上下文泛化良好（48K处PPL最优，128K仍可用）。低层专家最重要。
- **Limitations / 局限:** Chinese-Mixtral-Instruct still trails the DPO-trained Mixtral-8x7B-Instruct-v0.1 on LongBench; DPO not yet applied. Only QLoRA (not full fine-tuning) used. Conclusions drawn on one model family / limited benchmarks; GSM8K instruction-format sensitivity confounds evaluation. | Chinese-Mixtral-Instruct在LongBench上仍落后于经DPO训练的Mixtral-8x7B-Instruct-v0.1；尚未应用DPO。仅用QLoRA（非全参微调）。结论基于单一模型家族/有限基准；GSM8K对指令格式敏感干扰了评测。

### Aurora: Activating Chinese Chat Capability for Mixtral-8x7B Sparse Mixture-of-Experts through Instruction-Tuning (Wang et al., 2023)
- **Venue / Link:** arXiv preprint, arXiv:2312.14557 (v1 Dec 2023; v2 Jan 2024), https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.14557
- **Motivation / 动机:** Mixtral-8x7B has strong MoE performance but weak native Chinese chat (often replies in mixed/English or garbled text); the authors aim to activate Chinese conversational ability via instruction tuning and pioneer instruction tuning on a sparse MoE model. | Mixtral-8x7B的MoE性能强但原生中文对话弱（常混杂英文或乱码），作者旨在通过指令微调激活其中文对话能力，并首创在稀疏MoE模型上做指令微调。
- **Corpus & Data / 语料与数据:** Integrates three open Chinese instruction datasets: alpaca_data_zh_51k (~51K), alpaca_gpt4_data_zh (52K GPT-4-generated), and sharegpt_70k (70K multi-turn, ~35K multi-turn dialogues). After cleaning/filtering, ~176,678 instruction pairs across ~20 categories (top: computer, natural, science). Code, data, model on GitHub. | 整合三份开源中文指令数据：alpaca_data_zh_51k（约51K）、alpaca_gpt4_data_zh（52K，GPT-4生成）、sharegpt_70k（70K多轮，约35K多轮对话）。清洗过滤后约176,678条指令对，覆盖约20个类别（最多为计算机、自然、科学）。代码、数据、模型在GitHub开源。
- **Method / 方法:** Instruction fine-tuning only (no continued pretraining) on Mixtral-8x7B-Instruct-v0.1, using QLoRA with 4-bit quantized feed-forward/attention projections and a 4-bit optimizer; 3 epochs, learning rate 5e-5, trained on a single NVIDIA H100 (80GiB). Training GPU memory ~45GiB, inference ~25GiB. | 仅做指令微调（无继续预训练），基座为Mixtral-8x7B-Instruct-v0.1，采用QLoRA并对前馈/注意力投影用4-bit量化和4-bit优化器；训练3轮，学习率5e-5，单张NVIDIA H100（80GiB）。训练显存约45GiB，推理约25GiB。
- **Models & Eval / 模型与评测:** Aurora, evaluated 5-shot on C-Eval, MMLU, CMMLU: 51.9 / 67.74 / 49.69 respectively. - **Limitations / 局限:** Slight MMLU regression shows some residual forgetting; only an 8B backbone (larger sizes untested); synthetic data generated by a 7B model may carry quality/accuracy noise; surrogate findings from 1.1B TinyLlama may not perfectly transfer; no instruction/alignment stage reported (base-model evaluation). | MMLU小幅回退说明仍有残余遗忘；仅8B基座（未测更大规模）；合成数据由7B模型生成可能含质量/准确性噪声；从1.1B TinyLlama得到的代理结论未必完全迁移；未报告指令/对齐阶段（评测为基座模型）。

- **Motivation / 动机:** CPT for a new language is costly, and no systematic study links the optimal corpus mixture ratio to actual downstream performance nor bridges experimental scaling laws to full-size deployment; prior scaling-law work optimizes pretraining loss (which need not equal task performance) and uses small models (≤4B). | 为新语言做CPT成本高，且缺乏将最优语料配比与实际下游表现联系起来、以及把实验尺度定律衔接到全尺寸部署的系统研究；已有尺度定律工作以预训练损失为优化目标（未必等同任务表现）且用小模型（≤4B）。

Efficient frontiers: ALMR = 116.67·log(LR) + 1085.00 (metric) and ALMR = −0.33·log(LR) + 29.67 (loss), intersecting at optimal ALMR = 33%, LR = 1.0e-9 for 8B; for 70B LR reduced to 1.0e-10 with ALMR adjusted. 8B on 32 A100s, 70B on 128 GPUs, seq len 2048. | 在Llama-3 8B与70B上做全参CPT（Megatron）+SFT+DPO（LlamaFactory），不扩展词表。提出附加语言混合比例（ALMR=额外语言语料占比），在8B上用约100B token的实验研究其与学习率（LR）的联合有效前沿，同时用验证损失和平均基准表现。有效前沿：ALMR=116.67·log(LR)+1085.00（指标）与ALMR=−0.33·log(LR)+29.67（损失），交点为8B的最优ALMR=33%、LR=1.0e-9；70B将LR降至1.0e-10并相应调整ALMR。8B用32块A100，70B用128块GPU，序列长2048。
- **Models & Eval / 模型与评测:** CPT/C-SFT/C-DPO at 8B and 70B. 70B CPT vs Llama-3 70B: C-Eval 67.75→68.36, LCSTS 7.19→8.97, MMLU 79.47→79.53, GSM8K 79.38→81.40, HumanEval 28.05→31.23 (only BBH slightly down). 8B CPT beats Llama-3-Chinese on Chinese+math+code while keeping English (Llama-3-Chinese collapsed on MMLU/GSM8K/HumanEval). C-DPO/C-SFT beat official Llama-3-Instruct across Chinese NLU, translation, alignment. C-SFT-Empathy 70B tops Qwen2-72B-Instruct/Llama3-70B-Instruct on emotional benchmarks and wins 79.4% vs GPT-4 / 97.5% vs Llama3-70B-Instruct in human pairwise chat eval. | 8B与70B的CPT/C-SFT/C-DPO。70B CPT对比Llama-3 70B：C-Eval 67.75→68.36，LCSTS 7.19→8.97，MMLU 79.47→79.53，GSM8K 79.38→81.40，HumanEval 28.05→31.23（仅BBH略降）。8B CPT在中文+数学+代码上优于Llama-3-Chinese并保留英文（后者在MMLU/GSM8K/HumanEval上崩溃）。C-DPO/C-SFT在中文NLU、翻译、对齐上全面超过官方Llama-3-Instruct。C-SFT-Empathy 70B在情感基准上超过Qwen2-72B-Instruct/Llama3-70B-Instruct，人类成对评测中对GPT-4胜率79.4%、对Llama3-70B-Instruct胜率97.5%。
- **Key Findings / 主要发现:** Optimal ALMR should be chosen from averaged benchmark performance, not minimal pretraining loss (small loss ≠ good task performance). CPT for an extra language surprisingly also boosts math, coding, and emotional intelligence. Both CPT and Chinese-inclusive fine-tuning are necessary (ablation). The full CPT+SFT+DPO pipeline beats CPT-only or SFT-only on the same data; final 70B deployed in a real empathetic chat system. | 最优ALMR应由平均基准表现而非最小预训练损失来选（小损失≠好任务表现）。为额外语言做CPT意外地也提升数学、代码和情感智能。CPT与含中文的微调二者缺一不可（消融验证）。完整的CPT+SFT+DPO流程在相同数据上优于仅CPT或仅SFT；最终70B已部署于真实共情聊天系统。
- **Limitations / 局限:** Efficient-frontier study conducted only at 8B and extrapolated to 70B (70B LR/ALMR set heuristically); some proprietary data not disclosed; C-DPO can trade off some domains for preferred ones ("alignment tax"); deployment/EI evaluation partly relies on human annotation and a proprietary chat test set. | 有效前沿仅在8B上研究并外推到70B（70B的LR/ALMR为启发式设置）；部分自有数据未公开；C-DPO可能以牺牲某些领域换取偏好领域（"对齐税"）；部署与情感智能评测部分依赖人工标注及自有聊天测试集。

## 2. Continued Pretraining & Domain Adaptation (Traditional/Multilingual, Medical, Finance, Legal, Classical) / 二、持续预训练与领域适配（繁体/多语、医疗、金融、法律、古文）

### Efficient Training of Robust Traditional Chinese LLaMA-1B on a Single Consumer GPU: Continual Pre-training, SFT, and DPO (Chih et al., 2025)
- **Venue / Link:** arXiv preprint (arXiv:2510.01616), technical report, CC BY 4.0; https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.01616
- **Motivation / 动机:** Small Language Models (SLMs) enable cheap on-device Traditional-Chinese (TC) applications, but their deployment suffers from token-level instability: models unpredictably emit non-TC characters or code-switch into Simplified Chinese, Japanese, or English. The authors target this deployment-critical failure mode and aim to enforce TC adherence without expensive full-model retraining. | 小语言模型（SLM）可支持低成本的端侧繁体中文（TC）应用，但部署时存在 token 级不稳定问题：模型会无法预测地输出非繁中字符，或切换到简体中文、日语或英语。作者针对这一部署关键的失败模式，目标是在不进行昂贵的全模型重训练的情况下强制模型保持繁体中文一致性。
- **Corpus & Data / 语料与数据:** CPT corpus = two public TC datasets: (1) Taiwan-Text-Excellence-2B (~1.78M documents, ~2 billion tokens of high-quality news/articles) and (2) republic_of_china_judgements_4_continue_pretrain (~300 MB of Taiwanese legal judgments). SFT = 1,500 synthetic examples generated by GPT-4-mini across five instruction tasks (300 each). DPO = pairwise preference triplets (preferred = GPT-4-mini pure-TC responses; rejected = the team's own CPT+SFT model outputs exhibiting code-switching). Both CPT source datasets came pre-deduplicated/length-filtered; publicly available on HuggingFace. | CPT 语料 = 两个公开繁中数据集：(1) Taiwan-Text-Excellence-2B（约 178 万篇文档，约 20 亿 token 的高质量新闻/文章）和 (2) republic_of_china_judgements_4_continue_pretrain（约 300 MB 台湾法律判决书）。SFT = 由 GPT-4-mini 生成的 1,500 条合成样本，涵盖五个指令任务（每个 300 条）。DPO = 成对偏好三元组（首选=GPT-4-mini 生成的纯繁中回复；拒绝=团队自己的 CPT+SFT 模型产生的含语码转换的输出）。两个 CPT 源数据集已预先去重/长度过滤；在 HuggingFace 上公开。
- **Method / 方法:** CPT (not from-scratch). Base model = meta-llama/Llama-3.2-1B-Instruct with core weights kept frozen; original tokenizer used unchanged (NO vocabulary expansion). Three-stage adapter-only pipeline using LoRA applied to all attention/MLP linear layers, via LLaMA-Factory: (1) CPT — two steps: high-rank LoRA (r=512, α=1024, dropout 0.4, context 8192, packing on), general corpus (lr 6e-6) then merged, then specialized legal corpus (lr 1e-5); (2) SFT — continues the high-rank adapter (r=512, α=1024, context 4096, lr 7e-7, packing off); (3) DPO — SFT adapter merged, then a thin low-rank LoRA (r=12, α=24, lr 6e-6) trained and merged into a single deployable checkpoint. bf16 + FlashAttention, deployable on 12 GB GPUs. | 采用 CPT（非从头训练）。基座模型 = meta-llama/Llama-3.2-1B-Instruct，核心权重全程冻结；直接使用原始 tokenizer（不做词表扩展）。基于 LLaMA-Factory 的三阶段纯适配器流程，LoRA 作用于所有注意力/MLP 线性层：(1) CPT——两步：高秩 LoRA（r=512, α=1024, dropout 0.4, 上下文 8192, 开启 packing），先在通用语料上训练（学习率 6e-6）并合并，再在专门的法律语料上训练（学习率 1e-5）；(2) SFT——延续高秩适配器（r=512, α=1024, 上下文 4096, 学习率 7e-7, 关闭 packing）；(3) DPO——合并 SFT 适配器后，训练一个低秩"薄" LoRA（r=12, α=24, 学习率 6e-6）并合并成单一可部署检查点。bf16 + FlashAttention，可在 12 GB GPU 上部署。
- **Models & Eval / 模型与评测:** Model = PureTC-1B (Llama-3.2-1B based). Evaluated on ~605 GPT-4o-mini-generated prompts across 8 task families (660 in the aggregate table) under fixed decoding (temp 0.2, top-p 0.9). New metrics: Other-Language Rate (OLR) and Pass@TC (fraction of perfectly pure outputs). Results vs base Llama-3.2-1B-Instruct: micro-avg OLR 0.231→0.113 (−51.3% rel.), Pass@TC 10.4%→30.3% (+19.9 pp); macro-avg OLR 0.214→0.098 (−66.8% rel.), Pass@TC 9.5%→29.9% (+20.4 pp, ≈3.1×). On Named Entity Translation, non-TC contamination reduced 77.2% vs Llama-3B and 57.2% vs Qwen-1.5B. Gains hold on 7 of 8 task families (sole regression = Noise Robustness). | 模型 = PureTC-1B（基于 Llama-3.2-1B）。在约 605 条由 GPT-4o-mini 生成、涵盖 8 个任务族的提示上评测（汇总表为 660 条），固定解码（温度 0.2, top-p 0.9）。提出新指标：其他语言率（OLR）和 Pass@TC（完全纯净输出的比例）。相对基座 Llama-3.2-1B-Instruct 的结果：微平均 OLR 0.231→0.113（相对下降 51.3%），Pass@TC 10.4%→30.3%（+19.9 个百分点）；宏平均 OLR 0.214→0.098（相对下降 66.8%），Pass@TC 9.5%→29.9%（+20.4 个百分点，约 3.1 倍）。在命名实体翻译任务上，非繁中污染相对 Llama-3B 降低 77.2%，相对 Qwen-1.5B 降低 57.2%。8 个任务族中有 7 个取得提升（唯一退步 = 噪声鲁棒性）。
- **Key Findings / 主要发现:** Robust TC linguistic stability is attainable even at the 1B scale via an adapter-only CPT→SFT→DPO pipeline; stability is not simply a function of model scale (a stabilized 1B model beats 3B/1.5B baselines on most stability tasks). The recipe is reproducible, hardware-friendly, and potentially transferable to other non-English languages. | 通过纯适配器的 CPT→SFT→DPO 流程，即使在 1B 规模也能实现稳健的繁中语言稳定性；稳定性并非单纯由模型规模决定（稳定化后的 1B 模型在多数稳定性任务上胜过 3B/1.5B 基线）。该方案可复现、对硬件友好，并有望迁移到其他非英语语言。
- **Limitations / 局限:** Regression on Noise Robustness (over-translates URLs/code/emojis instead of ignoring them); the strict OLR framing treats all English as contamination even when proper nouns/acronyms are legitimate; the evaluation set is imbalanced across task families; larger baselines (7B+) unexplored due to hardware constraints. | 在噪声鲁棒性上退步（会翻译/音译 URL、代码、表情符号而非忽略它们）；严格的 OLR 框架把所有英文都视为污染，即使专有名词/缩写是合理的；评测集在各任务族间不均衡；受硬件限制未探索更大的基线（7B+）。

### TigerBot: An Open Multilingual Multitask LLM (Chen et al., 2023)

Alignment stage: 5 million SFT instruction-completion examples + 15k human comparison pairs for RLHF (10k for rejection sampling, 5k for DPO) + ~40k safety demonstrations. Released under Apache-2.0 (but base licenses of Llama-2 / BLOOM still apply). | 预训练约 5000 亿 token（表内约 4700 亿 + 约 5% 的整体训练数据），即从 5.6TB 清洗/去重/下采样得到的 1.8TB 纯文本，取自 25 个公开+专有数据集，中英比例约 5:5（中文源含 WuDao 和 WanJuan；英文含 C4、RefinedWeb、BookCorpus、arXiv、GitHub、Stack Overflow；还有日语/韩语维基）。对齐阶段：500 万条 SFT 指令补全样本 + 1.5 万条人工比较对用于 RLHF（1 万条用于拒绝采样，5 千条用于 DPO）+ 约 4 万条安全示范数据。以 Apache-2.0 发布（但仍受 Llama-2 / BLOOM 基座许可约束）。
- **Method / 方法:** CPT (continual pretraining), not from-scratch. 7B/13B/70B continual pretrained from Llama-2 variants; 180B pretrained from BLOOM. Vocabulary expansion for Llama-2 models: Llama-2 has only ~0.13% Chinese, so they trained a BPE SentencePiece tokenizer on 100GB of Chinese/Japanese/Korean and merged it with the original 32k vocab, expanding to just under 65k; the 180B keeps BLOOM's 250k vocab unchanged. Key techniques: "holistic training" (mixing 2–5% instruction-completion data into pretraining so base models follow instructions before alignment); custom Megatron-DeepSpeed fork with 3D parallelism (TP/PP/DP), GQA, FlashAttention, RoPE base freq raised to 500k (extrapolated to 32k context, scaling factor 8); SFT then RLHF via rejection-sampling fine-tuning + DPO; ExLlamaV2/W8A16 quantization. | 采用 CPT（继续预训练），非从头训练。7B/13B/70B 由 Llama-2 各变体继续预训练；180B 从 BLOOM 预训练。对 Llama-2 模型进行词表扩展：Llama-2 中文仅约 0.13%，因此在 100GB 中/日/韩语料上训练 BPE SentencePiece tokenizer 并与原始 32k 词表合并，扩展到略低于 65k；180B 保持 BLOOM 的 250k 词表不变。关键技术："整体训练"（在预训练中混入 2–5% 的指令补全数据，使基座模型在对齐前即能遵循指令）；自研的 Megatron-DeepSpeed 分支，实现 3D 并行（TP/PP/DP）、GQA、FlashAttention，将 RoPE 基频提高到 500k（外推到 32k 上下文，缩放因子 8）；先 SFT 再通过拒绝采样微调 + DPO 进行 RLHF；ExLlamaV2/W8A16 量化。
- **Models & Eval / 模型与评测:** Base + chat models at 7B, 13B, 70B, 180B. Aggregate claim: 6% gain in English and 20% gain in Chinese over SOTA open-source (e.g. Llama-2). On 13 benchmarks across 8 tasks (base 0-shot, OpenCompass framework): TigerBot-70B-base English average 66.58 (vs Llama-2 63.24), e.g. MMLU 68.68, GSM8K 65.66, HumanEval 28.66; Chinese average 65.26 (vs Llama-2 52.27), e.g. C-EVAL 67.75, CMRC 85.93. 70B-chat English avg 69.87, Chinese avg 64.80 (vs Llama-2 13B-chat 59.43/48.71). Also human evaluation (5k gold prompts, 1–5 Likert) as gatekeeper. | 提供 7B、13B、70B、180B 的基座 + chat 模型。整体宣称：相对 SOTA 开源模型（如 Llama-2）英文提升 6%、中文提升 20%。在覆盖 8 类任务的 13 个基准上（基座 0-shot，OpenCompass 框架）：TigerBot-70B-base 英文平均 66.58（对比 Llama-2 63.24），如 MMLU 68.68、GSM8K 65.66、HumanEval 28.66；中文平均 65.26（对比 Llama-2 52.27），如 C-EVAL 67.75、CMRC 85.93。70B-chat 英文平均 69.87、中文平均 64.80（对比 Llama-2 13B-chat 59.43/48.71）。另有人工评测（5 千条 gold 提示，1–5 李克特量表）作为把关。
- **Key Findings / 主要发现:** Continual pretraining from strong open bases plus tokenizer expansion and holistic training yields SOTA-tier multilingual (esp. Chinese) models at very low cost. Holistic training makes base models instruction-capable (Tigerbot-13b-base reaches 86% of chat model's next-token accuracy on SQuAD2.0) so alignment can be lightweight; smaller fine-tuning batches (100–400k tokens) give lower loss with high-quality data; smaller TP yields better global training efficiency. | 从强开源基座继续预训练，结合词表扩展与整体训练，能以极低成本得到 SOTA 级别的多语言（尤其中文）模型。整体训练使基座模型具备指令能力（Tigerbot-13b-base 在 SQuAD2.0 上达到 chat 模型 86% 的下一 token 准确率），从而对齐可以轻量化；在高质量数据下，更小的微调批量（10–40 万 token）能获得更低的损失；更小的 TP 带来更好的全局训练效率。
- **Limitations / 局限:** The paper is largely an engineering/system report; results are reported mostly for 7B/13B/70B (180B detailed benchmark scores are limited). Authors note LLMs remain at a "nascent stage," with challenges in reliable mission-critical tasks and sustainable infrastructure. No independent adversarial/held-out contamination analysis beyond their own eval design. | 该论文主要是工程/系统报告；结果多为 7B/13B/70B（180B 的详细基准分数有限）。作者指出 LLM 仍处"萌芽阶段"，在可靠的关键任务和可持续基础设施方面存在挑战。除其自身评测设计外，没有独立的对抗/留出污染分析。

### HuatuoGPT-II, One-stage Training for Medical Adaption of LLMs (Chen et al., 2024)
- **Venue / Link:** COLM 2024 (arXiv:2311.09774, v2); https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.09774
- **Motivation / 动机:** Conventional domain adaption uses a two-stage pipeline (continued pre-training then SFT). This introduces data heterogeneity across the two stages (differing languages, genres, formats), requires dual meticulous tuning, and causes two data-distribution shifts that exacerbate catastrophic forgetting. The authors seek a simpler, more stable one-stage protocol for medical adaption in Chinese. | 传统的领域自适应采用两阶段流程（先继续预训练再 SFT）。这会在两阶段间引入数据异质性（语言、体裁、格式不同），需要双重精细调参，并导致两次数据分布漂移，加剧灾难性遗忘。作者寻求一种更简单、更稳定的单阶段协议，用于中文医学自适应。
- **Corpus & Data / 语料与数据:** Medical pre-training corpus: 1.1TB raw Chinese+English text (encyclopedias, books, literature, web; general C4 + specialized PubMed), curated down to 5,252,894 premium medical documents (Chinese: web 640,621 / books 1,802,517 / encyclopedia 411,183 / literature 177,261; English: web 394,490 / books 801,522 / encyclopedia 147,059 / literature). Fine-tuning: 142K real-world medical questions from Huatuo-26M with GPT-4-generated answers; ShareGPT added for multi-round ability. All corpora publicly accessible; code/data/models open-sourced. | 医学预训练语料：1.1TB 中英文原始文本（百科、书籍、文献、网络；通用 C4 + 专业 PubMed），经筛选得到 5,252,894 篇优质医学文档（中文：网络 640,621 / 书籍 1,802,517 / 百科 411,183 / 文献 177,261；英文：网络 394,490 / 书籍 801,522 / 百科 147,059 / 文献）。微调：来自 Huatuo-26M 的 142K 条真实医学问题，答案由 GPT-4 生成；加入 ShareGPT 以支持多轮能力。所有语料公开；代码/数据/模型开源。
- **Method / 方法:** One-stage domain adaption (unifies CPT+SFT into a single stage), not from-scratch. Base models = Baichuan2-7B-Base and Baichuan2-13B-Base. Two steps: (1) Data Unification — an LLM (ChatGPT, gpt-3.5-turbo-0613) converts all heterogeneous/multilingual pre-training corpus into consistent Chinese (instruction, output) pairs identical in format to SFT data (LLM generates a question from the text, then an answer grounded in the text); (2) One-stage Training with a data priority sampling strategy — a static priority π(x)=β^Ki (β=2) gives pre-training instructions higher priority than fine-tuning ones, and sampling probability decays as data is consumed, so training starts on domain knowledge and gradually shifts to instruction interaction. No vocabulary expansion mentioned. | 单阶段领域自适应（将 CPT+SFT 统一为单一阶段），非从头训练。基座 = Baichuan2-7B-Base 和 Baichuan2-13B-Base。两步：(1) 数据统一——用 LLM（ChatGPT, gpt-3.5-turbo-0613）将所有异质/多语言预训练语料转换为格式与 SFT 数据一致的中文（指令，输出）对（LLM 先从文本生成问题，再生成基于文本的答案）；(2) 采用数据优先级采样策略的单阶段训练——静态优先级 π(x)=β^Ki（β=2）使预训练指令优先级高于微调指令，且采样概率随数据被消耗而衰减，因此训练先学领域知识再逐渐转向指令交互。未提及词表扩展。
- **Models & Eval / 模型与评测:** HuatuoGPT-II 7B and 13B. Benchmarks (MedQA, MedMCQA, CMB, CMExam, CMMLU-Med, C-Eval-Med): HuatuoGPT-II(13B) average 58.47, (7B) 55.13 — beating all open-source baselines and ChatGPT (48.51), e.g. CMB 63.34/60.39, CMExam 68.98/65.81. On the fresh 2023 Chinese National Pharmacist Licensure Examination, HuatuoGPT-II(13B) avg 52.3 ranked 2nd after GPT-4 (48.1) overall and LED the Traditional Chinese Medicine track with 51.6. GPT-4 automatic eval win/tie rate: 78.0% vs GPT-4, 81.5% vs ChatGPT, 88.0% vs HuatuoGPT. Human expert eval: 38% win + 38% tie vs GPT-4; 76% vs ChatGPT. One-stage beats two-stage by 5.3%–23% across six datasets. | HuatuoGPT-II 7B 和 13B。基准（MedQA、MedMCQA、CMB、CMExam、CMMLU-Med、C-Eval-Med）：HuatuoGPT-II(13B) 平均 58.47，(7B) 55.13——胜过所有开源基线及 ChatGPT（48.51），如 CMB 63.34/60.39、CMExam 68.98/65.81。在全新的 2023 年中国国家执业药师资格考试上，HuatuoGPT-II(13B) 平均 52.3，总分仅次于 GPT-4（48.1）居第二，并在中医药赛道以 51.6 分领先。GPT-4 自动评测胜/平率：对 GPT-4 78.0%，对 ChatGPT 81.5%，对 HuatuoGPT 88.0%。人工专家评测：对 GPT-4 38% 胜 + 38% 平；对 ChatGPT 76%。单阶段在六个数据集上比两阶段高 5.3%–23%。
- **Key Findings / 主要发现:** A one-stage protocol that unifies heterogeneous pre-training/SFT data into instruction-output pairs plus priority sampling achieves SOTA Chinese-medical performance, rivaling/surpassing GPT-4 and ChatGPT in some Chinese aspects (especially Traditional Chinese Medicine). Priority sampling with β≈2 is best (β=0 mixed or very high sequential both hurt); data unification is crucial (ablation) and smooths training loss vs the unstable two-stage loss. Fresh post-cutoff exam guards against contamination. | 将异质的预训练/SFT 数据统一为指令-输出对并配合优先级采样的单阶段协议，取得 SOTA 中文医学表现，在中文某些方面（尤其中医药）媲美/超越 GPT-4 和 ChatGPT。优先级采样在 β≈2 时最佳（β=0 混合采样或过高的顺序采样都会损害性能）；数据统一至关重要（消融实验证实）并使训练损失平滑，而两阶段损失不稳定。采用截止日期后的全新考试可防范数据污染。
- **Limitations / 局限:** Explicit ethics note: should NOT be used to give actual medical advice at this stage due to LLMs' capacity for plausible-yet-inaccurate/misleading output. Interestingly 7B beats 13B on some benchmarks (CMB-Clin), tied to backbone-capacity variance. Data unification depends on an external LLM (though shown replaceable); English/USMLE performance lags as the model is Chinese-focused. | 明确的伦理说明：因 LLM 可能产生看似合理却不准确/误导的输出，现阶段不应用于提供实际医疗建议。有趣的是在某些基准（CMB-Clin）上 7B 胜过 13B，与骨干容量差异相关。数据统一依赖外部 LLM（尽管证明可替换）；由于模型以中文为主，英文/USMLE 表现较弱。

### HuatuoGPT, towards Taming Language Model to Be a Doctor (Zhang et al., 2023)
- **Venue / Link:** arXiv preprint (arXiv:2305.15075, v1), CC BY 4.0; https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.15075
- **Motivation / 动机:** ChatGPT/GPT-4 perform relatively poorly in medicine (especially Chinese), refuse to diagnose/prescribe, never ask clarifying questions like a doctor, and hallucinate. Purely distilling from ChatGPT gives fluent but non-doctor-like models; purely learning from real doctor data gives professional but short/poorly-presented, misaligned responses. The authors argue the two data sources are complementary and combine them to "tame" a model to behave like a doctor. | ChatGPT/GPT-4 在医学（尤其中文）上表现相对较差，会拒绝诊断/开药，不像医生那样追问澄清，且会产生幻觉。仅从 ChatGPT 蒸馏得到流畅但不像医生的模型；仅从真实医生数据学习则得到专业但简短/呈现差、价值观不一致的回复。作者认为两类数据互补，将其结合以"驯化"模型使其表现得像医生。
- **Corpus & Data / 语料与数据:** Hybrid SFT data (~226K total): distilled instructions from ChatGPT 61,400 (via self-instruct); real-world instructions from doctors 69,768; distilled conversations from two role-playing ChatGPTs 68,888; real-world doctor-patient conversations 25,986 (LLM-refined). Plus general Chinese Alpaca instruction data and ShareGPT for general ability. Chinese language, medical domain. Data/code/model/reward model publicly released. | 混合 SFT 数据（共约 22.6 万条）：来自 ChatGPT 的蒸馏指令 61,400 条（通过 self-instruct）；来自医生的真实指令 69,768 条；由两个角色扮演 ChatGPT 生成的蒸馏对话 68,888 条；真实医患对话 25,986 条（经 LLM 精炼）。另加通用中文 Alpaca 指令数据和 ShareGPT 以增强通用能力。中文，医学领域。数据/代码/模型/奖励模型公开发布。
- **Method / 方法:** Two-stage training on a distilled+real hybrid, not CPT/from-scratch. Base model = BLOOMZ-7b1-mt (7B, chosen for multilingual ability and PILE/PubMed medical exposure). Stage 1: SFT on hybrid data (lr 2e-5, batch 128, context 2048, 3 epochs, 8× A100, ZeRO-3). Stage 2: RLAIF (RL from AI feedback) — train a reward model (backbone = the fine-tuned model) scored by an LLM (e.g. ChatGPT) on correctness, richness, logical consistency, diagnostic ability; then RL updating only the last two layers, 16,000 steps, lr 8e-6, reward r = rRM − λKL·DKL(π‖π0) with KL penalty λKL=0.05. First medical LLM to use RLAIF to fuse real and distilled data. | 在蒸馏+真实混合数据上进行两阶段训练，非 CPT/从头训练。基座 = BLOOMZ-7b1-mt（7B，因其多语言能力及 PILE/PubMed 医学接触而选用）。第一阶段：在混合数据上 SFT（学习率 2e-5，批量 128，上下文 2048，3 个 epoch，8× A100，ZeRO-3）。第二阶段：RLAIF（基于 AI 反馈的强化学习）——训练奖励模型（骨干=微调后的模型），由 LLM（如 ChatGPT）就正确性、丰富性、逻辑一致性、诊断能力打分；随后仅更新最后两层进行 RL，16,000 步，学习率 8e-6，奖励 r = rRM − λKL·DKL(π‖π0)，KL 惩罚 λKL=0.05。是首个用 RLAIF 融合真实与蒸馏数据的医学 LLM。
- **Models & Eval / 模型与评测:** HuatuoGPT (7B, BLOOMZ-based). On Chinese medical QA benchmarks (cMedQA2, webMedQA, Huatuo-26M) it outperforms zero-shot ChatGPT and even fine-tuned T5 on cMedQA2/webMedQA (e.g. cMedQA2 BLEU-1 25.37 vs ChatGPT 19.21). GPT-4 automatic eval (performance ratio, HuatuoGPT=1.0): beats BenTsao and DoctorGLM by large margins, beats ChatGPT overall on single-turn (7.73 vs ChatGPT's paired score), and surpasses ChatGPT in >60% of multi-turn cases; still below GPT-4. Manual eval single-turn win rates: 100% vs BenTsao, 98% vs DoctorGLM, 52% vs ChatGPT, 10.5% vs GPT-4; multi-turn: 86% vs DoctorGLM, 58% vs ChatGPT. | HuatuoGPT（7B，基于 BLOOMZ）。在中文医学 QA 基准（cMedQA2、webMedQA、Huatuo-26M）上，超过零样本 ChatGPT，甚至在 cMedQA2/webMedQA 上超过微调的 T5（如 cMedQA2 BLEU-1 25.37 对 ChatGPT 19.21）。GPT-4 自动评测（性能比，HuatuoGPT=1.0）：大幅胜过 BenTsao 和 DoctorGLM，单轮总体胜过 ChatGPT，多轮对话在 >60% 的案例中超过 ChatGPT；仍低于 GPT-4。人工评测单轮胜率：对 BenTsao 100%，对 DoctorGLM 98%，对 ChatGPT 52%，对 GPT-4 10.5%；多轮：对 DoctorGLM 86%，对 ChatGPT 58%。
- **Key Findings / 主要发现:** Fusing real doctor data with ChatGPT-distilled data via SFT + RLAIF lets a 7B distilled student surpass its teacher (ChatGPT) in most cases and achieve SOTA among open-source Chinese medical LLMs. Ablations show real data teaches doctor-like clarifying questions while distilled data teaches informative presentation; RLAIF is what enables the model to ask follow-up questions (the w/o-RLAIF variant does not). One of the first systematic medical-LLM evaluation schemes (auto + human, single- and multi-turn). | 通过 SFT + RLAIF 融合真实医生数据与 ChatGPT 蒸馏数据，使 7B 蒸馏学生在多数情况下超越其教师（ChatGPT），并在开源中文医学 LLM 中达到 SOTA。消融显示真实数据教会模型像医生一样追问，蒸馏数据教会信息丰富的呈现；RLAIF 是使模型能够追问后续问题的关键（无 RLAIF 变体不会追问）。这是最早的系统化医学 LLM 评测方案之一（自动 + 人工，单轮与多轮）。
- **Limitations / 局限:** The authors stress the difficulty of verifying accuracy/correctness of generated medical content — misleading output carries severe ethical implications, so the model is not ready for real-world biomedical deployment without further accuracy-checking and error-correction mechanisms. Still clearly below GPT-4 (manual single-turn win only 10.5%). Reference-based metrics (BLEU/ROUGE) are acknowledged as ill-suited to medical QA. | 作者强调核实生成医学内容准确性/正确性的困难——误导性输出有严重伦理后果，因此在缺乏进一步准确性检查与纠错机制前，该模型尚未准备好真实生物医学部署。仍明显低于 GPT-4（人工单轮胜率仅 10.5%）。作者承认基于参考的指标（BLEU/ROUGE）不适合医学 QA。

### SNFinLLM: Systematic and Nuanced Financial Domain Adaptation of Chinese Large Language Models (Zhao et al., 2024)
- **Venue / Link:** arXiv preprint (cs.CL), https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.02302
- **Motivation / 动机:** Existing financial LLMs suffer from hallucinations and superficial parameter training, giving weak results especially in financial computation and machine reading comprehension (MRC); the authors build a Chinese-financial LLM combining domain continued pre-training, SFT, and preference alignment. | 现有金融大模型存在幻觉和"浅层参数训练"问题，在金融计算与机器阅读理解（MRC）上表现欠佳；作者面向中文金融领域，结合领域持续预训练、SFT 与偏好对齐构建金融大模型。
- **Corpus & Data / 语料与数据:** Continued-pretraining corpus: 25B finance tokens plus general data for a total of 100B tokens (finance 25B, general Chinese 55B, general English 20B added to avoid catastrophic forgetting); domain:general ratio ~1:3. Sources: specialized books, academic papers, news, financial reports, official policy documents, insurance/tax laws, plus open-source general datasets. SentencePiece tokenizer added 7,689 domain-specific tokens (vocabulary expansion). SFT set: 550,000 high-quality instructions (QA, MRC, summarization, computation, sentiment, relation extraction), incl. GPT-4-expanded computation data seeded from 500 examples; deduplicated via BGE embeddings (threshold 0.76, ~8% removed) and IFD filtering (top 70%). DPO preference pairs built from GPT-4 judgments. License not specified. | 持续预训练语料：金融 25B token，加通用数据共 100B token（金融 25B、通用中文 55B、通用英文 20B，用于缓解灾难性遗忘），领域:通用比例约 1:3。来源：专业书籍、学术论文、新闻、研报、官方政策文件、保险/税收法规及开源通用数据集。用 SentencePiece 新增 7,689 个领域词（词表扩充）。SFT 集：55 万条高质量指令（问答、MRC、摘要、计算、情感、关系抽取），含由 500 个种子经 GPT-4 扩展的计算数据；用 BGE 向量去重（阈值 0.76，去除约 8%）与 IFD 筛选（取前 70%）；DPO 偏好对由 GPT-4 评判构造。许可证未说明。
- **Method / 方法:** Continued pre-training (CPT) on an unnamed "mainstream open-source base model" → SNFinLLM-base (100B tokens, 1 epoch, 3,000 warmup steps, LR 1e-5); full-parameter SFT with two system prompts (general vs. calculator) → SNFinLLM-chat; DPO (LR 1e-6, warmup ratio 0.1) → final SNFinLLM. Calculator tool integration (Toolformer-style `[Calculator(expr)->result]` executed via Python) for accurate computation. | 在未具名的"主流开源基座"上做持续预训练 → SNFinLLM-base（100B token，1 轮，3000 步预热，学习率 1e-5）；全参数 SFT，设两类系统提示（通用 vs. 计算器）→ SNFinLLM-chat；再做 DPO（学习率 1e-6，预热比 0.1）→ 最终 SNFinLLM。集成计算器工具（Toolformer 式 `[Calculator(expr)->result]`，经 Python 执行）以保证计算准确。

### CLUECorpus2020: A Large-scale Chinese Corpus for Pre-training Language Model (Xu et al., 2020)
- **Venue / Link:** arXiv preprint (arXiv:2003.01355), https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.01355
- **Motivation / 动机:** Existing Chinese pre-training corpora were limited; the authors wanted a large, clean, freely available Chinese corpus for self-supervised pre-training (masked LM) and language generation, and to test whether a compact Chinese vocabulary could match Google's larger one. | 已有的中文预训练语料规模有限,作者希望提供一个大规模、干净、可自由获取的中文语料,用于自监督预训练(掩码语言模型)与语言生成,并验证一个更紧凑的中文词表能否达到与谷歌更大词表相当的效果。
- **Corpus & Data / 语料与数据:** 100 GB of raw text with 35 billion Chinese characters, retrieved from Common Crawl (crawled July–December 2019). Split 99:0.5:0.5 into train/dev/test — train 34.7B tokens / 106M sentences / 99.0 GB (~29k files), dev and test each 0.18B tokens / 3.9M sentences / 0.5 GB. Language: Chinese. License: CC BY 4.0; dataset, vocabulary, code and models released on GitHub. | 100 GB 原始文本,含 350 亿个中文字符,来自 Common Crawl(2019 年 7—12 月抓取)。按 99:0.5:0.5 划分为训练/验证/测试集——训练集 347 亿 token / 1.06 亿句 / 99.0 GB(约 2.9 万个文件),验证集与测试集各 1.8 亿 token / 390 万句 / 0.5 GB。语言:中文。许可:CC BY 4.0;数据集、词表、代码与模型均在 GitHub 发布。
- **Method / 方法:** Heuristic cleaning pipeline extending C4's rules with Chinese-specific handling: keep only Chinese-language sentences, trim to a Chinese terminal punctuation mark, remove sentences with bad words (LDNOOBW list), drop lines containing "Javascript", deduplicate by discarding repeated four-sentence spans, collapse consecutive blanks, remove sentences containing "{", segment sentences with pyltp, and keep only sentences longer than 5. - **Models & Eval / 模型与评测:** Trained BERT-base (incl. an "mm" attention variant), RoBERTa-large-clue, and RoBERTa-tiny-clue (hidden 312, 4 layers, 7.5M params, ~7–8x faster). Tiny: RoBERTa-tiny-clue AVG 63.60 vs. ALBERT-tiny 60.65 (~4 pts below BERT-base). Large: RoBERTa-large-clue (130GB, 800K steps, CMNLI init) AVG 69.68 vs. RoBERTa-wwm-large' 69.64. - **Key Findings / 主要发现:** More data and training steps improve performance; the clean Common-Crawl corpus (C5) beat Wikipedia (MLM accuracy 77.94% vs. 72.24%); the compact clue_vocab consistently matched or beat Google's larger vocab at lower cost; CMNLI-based transfer initialization added 0.8–4 points on AFQMC. | 更多数据与训练步数能提升性能;清洗后的 Common Crawl 语料(C5)优于维基百科(MLM 准确率 77.94% 对 72.24%);紧凑的 clue_vocab 在更低成本下持续持平或超越谷歌更大词表;基于 CMNLI 的迁移初始化在 AFQMC 上带来 0.8—4 分提升。
- **Limitations / 局限:** Large-scale comparisons were constrained by limited computing resources (many experiments only at small scale); the work focused on language understanding, leaving language generation and other NLP uses for future study. | 大规模对比受限于计算资源(许多实验仅在小规模下进行);工作聚焦于语言理解,语言生成及其他 NLP 应用留待后续研究。

### ChineseWebText 2.0: Large-Scale High-quality Chinese Web Text with Multi-dimensional and Fine-grained Information (Chen et al., 2024)
- **Venue / Link:** arXiv preprint (arXiv:2411.19668), https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.19668
- **Motivation / 动机:** As LLMs move toward domain specialization and safety, coarse-grained datasets are insufficient — fine-grained information such as quality, domain and toxicity is increasingly important. ### CCI4.0: A Bilingual Pretraining Dataset for Enhancing Reasoning in LLMs (Liu et al., 2025)

- **Motivation / 动机:** Existing open corpora are limited in linguistic diversity or domain coverage, and high-quality synthetic pretraining data with explicit structured reasoning is lacking; since reasoning ability is largely formed during pretraining, the goal is a large-scale bilingual corpus that combines quality curation with diverse human-like reasoning trajectories. | 现有开源语料在语言多样性或领域覆盖上受限，缺少显式结构化推理的高质量合成预训练数据；由于推理能力主要在预训练阶段形成，目标是构建兼具质量与多样"类人推理轨迹"的大规模双语语料。
- **Corpus & Data / 语料与数据:** ~35 TB disk (35T tokens), bilingual (Chinese + English). Chinese sources include WanJuan, WuDaoCorpora, CCI-Data series, ChineseWebText2.0, MAP-CC, TeleChat-PTD, fineweb-2, HPLT2.0, CCI3.0-Data (10+ datasets). License: CC BY 4.0. | 约 35 TB 磁盘（35T tokens），双语（中文+英文）。两个子数据集：CCI4.0-M2-Base = 5.2 TB 精选中文网页语料 + 22.5 TB 来自 Nemotron-CC 的英文子集 + 数学/维基/arxiv/代码/书籍来源（中文约占 20%）；CCI4.0-M2-CoT = 45 亿条思维链（CoT）模板（超 400B tokens 合成推理数据）。中文来源包括 WanJuan、悟道、CCI 系列、ChineseWebText2.0、MAP-CC、TeleChat-PTD、fineweb-2、HPLT2.0、CCI3.0-Data（10+ 数据集）。许可证：CC BY 4.0。

Chinese preprocessing: converted to Simplified Chinese, sensitive-word filtering, min avg 10 chars/line, sample length 100-20,000 chars. Used for from-scratch pre-training evaluation. | 基于模型的五阶段流程：(1) 两阶段混合去重（Data-Juicer 模糊去重 + Google deduplicate-text-datasets 精确子串去重，长度阈值 800、最少词 35）；(2) 多分类器质量打分——英文用 3 个分类器分入 20 个质量桶（取最高分）；中文用两个微调的 XLM-Roberta 分类器（460k 训练样本来自 Qwen2.5-72B-Instruct + DeepSeek-V3，40k 测试集由 GPT-4o 生成）加一个 fastText 二分类器（22 万正样本取自 COIG-CQIA/OpenHermes-2.5-zh/smoltalk 等中文指令集，22 万负样本）；(3) 基于 LLM 的领域感知流畅度过滤——多语言领域分类器得到 26 个子领域，按领域移除困惑度损失超 99.5 百分位的样本；(4) CoT 数据合成——Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct 做语义分段+摘要，重构思维链与核心问题，输出 {核心问题, CoT, 原文档}；(5) 隐私（PII）+ 毒性处理。中文预处理：统一转简体、敏感词过滤、每行平均至少 10 字、样本长度 100-20,000 字。用于从头预训练评测。
- **Models & Eval / 模型与评测:** A 0.5B dense model (Qwen2-0.5B tokenizer/arch) trained from scratch on 100B tokens (same setup as CCI3-HQ), evaluated zero-shot via LightEval on CEval, CMMLU + ARC, HellaSwag, PIQA, MMLU, CommonsenseQA, TriviaQA, WinoGrande, OpenBookQA, SIQA. - **Key Findings / 主要发现:** Rigorous model-based curation plus explicit human reasoning templates yield cleaner training signals and consistent downstream gains, especially in math and code reflection tasks; CCI4.0 is more training-efficient (CCI4.0 at 10B tokens ≈ Nemotron-CC-HQ at 30B) and reaches saturation earlier. - **Models & Eval / 模型与评测:** A 2B-level Llama-architecture model trained from scratch (>50k steps, seq len 2048, global batch 512, constant LR 1e-3), evaluated 5-shot on C-Eval and CMMLU. Fineweb-Edu-Chinese sharply outperformed a size-matched raw-source baseline (accuracy jump around 45k steps). Cosmopedia-Chinese gave only small benchmark gains but produced coherent, knowledge-rich outputs per human evaluation. For fine-tuning, a Fineweb-Edu-Chinese-pretrained 2B model fine-tuned on Smoltalk-chinese (vs Infinity-Instruct 100k Chinese, Magpie-Qwen2-Pro-200K-Chinese; 2 epochs, LR 3e-4) achieved the strongest gains on AlignBench (multi-dimensional Chinese alignment). v2 not yet pretrained due to compute limits. | 从头训练的 2B 级 Llama 架构模型（>5 万步，序列长 2048，全局批 512，恒定学习率 1e-3），在 C-Eval 与 CMMLU 上 5-shot 评测。Fineweb-Edu-Chinese 大幅优于同规模原始来源基线（约 45k 步准确率跃升）。Cosmopedia-Chinese 基准增益很小，但人工评估显示输出连贯、知识丰富。微调方面，用 Fineweb-Edu-Chinese 预训练的 2B 模型在 Smoltalk-chinese 上微调（对比 Infinity-Instruct 10 万中文、Magpie-Qwen2-Pro-200K-Chinese；2 轮，学习率 3e-4），在 AlignBench（多维中文对齐）上取得最强提升。v2 因算力限制尚未预训练。
- **Key Findings / 主要发现:** Educational-value filtering (FineWeb-edu style) markedly boosts Chinese pretraining efficiency and downstream C-Eval/CMMLU. Truly high-quality data is rare in open Chinese corpora (most source samples score 1-2 by the v2 scorer). Fully synthetic Cosmopedia-Chinese underperforms on benchmarks — attributed to genre/stylistic homogeneity and excessive markdown formatting — despite producing polished text; blending real data and reducing markdown is recommended. Smoltalk-chinese excels for instruction alignment. | 教育价值过滤（FineWeb-edu 式）显著提升中文预训练效率与下游 C-Eval/CMMLU。开源中文语料中真正高质量数据稀少（多数源样本经 v2 打分器得 1-2 分）。全合成的 Cosmopedia-Chinese 在基准上表现欠佳——归因于体裁/风格同质化与过度 markdown 格式——尽管能产出润饰文本；建议混入真实数据并减少 markdown。Smoltalk-chinese 在指令对齐上表现突出。
- **Limitations / 局限:** Cosmopedia-Chinese suffers over-homogeneity and markdown overuse (needs real-data blending); v2 pretraining not yet run due to limited compute; evaluation relies mainly on C-Eval/CMMLU/AlignBench and would benefit from broader metrics (factual correctness, reasoning depth, safety). | Cosmopedia-Chinese 存在同质化与 markdown 过度问题（需混入真实数据）；因算力有限 v2 尚未做预训练实验；评测主要依赖 C-Eval/CMMLU/AlignBench，宜引入更广指标（事实正确性、推理深度、安全性）。

### WanJuan: A Comprehensive Multimodal Dataset for Advancing English and Chinese Large Models (He et al., 2023)
- **Venue / Link:** arXiv technical report (arXiv:2308.10755v3, cs.CL/cs.CV), Shanghai AI Laboratory, https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.10755
- **Motivation / 动机:** Leading LLMs/MLLMs owe their performance to high-quality data, but training data of leading paradigms is kept confidential; this lack of transparency plus scarce open-source data impedes community progress, motivating a large open bilingual multimodal dataset. | 领先的大语言/多模态模型的性能得益于高质量数据，但主流范式的训练数据往往保密；这种不透明加上开源数据稀缺阻碍社区发展，因此需要一个大规模开放的双语多模态数据集。
- **Corpus & Data / 语料与数据:** Total volume exceeding 2TB, Chinese + English, three modalities. Text: >600M documents (624M files), ~1019.7 GB (>1TB); sources by weight — EN/WebText 61.4%, CN/WebText 35.3%, CN/Law 1.3%, CN/ChinaNews 1.1%, CN/Exam 0.6%, CN/Patent 0.2%, CN/TextBook 0.07%, CN/Wiki 0.01% (covers technology, literature, media, education, law). Interleaved image-text: 22M documents, ~201.1 GB excluding images (images via URL) — CN/Self-Media News 53.4%, EN/Wiki 37.7%, CN/Authoritative Media News 5.3%, CN/Wiki 3.6%; final language split 62.3% Chinese / 37.7% English. Video: ~1,000 files, ~916.7 GB, from China Media Group (CMG) & Shanghai Media Group (SMG). License: dataset accessed at opendatalab.org.cn/WanJuan1.0 (paper under arXiv non-exclusive license). | 总量超过 2TB，中英双语，三种模态。文本：超 6 亿文档（6.24 亿文件），约 1019.7 GB（>1TB）；按权重来源——英文网页 61.4%、中文网页 35.3%、中文法律 1.3%、中国新闻 1.1%、考试 0.6%、专利 0.2%、教材 0.07%、维基 0.01%（覆盖科技、文学、媒体、教育、法律）。图文交错：2200 万文档，约 201.1 GB（不含图片，图片经 URL 提供）——中文自媒体新闻 53.4%、英文维基 37.7%、中文权威媒体新闻 5.3%、中文维基 3.6%；最终语言占比 62.3% 中文 / 37.7% 英文。视频：约 1000 个文件，约 916.7 GB，来自中央广播电视总台（CMG）与上海广播电视台（SMG）。许可证：数据集在 opendatalab.org.cn/WanJuan1.0 获取（论文采用 arXiv 非独占许可）。
- **Method / 方法:** Rule- and model-based cleaning with manual verification, unified JSON format. Text: extract from WARC files, language detection (pycld2), then filtering — remove irregular docs (bad average word/doc length, non-alphabetic most-frequent word), remove short docs (<3 sentences / <3 paragraphs / <3 paragraphs >200 words / <2 stopwords), clean paragraphs (drop JavaScript sections, >1000-word paragraphs), MinHashLSH + n-gram dedup (similarity >0.8 removed). FastText content-safety models (pornography, violence, gambling, attacks) and data-quality models trained separately for Chinese and English. Image-text: per-site parsing rules, extract ad/list/navigation/comment-free article body (XPath/CSS/regex), Wikipedia keep header image only, drop articles with >15 images or text-chars < 2x image count (retaining 55% of valid articles), similarity dedup. Video (CMG/SMG) treated as high quality, minimal cleaning. Used for pretraining (InternLM). | 基于规则与模型的清洗加人工验证，统一 JSON 格式。文本：从 WARC 文件抽取，语言检测（pycld2），再过滤——移除不规则文档（平均词/文档长度异常、最高频词非字母类）、移除过短文档（<3 句 / <3 段 / 少于 3 个超 200 词的段 / <2 个停用词）、清洗段落（去 JavaScript 段、>1000 词段落）、MinHashLSH + n-gram 去重（相似度 >0.8 删除）。用 FastText 分别为中英训练内容安全模型（色情、暴力、赌博、攻击）与数据质量模型。图文：按站点定制解析规则，抽取无广告/列表/导航/评论的正文（XPath/CSS/正则），维基仅保留首图，移除图片>15 张或文本字符数 < 图片数 2 倍的文章（保留 55% 有效文章），相似度去重。视频（CMG/SMG）视为高质量，清洗极少。用于预训练（InternLM）。
- **Models & Eval / 模型与评测:** WanJuan was used to train InternLM, which the paper reports showed significant advantages in multi-dimensional evaluations compared to models of similar scale. No specific benchmark names or numeric scores are reported in the paper. | WanJuan 用于训练 InternLM，论文称其在多维评测中相较同规模模型有显著优势。论文未给出具体基准名称或数值分数。
- **Key Findings / 主要发现:** A large (>2TB) open bilingual multimodal corpus spanning text, interleaved image-text, and video can be built with safety/quality/value-alignment filtering and shared in a unified format; it improved knowledge, reasoning, and generalization of trained models and supported InternLM. | 可以在安全/质量/价值对齐过滤下构建并以统一格式共享一个大型（>2TB）开放双语多模态语料（覆盖文本、图文交错、视频）；它提升了训练模型的知识、推理与泛化能力，并支撑了 InternLM。
- **Limitations / 局限:** The paper gives no quantitative model-evaluation results (no benchmark scores); token counts are not reported (only file counts and GB); images are provided only via URL links (not stored), risking link rot; video is limited (~1,000 files) and from only two official media sources; data reflects a 2023 snapshot. | 论文未给出定量的模型评测结果（无基准分数）；未报告 token 数（仅文件数与 GB）；图片仅以 URL 链接提供（不存储），存在链接失效风险；视频量有限（约 1000 个）且仅来自两个官方媒体来源；数据为 2023 年快照。

## 4. Chinese-Centric Open Models Releasing Large Corpora / 四、发布大规模语料的中文开源模型

### Chinese Tiny LLM: Pretraining a Chinese-Centric Large Language Model (Du et al., 2024)
- **Venue / Link:** Published as a conference paper at COLM 2024 (arXiv:2404.04167v5, 13 Sep 2024; paper license CC BY-SA 4.0), https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.04167
- **Motivation / 动机:** Challenges the dominant paradigm of training LLMs mainly on English and adapting to other languages afterwards; proposes a "pivotal shift" toward prioritizing Chinese from the outset to foster more inclusive models and broaden training methodologies. | 挑战当前"以英文为主训练、再迁移到其他语言"的主流范式;主张从一开始就"以中文为优先"的关键转向,以培育更具包容性的模型并拓宽训练方法学。
- **Corpus & Data / 语料与数据:** Introduces MAP-CC (Massive Appropriate Pretraining Chinese Corpus), an open-sourced Chinese pretraining dataset of ~800 billion tokens. It is the Chinese portion of a larger 1,254.68B-token training mix (840.48B Chinese, 314.88B English, 99.3B code; ~110B intentional duplicate tokens, mostly high-quality English reused twice). Sources: Common Crawl web documents, scholarly articles, encyclopedias, and books. Data pipeline uses heuristic filtering rules adapted from RefinedWeb, CCNet, Gopher, and T5 (specifically adapted for Chinese), plus multi-level deduplication (document-level exact dedup via Bloom filter at fpr 0.001, MinHash LSH, and intra-document similar-line dedup). The corpus and full processing procedure are open-sourced (paper under CC BY-SA 4.0; corpus-specific license not stated in text). | 提出 MAP-CC(海量适配中文预训练语料),开源规模约 8000 亿 token 的中文预训练数据集,它是更大规模 1,254.68B token 训练混合数据(中文 840.48B、英文 314.88B、代码 99.3B;约 1100 亿有意重复 token,主要为二次复用的高质量英文)中的中文部分。来源包括 Common Crawl 网页文档、学术论文、百科与书籍。数据流水线采用改编自 RefinedWeb、CCNet、Gopher、T5 的启发式过滤规则(专门针对中文调整),并进行多级去重(基于 Bloom 过滤器、误报率 0.001 的文档级精确去重、MinHash LSH、文档内相似行去重)。语料与完整处理流程均开源(论文采用 CC BY-SA 4.0;语料专属许可协议文中未明确)。
- **Method / 方法:** From-scratch pretraining of a 2B-parameter model using the Baichuan2 tokenizer (vocabulary 125,696) with a 4,096-token context length. Chinese proficiency is further enhanced via alignment: SFT with a 2:1 Chinese-to-English data ratio (matching pretraining) followed by DPO (CT-LLM-SFT-DPO). Also introduces CHC-Bench, a multidisciplinary Chinese Hard Case Benchmark (writing, humanities/history, science, math, reading comprehension, role-playing, etc.). | 从零预训练一个 2B 参数模型,使用 Baichuan2 分词器(词表 125,696)、上下文长度 4,096。中文能力经对齐进一步增强:先以 2:1 中英数据比进行 SFT(与预训练一致),再做 DPO(CT-LLM-SFT-DPO)。同时提出 CHC-Bench(多学科中文难例基准,涵盖写作、人文历史、科学、数学、阅读理解、角色扮演等)。
- **Models & Eval / 模型与评测:** Releases the 2B CT-LLM (base, SFT, and SFT-DPO). Base-model results (Table 2, vs. similar-scale models): MMLU 37.11, C-Eval 36.78, CMMLU 36.4, HumanEval (pass@1) 9.15, GSM8K 8.87, COPA 59.0, HellaSwag 50.37. The model shows a notably smaller gap between English (MMLU) and Chinese (CMMLU) than baselines like TinyLlama-1.1B and Bloom-1.7B, and rivals MiniCPM/Phi-2 on some reasoning tasks; it also performs strongly on CHC-Bench and safety evaluation. | 发布 2B 的 CT-LLM(base、SFT、SFT-DPO)。基座模型结果(表 2,对比同规模模型):MMLU 37.11、C-Eval 36.78、CMMLU 36.4、HumanEval(pass@1)9.15、GSM8K 8.87、COPA 59.0、HellaSwag 50.37。相较 TinyLlama-1.1B、Bloom-1.7B 等基线,其英文(MMLU)与中文(CMMLU)之间差距明显更小,在部分推理任务上与 MiniCPM/Phi-2 相当;在 CHC-Bench 与安全性评测上亦表现良好。
- **Key Findings / 主要发现:** A Chinese-centric from-scratch pretraining recipe can produce a compact model that is strong in Chinese while retaining balanced English/reasoning ability, with a smaller cross-lingual performance gap than Western-centric baselines. Full open-sourcing (MAP-CC corpus, CHC-Bench, data pipeline, and model) supports reproducibility. | 以中文为中心的从零预训练配方,可训练出中文能力强、且英文/推理能力保持均衡的小模型,其跨语言性能差距小于以西方语言为中心的基线。完整开源(MAP-CC 语料、CHC-Bench、数据流水线与模型)支撑可复现性。
- **Limitations / 局限:** Constrained by the small 2B scale (the authors note evaluation criteria must account for 2B-model limitations); high-quality curated Chinese resources are limited in quantity; overall absolute benchmark scores remain modest compared with larger models. | 受限于 2B 的小规模(作者指出评测标准须考虑 2B 模型的能力上限);高质量精编中文资源数量有限;整体绝对基准分相较更大模型仍偏低。

### Skywork: A More Open Bilingual Foundation Model (Wei et al., 2023)
- **Venue / Link:** arXiv:2310.19341 (30 Oct 2023), Skywork Team / Kunlun Inc. (paper license CC BY 4.0), https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.19341
- **Motivation / 动机:** Counter the trend of commercialization and declining transparency in the open-source LLM community by releasing an extensively trained, openly published bilingual (English/Chinese) foundation model; also highlights test-data contamination as a pressing, under-addressed issue. | 针对开源 LLM 社区商业化加剧、透明度下降的趋势,发布一个经过充分训练、公开发布的中英双语基础模型;同时强调测试数据污染是一个亟待解决且未被充分重视的问题。
- **Corpus & Data / 语料与数据:** Introduces SkyPile, a corpus built from public web sources comprising over 6 trillion tokens in total; a segment of over 150 billion tokens of web text (SkyPile-150B) is publicly released, described as the largest high-quality open Chinese pretraining corpus at the time. SkyPile is segmented into SkyPile-Main (general web) and SkyPile-STEM (coding problems, national exam questions, textbook exercises, curated parallel corpora, etc.). Deduplication is treated as an integral part of the distribution-filtering process (paper license CC BY 4.0; released weights and SkyPile-150B available on GitHub). | 提出 SkyPile 语料,基于公开网页构建、总规模超过 6 万亿 token;其中超过 1500 亿 token 的网页文本(SkyPile-150B)公开发布,被称为当时最大的高质量开源中文预训练语料。SkyPile 分为 SkyPile-Main(通用网页)与 SkyPile-STEM(编程题、国家考试题、教材习题、精编平行语料等)。去重被视为分布过滤流程的组成部分(论文采用 CC BY 4.0;模型权重与 SkyPile-150B 在 GitHub 提供)。
- **Method / 方法:** From-scratch training with a two-stage methodology on a segmented corpus: Stage-1 trains on SkyPile-Main for over 3 trillion tokens (two sessions: 0-2T then 2-3T), Stage-2 enriches with STEM knowledge using a mix of SkyPile-STEM and SkyPile-Main (rather than STEM alone, to avoid catastrophic forgetting). Total ~3.2T training tokens. Architecture follows LLaMA but favors a deeper, narrower network: 52 layers, hidden dim 4,608, FFN dim 12,288, vocab 65,536, SwiGLU, RoPE, RMSNorm with pre-normalization. Trained on 512 NVIDIA A800-80G GPUs (64 HGX-A800 nodes) via Megatron-LM with DP256+PP2+ZeRO-1 and FlashAttention V2, reaching 1,873 tokens/GPU/s and 56.5% MFU over 39 days. Proposes a novel leakage/contamination detection method. | 采用从零训练与分段语料的两阶段方法:第一阶段在 SkyPile-Main 上训练超过 3 万亿 token(两段:0-2T 与 2-3T);第二阶段用 SkyPile-STEM 与 SkyPile-Main 混合(而非仅用 STEM,以避免灾难性遗忘)注入 STEM 知识。共训练约 3.2T token。架构沿用 LLaMA 但偏向更深更窄的网络:52 层、隐藏维 4,608、FFN 维 12,288、词表 65,536,采用 SwiGLU、RoPE、RMSNorm 及前归一化。在 512 张 NVIDIA A800-80G GPU(64 个 HGX-A800 节点)上用 Megatron-LM 训练,采用 DP256+PP2+ZeRO-1 与 FlashAttention V2,吞吐 1,873 token/GPU/秒、MFU 56.5%,历时 39 天。提出一种新颖的数据泄漏/污染检测方法。
- **Models & Eval / 模型与评测:** Releases the Skywork-13B family (~13B params): Skywork-13B-Base and Skywork-13B-Chat, plus intermediate checkpoints. On 5-shot CEVAL/CMMLU/MMLU and 8-shot GSM8K (Table 5), Skywork-13B scores CEVAL 60.6, CMMLU 61.8, MMLU 62.1, GSM8K 55.8 — the highest on CEVAL, MMLU, and GSM8K among 13B-class peers (LLaMA-2-13B, Baichuan2-13B, XVERSE-13B, InternLM-20B, etc.). In language modeling (perplexity), Skywork-13B achieves the lowest average perplexity of 9.42, best across individual domains. | 发布 Skywork-13B 系列(约 13B 参数):Skywork-13B-Base 与 Skywork-13B-Chat,并提供中间检查点。在 5-shot CEVAL/CMMLU/MMLU 与 8-shot GSM8K(表 5)上,Skywork-13B 取得 CEVAL 60.6、CMMLU 61.8、MMLU 62.1、GSM8K 55.8——在 13B 级别对手(LLaMA-2-13B、Baichuan2-13B、XVERSE-13B、InternLM-20B 等)中于 CEVAL、MMLU、GSM8K 上得分最高。在语言建模(困惑度)上,Skywork-13B 取得最低平均困惑度 9.42,并在各细分领域均表现最佳。
- **Key Findings / 主要发现:** A moderately sized 13B model, extensively trained on 3.2T bilingual tokens with staged general-then-domain training, can reach SOTA Chinese language modeling and strong bilingual benchmark results. The authors also demonstrate that test-data contamination is real and measurable, and release intermediate checkpoints to enable scientific study of training dynamics. | 一个中等规模的 13B 模型,经过 3.2T 双语 token 的充分训练与"先通用后领域"的分阶段训练,即可达到中文语言建模的 SOTA 并取得强劲的双语基准结果。作者还证明测试数据污染真实且可度量,并发布中间检查点以支持对训练动态的科学研究。
- **Limitations / 局限:** Test-data contamination remains a pressing issue warranting further community investigation; pretraining on in-domain data carries both benefits and risks discussed only qualitatively; the released SkyPile-150B is only a fraction (~150B of >6T) of the full training corpus. | 测试数据污染仍是亟待社区进一步研究的紧迫问题;在领域内数据上预训练的收益与风险仅作定性讨论;公开的 SkyPile-150B 仅为完整训练语料的一小部分(约 150B/>6T)。

### MAP-Neo: Highly Capable and Transparent Bilingual Large Language Model Series (Zhang et al., 2024)
- **Venue / Link:** arXiv:2405.19327v4 (29 May 2024, rev. 10 Jul 2024), M-A-P / University of Waterloo / Wuhan AI Research / 01.AI (paper license CC BY-SA 4.0), https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.19327
- **Motivation / 动机:** The most competitive LLMs (GPT, Gemini, Claude) are gated behind proprietary APIs, and even open-weight models like LLaMA-3 disclose little (no intermediate checkpoints, corpus, or training code). "Truly open" LLMs (Pythia, Amber, OLMo) share more but remain inferior on reasoning, knowledge, coding, and multilingualism. MAP-Neo aims to close this gap with full transparency. | 最具竞争力的 LLM(GPT、Gemini、Claude)被封闭在专有 API 之后,即便是 LLaMA-3 等开放权重模型也几乎不披露细节(无中间检查点、语料或训练代码)。"真正开放"的 LLM(Pythia、Amber、OLMo)披露更多,但在推理、知识、代码与多语能力上仍落后。MAP-Neo 旨在以完全透明来弥合这一差距。
- **Corpus & Data / 语料与数据:** Releases the Matrix Data Pile (Matrix), a bilingual pretraining corpus of 4.5T tokens — described as the largest fully transparent LLM pretraining corpus at release. Composition: 52.55% Common Crawl, 22.29% programming code, and the remainder from academic papers, books, and other printed materials; the Chinese subset is ~80.6% self-crawled Chinese web pages. The pipeline includes a stable OCR system (Pix2Text with TrOCR for formulas, PP-OCRv4 for detection/recognition) plus OCR post-processing to fix line/page breaks and broken math formulas, a DeepSeek-Math-style data-recalling/quality-filtering mechanism (positive/negative sample classifiers over Common Crawl, RedPajama-V2, OpenWebMath seeds), URL blacklist filtering, and deduplication. Corpus, cleaning pipeline, checkpoints, and training/eval framework all open-sourced (paper CC BY-SA 4.0). | 发布 Matrix Data Pile(Matrix),一个 4.5T token 的双语预训练语料——被称为发布时最大的完全透明 LLM 预训练语料。组成:52.55% 来自 Common Crawl、22.29% 为编程代码,其余来自学术论文、书籍及其他印刷材料;中文子集约 80.6% 为自采中文网页。流水线包含稳定的 OCR 系统(公式用 Pix2Text/TrOCR,检测与识别用 PP-OCRv4)及 OCR 后处理(修复换行/分页与破损数学公式)、DeepSeek-Math 式的数据召回/质量过滤机制(在 Common Crawl、RedPajama-V2、OpenWebMath 种子上做正/负样本分类)、URL 黑名单过滤与去重。语料、清洗流水线、检查点及训练/评测框架全部开源(论文 CC BY-SA 4.0)。
- **Method / 方法:** From-scratch training of two model scales, 2B and 7B (the 2B variant uses Multi-Query Attention; the 7B uses Multi-Head Attention). Vocabulary size 64,000 with max sentence-piece length constrained to 16. Two-phase pretraining: a Fundamental Phase (general ability acquisition on generic text, two-stage LR schedule with cosine decay to ηb=2e-5) followed by a Decay Phase (improvement/rectification: inject high-quality data and increase code volume, using Stack V2 for code plus duplicated high-quality sources like books, judicial decisions, government reports). Full post-training pipeline includes SFT (fundamental + chat phases) and iterative DPO. | 从零训练两个规模:2B 与 7B(2B 使用多查询注意力 MQA,7B 使用多头注意力 MHA)。词表 64,000,并将最长 sentence-piece 长度限制为 16。两阶段预训练:基础阶段(在通用文本上获取通用能力,采用两段式学习率调度并以余弦衰减至 ηb=2e-5),随后进入衰减阶段(改进/纠偏:注入高质量数据并提高代码占比,代码使用 Stack V2,并对书籍、司法判决、政府报告等高质量来源做重复采样)。完整后训练流程包含 SFT(基础阶段+对话阶段)与迭代 DPO。
- **Models & Eval / 模型与评测:** Releases MAP-Neo 2B and 7B. MAP-Neo-7B results (Table 1, unified eval vs. other open LLMs): C-EVAL 57.68, MMLU 58.14, GSM8K 53.68, HumanEval 23.8. It clearly surpasses all "transparent" peers (Pythia-6.9B, Amber-7B, OLMo-7B — e.g., OLMo-7B scores C-EVAL 35.21 / MMLU 53.52 / GSM8K 28.43 / HumanEval 11.6) and is competitive with strong non-transparent models such as Mistral-7B and LLaMA3-8B (LLaMA3-8B: C-EVAL 49.83 / MMLU 66.52 / GSM8K 54.74 / HumanEval 33.5), notably leading on the Chinese C-EVAL. | 发布 MAP-Neo 2B 与 7B。MAP-Neo-7B 结果(表 1,统一评测对比其他开源 LLM):C-EVAL 57.68、MMLU 58.14、GSM8K 53.68、HumanEval 23.8。它明显超越所有"透明"同类(Pythia-6.9B、Amber-7B、OLMo-7B——如 OLMo-7B 为 C-EVAL 35.21 / MMLU 53.52 / GSM8K 28.43 / HumanEval 11.6),并与 Mistral-7B、LLaMA3-8B 等强力非透明模型相当(LLaMA3-8B:C-EVAL 49.83 / MMLU 66.52 / GSM8K 54.74 / HumanEval 33.5),尤其在中文 C-EVAL 上领先。
- **Key Findings / 主要发现:** MAP-Neo is presented as the first fully open-sourced bilingual LLM with performance comparable to existing SOTA models of similar size; unlike OLMo (the closest prior fully-open work), it handles Chinese and achieves strong math/reasoning/code. Releasing every artifact (corpus, cleaning pipeline, checkpoints, training/eval code) provides a reproducible handbook for building LLMs from scratch. | MAP-Neo 被定位为首个性能可与同规模现有 SOTA 相媲美的完全开源双语 LLM;与此前最接近的完全开源工作 OLMo 不同,它能处理中文并在数学/推理/代码上表现强劲。开放全部产物(语料、清洗流水线、检查点、训练/评测代码)提供了从零构建 LLM 的可复现"手册"。
- **Limitations / 局限:** OCR tools have known limitations (limited multi-column/multi-language support); under the earlier tokenizer setting, code metrics fluctuated and did not improve steadily during the fundamental phase (addressed only later in the decay phase with the new tokenizer); on English MMLU it still trails LLaMA3-8B. | OCR 工具存在已知局限(对多栏排版/多语言支持有限);在早期分词器设置下,基础阶段的代码指标波动且未稳步提升(直到衰减阶段更换新分词器后才得到解决);在英文 MMLU 上仍落后于 LLaMA3-8B。

### Steel-LLM: From Scratch to Open Source — A Personal Journey in Building a Chinese-Centric LLM (Gu et al., 2025)
- **Venue / Link:** arXiv:2502.06635v2 (10 Feb 2025, rev. 13 Feb 2025), Tsinghua University / BUPT (paper license CC BY-SA 4.0; code at github.com/zhanshijinwat/Steel-LLM), https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.06635
- **Motivation / 动机:** Build a high-quality, fully open-source Chinese-centric LLM despite very limited compute, and share a detailed, practical account of the entire model-building journey to help small teams and resource-constrained researchers — addressing the limited transparency of large-institution models (Qwen, Llama, DeepSeek, Gemma, InternLM) that withhold data/code/checkpoints. | 在算力极其有限的条件下构建一个高质量、完全开源的中文为中心 LLM,并分享贯穿整个建模过程的详尽、务实记录,以帮助小团队与资源受限的研究者——针对大机构模型(Qwen、Llama、DeepSeek、Gemma、InternLM)不公开数据/代码/检查点的透明度不足问题。
- **Corpus & Data / 语料与数据:** Trained on a large-scale, predominantly Chinese dataset (with a small proportion of English) of approximately 1 trillion tokens, assembled entirely from open-source datasets. Named sources include SkyPile-150B (~150B tokens, 620 GB cleaned web text), Wanjuan1.0, Wikipedia-cn (254,574 filtered high-quality entries with simplified/traditional conversion), Baidu Baike (5.63M uncleaned entries), Baidu QA, Zhihu QA, and Starcoder code data (783 GB of code across 86 programming languages, plus 54 GB GitHub Issues, 13 GB Jupyter notebooks, 32 GB GitHub commits). The pipeline applies 21 text-processing and 13 code-processing operators; the Qwen1.5 BPE tokenizer (vocab 151,936) converts the corpus to token-ids. | 在约 1 万亿 token、以中文为主(含少量英文)的大规模数据集上训练,数据完全取自开源数据集。具名来源包括 SkyPile-150B(约 150B token、620 GB 清洗后网页文本)、Wanjuan1.0(万卷1.0)、Wikipedia-cn(过滤后 254,574 条高质量词条并做简繁转换)、百度百科(563 万条未清洗词条)、百度问答、知乎问答,以及 Starcoder 代码数据(涵盖 86 种编程语言的 783 GB 代码,另有 54 GB GitHub Issues、13 GB Jupyter notebooks、32 GB GitHub commits)。流水线应用 21 个文本处理算子与 13 个代码处理算子;用 Qwen1.5 的 BPE 分词器(词表 151,936)将语料转为 token-id。
- **Method / 方法:** From-scratch training of a ~1B-parameter model whose architecture is adapted from Qwen but modifies the FFN: it replaces standard FFN with a Soft Mixture-of-Experts (Soft MoE, fully differentiable — chosen over sparse MoE so every parameter is fully trained on limited GPUs) plus an enhanced FFN. Config: 18 layers, 32 heads (32 KV heads), 6 experts, 1 slot/expert, hidden size 1,792, intermediate size 1,792; RoPE, Pre-Norm + RMSNorm, FlashAttention. Training framework is modified from TinyLlama with additions for resumable training, appendable data, and improved model loading. Trained on only 8 GPUs (first 200K steps on 8× A100-80G, remainder on 8× H800-80G) over 30 days and 1.07M steps (seq len 2,048, ~1T tokens), using bfloat16, FSDP, and operator fusion. Post-training: SFT (best recipe = 700K Chinese Infinity-Instruct + Wanjuan MCQ) and DPO. | 从零训练一个约 1B 参数模型,架构改编自 Qwen 但改造了 FFN:用软混合专家(Soft MoE,完全可微——相较稀疏 MoE 而言,在有限 GPU 上可让每个参数被充分训练)加增强型 FFN 替换标准 FFN。配置:18 层、32 头(32 KV 头)、6 个专家、每专家 1 个 slot、隐藏维 1,792、中间维 1,792;采用 RoPE、Pre-Norm + RMSNorm、FlashAttention。训练框架改自 TinyLlama,新增可续训、可追加数据与改进的模型加载。仅用 8 张 GPU 训练(前 20 万步用 8× A100-80G,其余用 8× H800-80G),历时 30 天、共 1.07M 步(序列长 2,048,约 1T token),使用 bfloat16、FSDP 与算子融合。后训练:SFT(最佳配方为 70 万条中文 Infinity-Instruct + 万卷选择题)与 DPO。
- **Models & Eval / 模型与评测:** Releases the ~1B Steel-LLM (base, Chat, Chat-DPO) with training scripts and intermediate checkpoints. Steel-LLM-1B-Chat scores CEVAL 41.90 / CMMLU 36.08 (Chat-DPO: 42.04 / 36.04). It outperforms similar-scale models such as Tiny-Llama-1.1B (CEVAL 25.02 / CMMLU 24.03) and Gemma-2b-it (32.30 / 33.07), and approaches the larger CT-LLM-SFT-2B (41.54 / 41.48), while remaining below MiniCPM-1.2B (49.14 / 46.81), Qwen1.5-1.8B-Chat (56.84 / 54.11), and 7B models like MAP-Neo-7B (56.97 / 55.01). | 发布约 1B 的 Steel-LLM(base、Chat、Chat-DPO),并提供训练脚本与中间检查点。Steel-LLM-1B-Chat 取得 CEVAL 41.90 / CMMLU 36.08(Chat-DPO:42.04 / 36.04)。它超越 Tiny-Llama-1.1B(CEVAL 25.02 / CMMLU 24.03)、Gemma-2b-it(32.30 / 33.07)等同规模模型,并接近更大的 CT-LLM-SFT-2B(41.54 / 41.48),但仍低于 MiniCPM-1.2B(49.14 / 46.81)、Qwen1.5-1.8B-Chat(56.84 / 54.11)及 MAP-Neo-7B(56.97 / 55.01)等 7B 模型。
- **Key Findings / 主要发现:** A small team with only 8 GPUs and ~1T tokens can build a competitive Chinese-centric ~1B LLM that outperforms some early models from larger institutions; careful SFT data selection and alignment with the pretraining distribution (e.g., filtering out mismatched English data) meaningfully lifts Chinese benchmark scores (CEVAL 39.21%→40.43%, CMMLU 33.2%→35.86%). Full transparency — pipeline, datasets, and checkpoints — is a core contribution. | 一个仅有 8 张 GPU、约 1T token 的小团队即可构建出有竞争力的中文为中心约 1B LLM,并超越部分大机构的早期模型;精心选择 SFT 数据并与预训练分布对齐(如滤除不匹配的英文数据)可显著提升中文基准分(CEVAL 39.21%→40.43%,CMMLU 33.2%→35.86%)。完整透明——流水线、数据集与检查点——是其核心贡献。
- **Limitations / 局限:** Scope constrained by very limited compute (8 GPUs); the ~1B scale caps absolute performance, and results trail larger models (Qwen1.5-1.8B-Chat, 7B models); English data is only a minor portion, so English/general capability is limited; the corpus is assembled from existing open datasets rather than newly crawled at scale. | 受极其有限的算力(8 GPU)限制;约 1B 的规模限制了绝对性能,结果落后于更大模型(Qwen1.5-1.8B-Chat 及 7B 模型);英文数据占比很小,英文/通用能力有限;语料由现有开源数据集拼装而非大规模新采。

## 5. Foundation & Bilingual LLM Technical Reports / 五、基座与双语大模型技术报告

### Qwen Technical Report (Bai et al., 2023)
- **Venue / Link:** arXiv preprint (2023), https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.16609
- **Motivation / 动机:** Most powerful LLMs are closed-source or weak in non-English languages; the authors open-source a competitive base and chat model series (plus Code-Qwen and Math-Qwen) with strong Chinese and English ability. | 最强的大模型多为闭源，或对非英语语言支持薄弱；作者开源了一套具有竞争力的基础模型与对话模型系列（外加 Code-Qwen 与 Math-Qwen），在中英文上均表现优异。
- **Corpus & Data / 语料与数据:** Base models trained on up to 3 trillion tokens (per-model: 1.8B=2.2T, 7B=2.4T, 14B=3.0T). Multilingual with a significant portion in English and Chinese. Sources: public web documents, encyclopedia, books, and code. Cleaning pipeline: HTML text extraction + language identification; exact-match deduplication after normalization plus fuzzy deduplication via MinHash and LSH; rule-based and ML-based quality filtering (language models, text-quality scoring models, offensive/inappropriate content classifiers), manual sampling/review, and selective up-sampling of high-quality sources. High-quality instruction data mixed into pretraining; benchmark decontamination by removing training samples with 13-gram overlap with test sets. Corpus itself not released; model weights released under a Qwen-specific license (Tongyi Qianwen). | 基础模型训练最多达 3 万亿 token（各模型：1.8B=2.2T，7B=2.4T，14B=3.0T）。语料为多语言，其中英文与中文占相当比例。来源包括公开网页文档、百科、书籍与代码。清洗流程：从 HTML 抽取文本并做语言识别；归一化后的精确匹配去重，以及基于 MinHash 与 LSH 的模糊去重；规则与机器学习相结合的质量过滤（语言模型、文本质量打分模型、冒犯/不当内容分类器），人工抽样审查，并对高质量来源进行选择性上采样。将高质量指令数据混入预训练；通过剔除与测试集有 13-gram 重叠的训练样本进行基准去污染。语料本身未开放，模型权重以通义千问专用许可发布。
- **Method / 方法:** Trained from scratch. Modified Transformer following LLaMA: untied input/output embeddings; RoPE with FP32 inverse-frequency matrix; biases removed except in the QKV attention layer; Pre-Norm with RMSNorm; SwiGLU activation with FFN dimension reduced to 8/3 of hidden size. Tokenizer: BPE built on tiktoken cl100k base, augmented with Chinese characters/words and other languages, numbers split into single digits, final vocab ~152K. Training context length 2048 with Flash Attention, AdamW (β1=0.9, β2=0.95), cosine LR schedule, BFloat16. Inference-time context extension via NTK-aware interpolation, LogN-Scaling, and window attention. | 从零训练。基于 LLaMA 的改进 Transformer：输入/输出嵌入不共享；使用 RoPE（逆频率矩阵用 FP32）；除注意力 QKV 层外去除偏置；采用 Pre-Norm 与 RMSNorm；SwiGLU 激活且 FFN 维度降为隐藏维的 8/3。分词器：在 tiktoken cl100k base 基础上构建 BPE，增补中文字词及其他语言，数字拆分为单个数位，最终词表约 152K。训练上下文长度 2048，使用 Flash Attention、AdamW（β1=0.9，β2=0.95）、余弦学习率、BFloat16。推理时通过 NTK-aware 插值、LogN-Scaling 与窗口注意力扩展上下文。
- **Models & Eval / 模型与评测:** Base models at 1.8B, 7B, and 14B (hidden sizes 2048/4096/5120). On Table 2 benchmarks, Qwen-14B scores MMLU 66.3 (5-shot), C-Eval 72.1 (5-shot), GSM8K 61.3, MATH 24.8, HumanEval 32.3, MBPP 40.8, BBH 53.4 — outperforming all listed 13B-class open models. Qwen-7B: MMLU 58.2, C-Eval 63.5. Qwen-1.8B: MMLU 44.6, C-Eval 54.7. Qwen still lags GPT-3.5 and GPT-4 overall. (CMMLU not reported in the main base-model table.) | 基础模型规模为 1.8B、7B、14B（隐藏维 2048/4096/5120）。表 2 中，Qwen-14B 取得 MMLU 66.3（5-shot）、C-Eval 72.1（5-shot）、GSM8K 61.3、MATH 24.8、HumanEval 32.3、MBPP 40.8、BBH 53.4，优于所列全部 13B 级开源模型。Qwen-7B：MMLU 58.2、C-Eval 63.5。Qwen-1.8B：MMLU 44.6、C-Eval 54.7。整体上仍落后于 GPT-3.5 与 GPT-4。（主基础模型表中未报告 CMMLU。）
- **Key Findings / 主要发现:** Data scale and quality are decisive; a larger 152K vocab improves multilingual (especially Chinese, code) compression without hurting downstream performance. Base models beat significantly larger open counterparts, and RLHF chat models are highly competitive though still behind GPT-4. | 数据规模与质量起决定性作用；更大的 152K 词表在不损害下游性能的前提下提升了多语言（尤其中文、代码）压缩率。基础模型胜过规模明显更大的开源模型，RLHF 对话模型极具竞争力但仍不及 GPT-4。
- **Limitations / 局限:** Pretraining corpus and exact source proportions are not released; context extension relies on inference-time tricks rather than long-context pretraining; still behind top proprietary models; potential residual benchmark contamination beyond the reported-task filtering. | 预训练语料及各来源确切占比未公开；上下文扩展依赖推理期技巧而非长上下文预训练；仍落后于顶级闭源模型；除已报告任务的过滤外，可能残留基准污染。
- **Note on successors / 后续版本说明:** Qwen2 (Yang et al., 2024, https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.10671) covers dense and MoE models from 0.5B to 72B and supports ~30 languages (English, Chinese, Spanish, French, German, Arabic, Russian, Korean, Japanese, Thai, Vietnamese, etc.). Qwen2.5 (Qwen Team, 2024, https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.15115) scales the high-quality pretraining set from 7 trillion to 18 trillion tokens, adding proprietary MoE variants Qwen2.5-Turbo and Qwen2.5-Plus alongside the open Qwen2.5-72B-Instruct. | Qwen2（Yang 等，2024，https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.10671）涵盖 0.5B 至 72B 的稠密与 MoE 模型，支持约 30 种语言（英、中、西、法、德、阿、俄、韩、日、泰、越等）。Qwen2.5（Qwen 团队，2024，https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.15115）将高质量预训练数据从 7 万亿扩展到 18 万亿 token，并在开源 Qwen2.5-72B-Instruct 之外新增闭源 MoE 变体 Qwen2.5-Turbo 与 Qwen2.5-Plus。

### Baichuan 2: Open Large-scale Language Models (Yang et al., 2023)
- **Venue / Link:** arXiv preprint (2023), https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.10305
- **Motivation / 动机:** Powerful LLMs are mostly closed-source or limited outside English; Baichuan 2 offers strong open multilingual base/chat models and releases all pretraining checkpoints to help study training dynamics. | 强大的大模型大多闭源或在英文之外能力有限；Baichuan 2 提供强大的开源多语言基础/对话模型，并释放全部预训练检查点以助力训练动态研究。
- **Corpus & Data / 语料与数据:** Trained on 2.6 trillion tokens — the largest at the time and more than double Baichuan 1. Sources: general internet webpages, books, research papers, codebases, etc. (a Chinese-English bilingual dataset of several million webpages was used for the safety corpus); per-category percentages appear only in a figure. Cleaning: focused on data frequency and quality, with a large-scale system supporting LSH-like and dense-embedding features that clusters and deduplicates trillion-scale data within hours; documents, paragraphs, and sentences are deduplicated and scored, with scores driving sampling. | 训练使用 2.6 万亿 token，为当时最大且是 Baichuan 1 的两倍多。来源包括通用互联网网页、书籍、研究论文、代码库等（安全语料使用了数百万网页的中英双语数据集）；各类别占比仅以图形式给出。清洗聚焦于数据频次与质量，构建了同时支持类 LSH 特征与稠密嵌入特征的大规模系统，可在数小时内对万亿级数据聚类去重；文档、段落、句子均经去重与打分，并据分数进行采样。
- **Method / 方法:** Trained from scratch. Tokenizer: SentencePiece BPE with vocab 125,696 (up from 64,000 in Baichuan 1), compression rate 0.498, numbers split into individual digits, character coverage 0.9999. Architecture uses SwiGLU, RMSNorm, and NormHead. Baichuan2-7B: RoPE, hidden 4096, FFN 11008, 32 heads, 32 layers, seq len 4096, max LR 2e-4. Baichuan2-13B: ALiBi, hidden 5120, FFN 13696, 40 heads, 40 layers, seq len 4096, max LR 1.5e-4. | 从零训练。分词器：SentencePiece BPE，词表 125,696（Baichuan 1 为 64,000），压缩率 0.498，数字拆为单个数位，字符覆盖率 0.9999。架构采用 SwiGLU、RMSNorm 与 NormHead。Baichuan2-7B：RoPE，隐藏维 4096，FFN 11008，32 头，32 层，序列长 4096，最大学习率 2e-4。Baichuan2-13B：ALiBi，隐藏维 5120，FFN 13696，40 头，40 层，序列长 4096，最大学习率 1.5e-4。
- **Models & Eval / 模型与评测:** Two base sizes, 7B and 13B. Baichuan2-7B-Base: MMLU 54.16, CMMLU 57.07, C-Eval 54.00, GSM8K 24.49, HumanEval 18.29. - **Venue / Link:** ICLR 2023, https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.02414

### Yi: Open Foundation Models by 01.AI (Young et al., 2024)
- **Venue / Link:** arXiv preprint (01.AI technical report), https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.04652
- **Motivation / 动机:** Build strong bilingual (English + Chinese) open foundation models whose capability is driven primarily by data quality rather than sheer scale, arguing that carefully engineered data plus modest scaling can approach frontier performance. | 构建强大的中英双语开源基础模型，其能力主要由数据质量而非单纯规模驱动；作者认为精细的数据工程加上适度扩展即可逼近前沿性能。
- **Corpus & Data / 语料与数据:** 3.1 trillion tokens of bilingual English–Chinese text; primary source is web documents from Common Crawl processed with the CCNet pipeline (language ID + perplexity scoring). Data engineering (the cascaded pipeline above) is the central methodological contribution. | 从零训练。解码器 Transformer（基于 LLaMA 实现），采用 GQA（6B：32 查询头/4 KV 头；34B：56/8）、SwiGLU 激活（FFN 缩至 8/3·h）以及调整基频的 RoPE（RoPE ABF）。基础预训练上下文 4K，随后通过轻量持续预训练（约 5B–10B token）扩展到 200K，同时保留全注意力。上述级联数据流程是核心方法贡献。
- **Models & Eval / 模型与评测:** Yi-6B (hidden 4096, 32 layers) and Yi-34B (hidden 7168, 60 layers); chat, 200K long-context, depth-upscaled, and vision-language variants also released. Base results — MMLU: 63.2 / 76.3; C-Eval: 72.0 / 81.4; CMMLU: 75.5 / 83.7; BBH: 42.8 / 54.3; Gaokao: 72.2 / 82.8; GSM8K: 32.5 / 67.2; MATH: 4.6 / 14.4; HumanEval: 15.9 / 23.2; MBPP: 26.3 / 41.0 (6B / 34B). Chat models evaluated on AlpacaEval and Chatbot Arena. | Yi-6B（hidden 4096，32 层）与 Yi-34B（hidden 7168，60 层）；另发布 chat、200K 长上下文、深度扩展与视觉语言变体。基座结果——MMLU：63.2/76.3；C-Eval：72.0/81.4；CMMLU：75.5/83.7；BBH：42.8/54.3；高考：72.2/82.8；GSM8K：32.5/67.2；MATH：4.6/14.4；HumanEval：15.9/23.2；MBPP：26.3/41.0（6B/34B）。Chat 模型在 AlpacaEval 和 Chatbot Arena 上评测。

- **Models & Eval / 模型与评测:** PanGu-α 2.6B (32 layers, d=2560, FFN 10240, 40 heads), 13B (13.1B; 40 layers, d=5120, FFN 20480), 200B (207.0B; 64 layers, d=16384, FFN 65536, 128 heads). PanGu-α 2.6B outperforms CPM 2.6B on generation tasks; PanGu-α 13B beats 2.6B on all generation tasks (+~3 pts avg) and gains >10 pts from few-shot over zero-shot on some tasks. | PanGu-α 2.6B（32 层，d=2560，FFN 10240，40 头）、13B（13.1B；40 层，d=5120，FFN 20480）、200B（207.0B；64 层，d=16384，FFN 65536，128 头）。在完形、阅读理解、闭卷问答、NLI、文本摘要与对话生成上做少样本/零样本评测。PanGu-α 2.6B 在生成任务上优于 CPM 2.6B；13B 在所有生成任务上超过 2.6B（平均约 +3 分），部分任务少样本比零样本高出 10 分以上。
- **Key Findings / 主要发现:** Careful cleaning/filtering yields a 1.1 TB corpus from 80 TB raw; Chinese few-shot/zero-shot in-context learning is feasible and improves steadily with model scale; the query-layer design and MindSpore 5D parallelism enable scaling to 200B on Ascend hardware. | 精细清洗过滤将 80 TB 原始数据提炼为 1.1 TB 语料；中文少样本/零样本上下文学习可行且随规模稳步提升；查询层设计与 MindSpore 五维并行使模型在昇腾硬件上扩展到 200B。

### DeepSeek LLM: Scaling Open-Source Language Models with Longtermism (DeepSeek-AI, 2024)
- **Venue / Link:** arXiv preprint (DeepSeek-AI technical report), https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.02954
- **Motivation / 动机:** Prior scaling-law studies give conflicting conclusions; the authors conduct their own scaling study to guide long-term scaling of open-source bilingual (English + Chinese) models at the 7B and 67B scales. | 以往的缩放律研究结论相互矛盾；作者开展自有缩放研究，以指导 7B 与 67B 规模的开源中英双语模型的长期扩展。

Models and weights released open-source (DeepSeek license). Exact Chinese/English split not disclosed. | 2 万亿 token 的中英双语数据集，并称持续扩充。三阶段流程：(1) 去重——对 Common Crawl 跨快照激进去重（去除率随快照数上升：1 个 22.2%、2 个 46.7%、12 个 69.9%、41 个 81.6%、91 个 89.8%；跨 91 个快照去重比单快照多去约 4 倍）；(2) 过滤——文档质量评估（语言学 + 语义）以提升信息密度；(3) 重混——再平衡以增强欠代表领域。分词器：基于 HuggingFace tokenizers 的字节级 BPE（BBPE），10 万常规 token + 15 个特殊 token，模型词表设为 102,400，在约 24 GB 多语文本上训练；数字按单个数位拆分。模型与权重开源（DeepSeek 许可）。中英具体比例未公开。
- **Method / 方法:** Trained from scratch. - **Key Findings / 主要发现:** DeepSeek 67B surpasses LLaMA-2 70B across benchmarks with the largest margins in code, math, reasoning, and Chinese; the non-embedding-FLOPs metric M gives more accurate scaling predictions than parameter count; higher data quality justifies allocating relatively more compute to model size than to data. | DeepSeek 67B 在各基准上超过 LLaMA-2 70B，在代码、数学、推理和中文上优势最大；非嵌入 FLOPs 度量 M 比参数量给出更准确的缩放预测；数据质量越高，应将相对更多算力分配给模型规模而非数据量。
- **Limitations / 局限:** Exact Chinese/English token ratio and source-level composition not disclosed; scaling limited to 67B; scaling-law fits are dataset-specific (exponents shift with data quality), so conclusions may not transfer to other corpora; standard LLM safety/hallucination caveats apply. | 未公开精确的中英 token 比例与来源级别构成；规模仅到 67B；缩放律拟合依赖具体数据集（指数随数据质量变化），结论未必迁移到其他语料；仍有标准的大模型安全/幻觉问题。

- **Venue / Link:** arXiv preprint (Inspur / IEIT Systems), https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.15786
- **Motivation / 动机:** Vanilla self-attention treats all token pairs equally regardless of distance, ignoring that natural-language dependencies are stronger among neighboring words; the authors also argue data quality (not just scale) drives performance, so they build a high-quality synthesized corpus. | 标准自注意力对所有词元对一视同仁、忽略了自然语言中相邻词之间更强的依赖关系；作者还认为数据质量（而非仅规模）决定模型表现，因此构建了高质量的合成语料。

Chinese-labeled sources dominate: Book (CN) 19.5%, Baike/encyclopedia (CN) 15.5%, Python (CN) 5.73%, plus CN notebooks/code-instruct/math (~7% more) — Chinese content is roughly half the corpus; English side is Python 9.27% and other StarCoder code plus Pile subsets (Arxiv 7%, Book3 3.06%, DM 1.55%, Wikipedia 1%, Freelaw 1.97%, stack_exchange 0.78%). The 102B model was trained on 288B tokens. Cleaning: start from public datasets, then filter and de-duplicate; a content-filtering system with 25 filters (sensitive/illegal, etc.) is applied to books; math (CN) mined from 5 years of Common Crawl (2018–2022) via the MDCS tool, converting MathJax/MathML to LaTeX; a distinctive feature is LLM-generated synthetic high-quality data for both pretraining and fine-tuning (e.g. 4M Chinese code samples). Weights and code released on GitHub; a specific corpus/data license is not stated in the paper. | 预训练语料为中英双语，混合书籍、代码与百科（表2以百分比给出）。中文来源占比高：中文书籍19.5%、百科（Baike）15.5%、Python(CN)5.73%，加上中文notebook/代码指令/数学（约再7%），中文内容约占语料一半；英文侧为Python 9.27%等StarCoder代码及Pile子集（Arxiv 7%、Book3 3.06%、DM 1.55%、维基1%、Freelaw 1.97%、stack_exchange 0.78%）。102B模型在288B词元上训练。清洗：先取公开数据集，再过滤与去重；对书籍使用含25个过滤器（敏感、违规等）的内容过滤系统；中文数学数据用MDCS工具从5年（2018–2022）Common Crawl中挖掘，将MathJax/MathML转为LaTeX；显著特点是用大模型生成高质量合成数据用于预训练与微调（如400万条中文代码样本）。权重与代码在GitHub开源；论文未明确说明语料/数据许可证。

Distributed training uses non-uniform pipeline parallel + data parallel + optimizer parallel to cut intra-node bandwidth needs; loss uses Fill-in-the-Middle (FIM rate 0.5). Data pipeline centers on a "data filtering and generating system" (self-instruct / evol-instruct style generation plus filtering). | 从零训练。核心架构创新为局部过滤注意力（LFA）：用单向一维卷积注入归纳偏置，使每个词元先与前两个词元建立关系再做全局注意力；RMSNorm置于LFA之前。序列长度4096；合并分词器（中文+arxiv+StarCoder+LLaMA），词表134,953。分布式训练采用非均匀流水线并行+数据并行+优化器并行以降低节点内带宽需求；损失采用中间填充（FIM，比例0.5）。数据管线以"数据过滤与生成系统"为核心（self-instruct/evol-instruct式生成加过滤）。
- **Models & Eval / 模型与评测:** Three sizes — 2.1B, 51.8B, 102.6B. HumanEval pass@1: Yuan2.0-102B 67.1% (77.4% with self-consistency), 51B 66.5%, 2B 54.9%. GSM8K: 102B 76.6% (86.2% with SC), 51B 76.3%, 2B 66.6%. AGIEval Gaokao-math: 102B beats ChatGPT (e.g. Gaokao-Math-QA 38.7 vs 36.5, 45.5 with SC). - **Key Findings / 主要发现:** LFA yields better accuracy than vanilla attention at similar cost; LLM-generated synthetic pretraining/fine-tuning data plus rigorous filtering lets relatively small models achieve strong code/math results; self-consistency with model-generated unit tests markedly boosts HumanEval and GSM8K. | LFA在相近成本下精度优于标准注意力；大模型生成的合成预训练/微调数据加严格过滤，使相对较小的模型也能取得强的代码/数学表现；结合模型自生成单元测试的自一致性显著提升HumanEval与GSM8K。
- **Limitations / 局限:** Heavy reliance on synthetic/LLM-generated and translated data risks propagating teacher-model biases/errors; the paper reports selected benchmarks (code, math, TruthfulQA) rather than broad general-knowledge suites; full-corpus total token count and an explicit data license are not clearly stated. | 大量依赖合成/大模型生成及翻译数据，可能传递教师模型的偏差与错误；论文主要报告选定基准（代码、数学、TruthfulQA）而非广泛的通用知识评测；未清晰给出全语料总词元数与明确的数据许可证。

### FLM-101B: An Open LLM and How to Train It with $100K Budget (Li et al., 2023)
- **Venue / Link:** arXiv preprint (BAAI / CofeAI et al.), https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.03852
- **Motivation / 动机:** The heavy compute, carbon footprint, and financial cost of training 100B+ LLMs are barriers; the authors explore a growth (progressive) training strategy — inspired by neurogenesis — to reach 100B+ scale cheaply, and argue knowledge-only benchmarks under-measure true intelligence. | 训练100B+大模型的巨大算力、碳排放与资金成本是障碍；作者探索受神经发生启发的增长（渐进）训练策略，以低成本达到100B+规模，并主张仅测知识的基准无法充分衡量真正智能。
- **Corpus & Data / 语料与数据:** Trained on around 300 billion English + Chinese tokens — an English:Chinese mix of approximately 53.5% : 46.5%. Instruction-formatted data is mixed in: OIG (Open Instruction Generalist) and COIG (Chinese Open Instruction Generalist). English data amount is ~0.2T (compared to GLM-130B). The paper notes no English textbook/exam data is intentionally included (relevant to MMLU). Detailed source-by-source breakdown and an explicit data license are not enumerated in the paper; the FLM-101B checkpoint is publicly released on Hugging Face (CofeAI/FLM-101B). | 训练约3000亿中英词元——英文:中文约为53.5%:46.5%。混入指令格式数据：OIG（开放指令通才）与COIG（中文开放指令通才）。英文数据量约0.2T（与GLM-130B相比）。论文指出未刻意加入英文教材/考试数据（与MMLU相关）。论文未逐一列出数据来源明细与明确的数据许可证；FLM-101B检查点在Hugging Face（CofeAI/FLM-101B）公开发布。
- **Method / 方法:** Trained from scratch via a function-preserving growth strategy: one training run grows a 16B model → 51B → 101B (superlinear cost saving), adapting MSG growth operators. Backbone is FreeLM (a modified GPT decoder-only model), with xPos (extrapolatable position embedding) for long-sequence modeling and the GPT-4 tokenizer. Trained on 24 DGX-A800 servers (192 A800 GPUs) under a $100K budget; ~26.54B tokens/day throughput at the largest stage, with ~51.3% FLOPs utilization (vs GLM-130B's 42.27%). Stability addressed via improved objectives, hyperparameter search, and function-preserving growth. | 通过保功能增长策略从零训练：单次训练将16B→51B→101B（超线性省算力），改造自MSG增长算子。骨干为FreeLM（改进的GPT纯解码器模型），用xPos（可外推位置嵌入）做长序列建模，采用GPT-4分词器。在24台DGX-A800服务器（192块A800 GPU）上以10万美元预算训练；最大阶段吞吐约每天26.54B词元，FLOPs利用率约51.3%（GLM-130B为42.27%）。稳定性通过改进目标、超参搜索与保功能增长解决。
- **Models & Eval / 模型与评测:** Produces 16B, 51B, and 101B (FLM-101B) models in one run; also eFLM-16B (professional-knowledge-enhanced via FreeLM teacher signals). Open LLM Leaderboard (Table 4) FLM-101B: Average 43.94 (ARC 39.76, HellaSwag 66.23, MMLU 28.30, TruthfulQA 41.47) vs GLM-130B 48.11 — reaching over 90% of GLM-130B at ~28.22 vs 210.80 zettaFLOPs. eFLM-16B reaches MMLU 44.50, surpassing GLM-130B (42.59). Introduces IQ-style evaluations across four axes — symbolic mapping, rule understanding, pattern mining, anti-interference (SuperGLUE-IQ, CLUE-IQ) — where FLM-101B is competitive with GPT-3 and beats GLM-130B on CLUE-IQ despite ~10x less compute. | 单次运行产出16B、51B、101B（FLM-101B）；另有eFLM-16B（借FreeLM教师信号增强专业知识）。Open LLM榜（表4）FLM-101B：平均43.94（ARC 39.76、HellaSwag 66.23、MMLU 28.30、TruthfulQA 41.47），GLM-130B为48.11——以约28.22对210.80 zettaFLOPs达到GLM-130B九成以上性能。eFLM-16B的MMLU达44.50，超过GLM-130B（42.59）。提出IQ式评测四维度——符号映射、规则理解、模式挖掘、抗干扰（SuperGLUE-IQ、CLUE-IQ）——FLM-101B在算力约少10倍下与GPT-3相当，并在CLUE-IQ上胜过GLM-130B。
- **Key Findings / 主要发现:** A function-preserving growth strategy can train a 100B+ bilingual LLM for ~$100K (10% of baseline FLOPs) while reaching ~80–90% of baseline performance; low MMLU reflects the absence of intentional English textbook/exam data rather than a fundamental gap (eFLM-16B closes it); IQ-style tasks are proposed as a compute-robust intelligence measure beyond knowledge benchmarks. | 保功能增长策略可用约10万美元（基线FLOPs的10%）训练100B+双语大模型，同时达到基线约80–90%性能；较低的MMLU源于未刻意加入英文教材/考试数据而非根本差距（eFLM-16B可弥补）；提出IQ式任务作为超越知识基准、对算力更稳健的智能度量。
- **Limitations / 局限:** Absolute knowledge-benchmark scores (e.g. MMLU 28.30) lag scaled/expensive baselines; the pretraining corpus is not documented source-by-source and lacks an explicit data license; growth-strategy training introduces instability requiring careful mitigation; IQ-style benchmarks are newly proposed and not yet community-standardized. | 绝对知识基准分数（如MMLU 28.30）落后于扩规模/高成本基线；预训练语料未逐源记录且缺乏明确数据许可证；增长策略训练带来需精心缓解的不稳定性；IQ式基准为新提出、尚未成为社区标准。
